Otter Free Plan Limits (2026) and Free Alternatives

Last checked: 25 November 2026 (Japan time). I read Otter’s official pricing page that day. Otter links on this page are official links, not affiliate links. For paid tiers in detail, see Otter pricing.

Quick answer

Otter Basic is free. As of 25 November 2026 its card lists 300 monthly transcription minutes, 30 minutes per conversation, access to the 25 most recent conversations, 3 lifetime file imports, and 1 concurrent meeting. The cheapest paid plan is Pro at $8.33 per user per month billed annually ($16.99 monthly).

What the limits mean in practice

  • 30 minutes per conversation is the limit most people hit first: a 60-minute call would not be transcribed in full on Basic.
  • 300 minutes a month is about ten 30-minute meetings.
  • 25 most recent conversations: older ones are not accessible on Basic.
  • 3 imports, lifetime: Basic is not a plan for transcribing recorded files.

FAQ

Is Otter free?

Yes. Otter Basic is $0. As of 25 November 2026 it includes 300 monthly transcription minutes, 30 minutes per conversation, the 25 most recent conversations, 3 lifetime imports, and 1 concurrent meeting.

What is the time limit on Otter free?

30 minutes per conversation and 300 minutes a month on Basic, per the official pricing page on 25 November 2026.

What is a free alternative to Otter for long meetings?

Circleback Free (unlimited meetings, 30-day history), Fathom Free (unlimited recordings), Fireflies Free (2-hour recording limit), and tl;dv Free (unlimited recordings) publish limits that suit meetings longer than 30 minutes.

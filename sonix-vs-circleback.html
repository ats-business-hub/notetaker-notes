Sonix vs Circleback for Small Teams (2026)

I read Sonix’s pricing page and AI meeting recorder page, and Circleback’s pricing page and homepage, on 21 November 2026 (Japan time). I have not used either product on a client call. Longer write-ups are in the Sonix review, Sonix pricing, Circleback review, and Circleback pricing. Related comparisons include Sonix vs Fireflies and Circleback vs Fireflies.

Quick pick

  • Circleback: Official pages offer a meeting bot or a bot-free desktop app, plus a $0 Free plan with unlimited meetings and 30-day history. Yearly cards list Pro at $15, Business at $25, and Enterprise at $35 per user per month; the same page’s source list also has monthly amounts of $18 and $29 for Pro and Business.

How each one records

Circleback’s pricing cards say you can use “a bot or the bot-free desktop app for online meetings,” and list mobile and Apple Watch apps for in-person meetings. The homepage FAQ “Can I use Circleback without a bot?” says yes: you can download the Circleback desktop app for macOS and Windows to record meetings without a bot joining your calls, and that by default it records meeting video, with what’s shown on screen making it into your notes. The same FAQ section says Circleback supports in-person meetings and all meeting platforms, including Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams. I have not watched either capture path on a call.

Sonix’s AI meeting recorder page says Sonix Agent joins your Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams call and writes the transcript while people talk. It says speakers are named from the call, 52 languages work live, and the agent is included with Pro and Enterprise plans, while the free trial covers uploaded recordings. A comparison on that page lists “Sonix Agent in the call” on Pro and Enterprise, and “Upload the recording” on all plans and the free trial. The FAQ says the agent does not join Webex or other meeting apps, so those recordings should be uploaded instead, and that the free trial and Pay as You Go cover uploaded files, not the live agent.

Free plans and trials

Circleback’s Free card is $0 per user per month. It lists AI meeting notes and action items, a bot or the bot-free desktop app, and 30-day meeting and recording history. The pricing FAQ says every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings.

Sonix’s pricing page offers a 30-minute free trial, with no credit card required, and says you can cancel anytime. It does not present a standing $0 monthly plan like Circleback Free. The AI meeting recorder FAQ, “Is the AI meeting recorder part of the free trial?”, answers no: the meeting recorder comes with Pro and Enterprise, and the free trial and Pay as You Go cover uploaded files. The two $0 starting points are not the same kind of offer, and I am not declaring a winner.

Paid prices, as of 21 November 2026

Circleback’s pricing page has Monthly and Yearly toggles. On the Yearly cards I saved, Pro is $15, Business is $25, and Enterprise is $35, each labeled per user per month. The same page’s billingPlanPrices list, in US cents, has Pro at $18 billed monthly or $180 a year, Business at $29 billed monthly or $300 a year, and Enterprise at $420 a year, with no monthly interval for Enterprise in that list. The annual unit amounts match the Yearly cards. The pricing FAQ says that on annual billing, 50 users cost $9,000 a year on Pro, $15,000 on Business, and $21,000 on Enterprise, and that Business and Enterprise include unlimited free viewers. I am quoting the visible cards, the source list, and the FAQ rather than collapsing them into one figure.

Sonix’s pricing page lists Pay as You Go at $10 an hour, with AI workspace usage not available, 5 GB of storage, and a single-user account. Core is $25 a month or $275 a year, with 5 hours a month or 60 hours a year of transcription and translation, plus a separate 5 hours a month or 60 hours a year of AI workspace usage, and 25 GB of storage. Advanced is $50 a month or $550 a year, with 20 / 240 transcription hours, 25 / 300 AI workspace hours, and 50 GB of storage. Pro is $80 a month or $880 a year, with 40 / 480 transcription hours, 100 / 1,200 AI workspace hours, and 100 GB of storage. Extra hours on any subscription plan are $10 an hour. Extra seats are $25 a month or $275 a year and, the page says, do not add more hours. The AI meeting recorder page says the recorder is part of Pro at $80 a month and of every Enterprise plan. I have not turned Sonix hours into Circleback seats.

Who should pick which

These suggestions follow only what the official pages say. I have not tested accuracy, the bot, the desktop app, or Sonix Agent.

Look at Circleback if you want a meeting notetaker whose official pages offer both a bot and a bot-free desktop app, a $0 Free plan with unlimited meetings and a 30-day history cap, and per-seat list prices in U.S. dollars. Look at Sonix if you mainly upload audio or video, want a 30-minute no-card trial of that upload path, and can move to Pro when you want Sonix Agent in Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams, as the official recorder page states.

FAQ

Can I record a live meeting?

Circleback’s official pages offer a bot or a bot-free desktop app on the Free plan. Sonix Agent joins Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams and is included with Pro and Enterprise; the free trial covers uploads, not the agent. This page has not tested either product.

What can I try for $0?

Circleback Free is a standing $0 plan with unlimited meetings and 30-day history. Sonix offers a 30-minute free trial of uploaded recordings, not a standing free monthly plan and not the live agent. I am not declaring a winner from those different offers.

Which costs less, Sonix or Circleback?

They price on different metrics. As of 21 November 2026, Circleback Pro was $15 per user per month on the Yearly cards, or $18 billed monthly in the page-source list. Sonix Core was $25 a month or $275 a year, and Sonix Pro, which includes the agent, was $80 a month or $880 a year. Check both live pages before you buy.

Notta vs Noota: pricing and transcription limits compared

Notta and Noota are different companies with similar names. Both transcribe meetings and offer AI summaries, but they price in different currencies and set different limits. Limits

On Notta’s pricing page, checked on 26 November 2026:

  • Free: 120 transcription minutes per month, up to 3 minutes per conversation, 50 file uploads per month, 10 AI summaries per month
  • Pro: 1,800 transcription minutes per month, up to 5 hours per recording, 100 file uploads and 100 AI summaries per month
  • Business: unlimited transcription, up to 5 hours per recording, 200 file uploads and 200 AI summaries per month

On Noota’s pricing and support pages:

  • Free recording is unlimited only with a connected calendar and “Join all meetings” enabled, which shares the summary with all participants. Otherwise, live, desktop-app and Chrome extension recordings are limited to 300 minutes per month
  • History: 1 month on Free, 6 months on Pro, unlimited on Business and Enterprise
  • Meetings older than the history limit are archived, according to the support article

Noota’s pricing FAQ says the Free plan includes an email agent on monthly AI credits. The support feature table, on the same day, lists mailbox sync from Pro and “follow-up emails, email agent, automatic drafts” from Business, and it lists voice dictation as unavailable on Free. Because those pages disagree, this article does not treat email or dictation as confirmed Free-plan features.

Who each tool is for, on paper

Choose Notta if you want a US-dollar free plan with a modest monthly transcription cap, or a Pro plan whose official annual price is shown per month as $8.17. Choose Noota if you want a euro-priced free plan with the calendar “Join all” rule for unlimited recording, bot-free capture on Pro and above, and longer (or unlimited) history on paid plans. These are editorial positions based on the official pages above, not a test of quality or accuracy.

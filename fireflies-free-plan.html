What official pages agree on

The pricing page lists Free at $0 and labels the plan “Free forever.” The pricing-plans help article, last updated 1 month before this check, also lists Free at $0. Paid list prices in that same article, and on the pricing cards I saw, are Pro $10 per seat per month billed annually or $18 billed monthly, Business $19 or $29 on the same pattern, and Enterprise $39 per seat per month, marked annual only. I did not find a sentence that says a credit card is required, or not required, to open the Free plan.

The Free card on the pricing page also names Zoom, GMeet, and Teams, transcription in 100+ languages, a desktop app, a mobile app, and a Chrome extension. Free-plan help says Fireflies Talk (unlimited voice dictation in the desktop app) is included on every plan, including Free, that all integrations are available on the Free plan, and that downloading transcripts requires a paid plan.

Where official pages disagree

I am not choosing between these descriptions. Here is what each official page said on 22 November 2026.

Unlimited transcriptions and Auto-join

The unlimited-transcriptions article, last updated 2 months before this check, says this feature is exclusive to Free plan users. Auto-join ON means no cap on the number of transcriptions. Auto-join OFF means the default of 3 meeting transcriptions. These limits apply to users who sign up on or after 1 September 2026. If you signed up before that date and already had unlimited free meetings, the article says your account is unaffected.

The “Only when I invite fred@fireflies.ai” Auto-join option “does not support unlimited transcriptions.” You can still add fred@fireflies.ai as a guest for an individual meeting when Auto-join is off, but that “only covers that specific meeting and does not unlock unlimited transcriptions overall.” The same article says unlimited transcription works across Google Meet with the Chrome extension, shared bot meetings, and the mobile app, and that AI-powered features such as Smart Summaries, Meeting Recaps, Action Items, and Analytics still depend on your AI credit balance.

Free-plan help also lists a separate pool of “transcription credits” at sign-up: 3 if you sign up from the Fireflies website (bot), 5 from the Chrome extension, 10 from the mobile app as a new user, or 5 from the mobile app as an existing user. Those credits are described as powering meeting summaries and uploaded-file transcriptions. I have not reconciled that table with Auto-join unlimited transcriptions, or with the AI-credits guide.

AI credits, in one place

This is the sharpest official conflict on the Free plan. Free-plan help, which showed “Last updated 2 days ago” when I read it, says the Free plan includes 20 AI credits per month. The AI-credits guide, last updated 1 month before this check, says every account includes one-time AI credits based on your plan, and lists “Free: 20 AI credits on sign-up.” A later FAQ in that same guide says plan credits “are one-time and do not expire,” while unused purchased credits do not carry over. It also says transcription is not charged in AI credits, that credits are pooled at the team level and do not increase with added teammates, and that buying more credits as a paid add-on is “available only on paid plans.” Free users who run out “will see a paywall and be prompted to upgrade — no auto-charge applies.”

Uploads and downloads

Free-plan help says you can upload MP3, MP4, WAV, or M4A files up to 100MB and 150 minutes per file, that this consumes 1 transcription credit per file, and that downloading transcripts requires a paid plan. The pricing page lists “Upload audio/video file” on the Free card.

Google Meet on the Free plan

The pricing page names GMeet on the Free card. The unlimited-transcriptions article says that, with the Chrome extension and Auto-join on, you get unlimited free transcripts on Google Meet. The Chrome extension article says the extension “only works with Google Meet in Chrome,” that it can capture meeting audio, and that video recording requires inviting the Notetaker bot. The joins FAQ says a host may need to admit the notetaker, including from Google Meet’s “With potential risks” section, and that it will wait up to 10 minutes in a Google Meet lobby, versus 15 minutes on other platforms. For a wider Google Meet comparison, see Best free AI note takers for Google Meet and Best AI note takers for Google Meet.

When official pages point to a paid plan

Free-plan help says downloading transcripts requires a paid plan, and that unlimited storage and unlimited private channels start on Business. The first paid card on the pricing page is Pro, at $10 per seat per month billed annually or $18 billed monthly, with unlimited transcription, unlimited AI summaries, 8,000 mins of storage per seat, 20 AI credits, and downloads of transcripts, summaries, and recordings. That is list-price context, not a recommendation. Alternatives with a standing $0 or €0 plan are in Tactiq free plan limits, Free plans and trials, and Cheapest AI meeting note takers in 2026.

FAQ

Is Fireflies Free really free forever?

As of 22 November 2026, the Fireflies pricing page lists Free at $0 and calls it “Free forever.” The pricing-plans help article also lists Free at $0. I did not find a sentence on those pages that says a credit card is required, or not required, to open the Free plan. Limits still apply, and official pages do not describe those limits the same way.

What are the Fireflies Free plan limits?

Official pages do not agree. The pricing page marks transcripts and AI summaries Limited, lists 400 mins of storage per team and 20 AI credits, and shows a 2-hour recording limit in the Free column. Free-plan help, last updated 2 days before this check, says 3 meetings are included, Auto-join allows unlimited transcriptions, storage is 400 minutes per seat, and the plan includes 20 AI credits per month. The AI-credits guide says Free includes 20 AI credits on sign-up, that those plan credits are one-time and do not expire, and that transcription is not charged in AI credits.

How do I get unlimited transcriptions on Fireflies Free?

The unlimited-transcriptions help article, last updated 2 months before this 22 November 2026 check, says the feature is exclusive to Free plan users. Auto-join ON means no cap on the number of transcriptions; Auto-join OFF means the default of 3 meeting transcriptions. Those limits apply to users who sign up on or after 1 September 2026; earlier accounts that already had unlimited free meetings are unaffected. The “Only when I invite fred@fireflies.ai” Auto-join option does not support unlimited transcriptions. The same article says unlimited transcription works across Google Meet with the Chrome extension, shared bot meetings, and the mobile app, and that AI features still depend on your AI credit balance.

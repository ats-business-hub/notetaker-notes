Sonix vs Laxis for Small Teams (2026)

I read Sonix’s pricing page and AI meeting recorder page, and Laxis’s pricing page, homepage, and billing help article, on 21 November 2026 (Japan time). I have not used either product on a client call. Longer write-ups are in the Sonix review, Sonix pricing, Laxis review, and Laxis pricing.   • Laxis: Official pages present a bot-free note taker, with a 14-day Premium trial that does not require a credit card, then Basic at $0. Basic includes 300 minutes a month, a 45-minute cap per recording, and 30 days of history. The Yearly cards list Premium at $9.99 and Business at $19.99 per month; the billing help article also lists monthly rates of $15.99 and $29.99.

How each one records

Laxis’s homepage says the product transcribes every meeting into polished AI notes, tasks, and reports — “bot-free, in 100+ languages.” I am repeating that official wording; I have not watched a Laxis recorder on a call.

Sonix’s AI meeting recorder page says Sonix Agent joins your Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams call and writes the transcript while people talk. It says speakers are named from the call, 52 languages work live, and the agent is included with Pro and Enterprise plans, while the free trial covers uploaded recordings. A comparison on that page lists “Sonix Agent in the call” on Pro and Enterprise, and “Upload the recording” on all plans and the free trial. The FAQ says the agent does not join Webex or other meeting apps, so those recordings should be uploaded instead, and that the free trial and Pay as You Go cover uploaded files, not the live agent. The FAQ also says Sonix Agent appears in the participant list under that name.

Free plans and trials

Laxis’s pricing FAQ says every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of Premium, with no credit card required, and that when the trial ends you move to the free Basic plan and keep all your notes. The Basic card is $0 and lists 300 minutes of transcription a month, 30 days of conversation history, and recording of online and in-person meetings. The comparison table lists a 45-minute cap per recording on Basic. The pricing FAQ and the billing help article both say meetings older than 30 days on Basic are archived, not deleted, and that upgrading unlocks them again. The billing help article also says meeting transcription, Voice Keyboard dictation, and live translation share the same monthly minutes.

Sonix’s pricing page offers a 30-minute free trial, with no credit card required, and says you can cancel anytime. It does not present a standing $0 monthly plan like Laxis Basic. The AI meeting recorder FAQ, “Is the AI meeting recorder part of the free trial?”, answers no: the meeting recorder comes with Pro and Enterprise, and the free trial and Pay as You Go cover uploaded files. The two $0 starting points are not the same kind of offer, and I am not declaring a winner.

Paid prices, as of 21 November 2026

Laxis’s pricing page has Monthly and Yearly toggles. On the Yearly cards I saved, Premium is $9.99 per month and Business is $19.99 per month. The official billing help article, “Billing & Plans,” lists Premium at $15.99 a month or $9.99 yearly ($119.88 a year), and Business at $29.99 a month or $19.99 yearly ($239.88 a year). The pricing FAQ says website purchases are billed in USD through Stripe, while in-app purchases use Apple or Google regional price tiers. I am quoting both official wordings rather than collapsing them into one figure.

Sonix’s pricing page lists Pay as You Go at $10 an hour, with AI workspace usage not available, 5 GB of storage, and a single-user account. Core is $25 a month or $275 a year, with 5 hours a month or 60 hours a year of transcription and translation, plus a separate 5 hours a month or 60 hours a year of AI workspace usage, and 25 GB of storage. Advanced is $50 a month or $550 a year, with 20 / 240 transcription hours, 25 / 300 AI workspace hours, and 50 GB of storage. Pro is $80 a month or $880 a year, with 40 / 480 transcription hours, 100 / 1,200 AI workspace hours, and 100 GB of storage. Extra hours on any subscription plan are $10 an hour. Extra seats are $25 a month or $275 a year and, the page says, do not add more hours. The AI meeting recorder page says the recorder is part of Pro at $80 a month and of every Enterprise plan. I have not turned Sonix hours into Laxis minutes.

Who should pick which

These suggestions follow only what the official pages say. I have not tested accuracy, the apps, or Sonix Agent.

Look at Laxis if you want a product whose official homepage calls itself bot-free, prices in U.S. dollars, a 14-day Premium trial with no credit card, and a $0 Basic plan with a monthly minute pool that is also shared with Voice Keyboard, as the official pages state. Look at Sonix if you mainly upload audio or video, want a 30-minute no-card trial of that upload path, and can move to Pro when you want Sonix Agent in Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams, as the official recorder page states.

FAQ

Can I record a live meeting?

Laxis’s homepage presents the product as bot-free. Sonix Agent joins Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams and is included with Pro and Enterprise; the free trial covers uploads, not the agent. This page has not tested either product.

What can I try for $0?

Laxis starts new accounts on a 14-day Premium trial with no credit card, then moves you to the free Basic plan: 300 minutes a month, a 45-minute cap per recording, and 30 days of history (older meetings are archived, not deleted). Sonix offers a 30-minute free trial of uploaded recordings, not a standing free monthly plan and not the live agent. I am not declaring a winner from those different offers.

Which costs less, Sonix or Laxis?

They price on different metrics. As of 21 November 2026, Laxis Premium was $9.99 per month on the Yearly cards, or $15.99 monthly in the billing help article ($119.88 a year). Sonix Core was $25 a month or $275 a year, and Sonix Pro, which includes the agent, was $80 a month or $880 a year. Check both live pages before you buy.

What five seats cost on list prices

Five seats on Krisp Core annual: 5 × $8 = $40 a month. Five seats on Circleback Pro yearly: 5 × $15 = $75 a month. On monthly billing Krisp Core is 5 × $16 = $80. Circleback also gives a worked example in its FAQ: 50 users on annual billing cost $9,000 a year on Pro. Before any discount; not a quote.

Who each one fits

Choose Circleback if you want to start at $0 and keep it running, need automations into Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio or webhooks (Pro and above), or want unlimited free viewers on Business. More detail: Circleback review and Circleback pricing.

Try Circleback free

Choose Krisp if background noise on your own calls is the bigger problem, you want notes without a bot by default, and you are fine with a trial instead of a free tier. Krisp’s named language list does not include Japanese (see the Krisp review). Pricing detail: Krisp pricing and free plan.

Some teams run both: Krisp for noise cancellation (you can disable its Meeting Assistant, per its FAQ) and Circleback for notes. That doubles seat costs, so price it first.

FAQ

Is Krisp or Circleback cheaper?

On list prices as of 25 November 2026, Krisp Core is $8 per user per month billed annually ($16 monthly) and Circleback Pro is $15 per user per month on the yearly cards. Circleback also has a $0 Free plan with unlimited meetings and 30-day history; Krisp has only a 7-day trial.

Does Circleback have a free plan?

Yes. The pricing page lists Free at $0 per user per month, and the FAQ says every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings. Free keeps 30-day meeting and recording history.

Do Krisp and Circleback need a bot in the meeting?

Krisp is bot-free by default and lists a record-with-bot option. Circleback offers a bot or a bot-free desktop app for online meetings, according to its pricing page.

Quick pick

Bluedot: As of 21 November 2026, the official pricing page lists Free at $0, with 5 meetings for the lifetime of the account. Basic is $14 per user per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly, for unlimited audio-only meetings with a one-hour cap per recording. Pro, which the cards describe as adding unlimited video and unlimited recording duration, is $20 annually or $25 monthly.

Who counts as a paid seat

The first pricing FAQ, “Do I need to pay for each user in my workspace?”, says no: unlike other AI note-takers, Bluedot only charges for users who record meetings. I have not used the workspace billing screen, so I am quoting that FAQ rather than reporting how seats appear in an account.

The FAQ “What does ‘import’ mean in Bluedot?” says these are existing recordings that you upload from your local computer, Google Drive, or other sources. Zoom and Google Drive imports are listed on the Pro card; unlimited audio/video imports are listed on Business.

Cancellation and recordings

The pricing FAQ “What happens if I cancel?” says that if you cancel your subscription, you will lose access to your recordings. You have one month to restore your subscription; after that, your recordings will be permanently deleted. I have not cancelled an account.

How the Annual Basic and Pro seats sit next to other approved affiliates

These are not interchangeable products. As of 21 November 2026, Bluedot Basic’s annual list price of $14 per user per month is close to Circleback Pro’s Yearly list price of $15 per user per month, as quoted on this site’s Circleback pricing page, but the plans are not equivalent: Bluedot Basic is described as audio only, with a one-hour cap per recording, while Circleback’s Free and Pro cards describe a bot or the bot-free desktop app and, on Pro, unlimited meeting history. Tactiq Pro, as quoted on this site’s Tactiq pricing page from Tactiq’s official USD help article, is $8 per user per month billed annually, with a different capture model (a caption-based extension) and AI-credit caps on Pro. Questions, answered from the official pages

How much does Bluedot cost?

As of 21 November 2026, Free was $0; Basic was $14 per user per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly; Pro was $20 or $25; and Business was $32 or $39. A flat-rate company plan had no public amount. Check the live page before you buy.

Does Bluedot have a free plan?

Yes. The Free card is $0, labeled start free, forever, and lists 5 meetings per lifetime, a Chrome extension, desktop and mobile apps, and Slack, Notion, Zapier, and Make.

Do I need to pay for every person in my Bluedot workspace?

Bluedot’s pricing FAQ says no: it only charges for users who record meetings. I have not used the billing screen.

What happens if I cancel Bluedot?

The pricing FAQ says you will lose access to your recordings, and that you have one month to restore your subscription; after that, your recordings will be permanently deleted.

Bluedot vs Tactiq for Small Teams (2026)

I read Bluedot’s pricing page and homepage, and Tactiq’s pricing page and plan-upgrade help article, on 21 November 2026 (Japan time). I have not used either product on a client call. Longer write-ups are in the Bluedot review, Bluedot pricing, Tactiq review, and Tactiq pricing. Related comparisons are Bluedot vs Circleback, Circleback vs Tactiq, and Fireflies vs Tactiq.

Quick pick

  • Tactiq: Official pages describe a bot-free Chrome extension that transcribes Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams with no bot. Tactiq’s help article lists Pro at $8 per user per month billed annually, or USD $12 billed monthly. Free is $0, with 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month.

How each one records

Bluedot’s homepage describes an AI notetaker that does not join your meetings, and says you can capture Meet, Zoom, Teams, and Slack on Mac or Windows, with video optional and no bots; take notes at in-person meetings with iPhone, Android, or Apple Watch; and make, record, and transcribe phone calls from Bluedot using your own phone number. A banner on the pricing page also said “Introducing Bluedot 2.2. Now with phone call recording.” The plan cards I saved do not say which plan includes phone call recording, so I am not assigning that feature to a tier.

Tactiq’s site describes a different capture model. The product navigation on the pricing page says you can transcribe meetings in real time with no bot, names Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams, and points to a “bot-free Tactiq extension.” The pricing FAQ says the transcription is only displayed for you, but you can notify everyone that you are transcribing during the meeting. I have not used the extension, and I am not describing how captions are captured beyond what those official pages say.

Bluedot’s homepage says “Your data is never used to train or improve AI models.” I did not find an equivalent no-training sentence on the Tactiq pricing page or on the upgrade help article I opened today, so I am not making a training claim for Tactiq here.

Free plans

Bluedot Free is $0, labeled start free, forever, and lists 5 meetings per lifetime, a Chrome extension, desktop and mobile apps, and Slack, Notion, Zapier, and Make. That is a lifetime cap of five meetings, not a monthly allowance.

Tactiq Free is $0, labeled free forever, with no credit card, for 1 user. The pricing cards list 5 AI credits a month, 10 transcripts a month, AI-generated insights, share and export transcripts, and in-meeting AI. The pricing FAQ says one AI credit covers a single AI action — generating a meeting summary, drafting action items, or asking Tactiq AI a question — and that when you hit a transcript or AI-credit limit, Tactiq keeps transcribing, but AI features pause until the limit resets at the start of the next month, or until you upgrade. Existing transcripts stay accessible.

On those published caps, Tactiq Free is an ongoing monthly allowance; Bluedot Free is easier to exhaust if you want a long-term free option. The products are not otherwise equivalent.

Paid prices, as of 21 November 2026

Bluedot’s Annual cards listed Basic at $14, Pro at $20, and Business at $32 per user per month. The Monthly cards listed Basic at $18, Pro at $25, and Business at $39. Basic is described as audio only, with unlimited meetings, unlimited storage, a one-hour cap per recording, webhook access, and MCP integration. Pro adds unlimited meetings with video, unlimited recording duration, custom note templates, and Zoom and Google Drive imports. The pricing FAQ says Bluedot only charges for users who record meetings. A plan-by-plan walkthrough is in Bluedot pricing explained.

Tactiq’s pricing page at tactiq.io/buy is currency-localized. From this check location the visible annual cards were priced in Japanese yen: Pro ¥1,249, Team ¥2,541, and Business ¥4,800 per user per month, plus Free at ¥0. A separate fetch of the same URL showed the USD annual cards as Pro $8, Team $16.67, and Business $29.17 per user per month. Tactiq’s help article “How to upgrade your Tactiq plan,” also checked on 21 November 2026, lists USD prices of Pro $8 per user per month billed annually, or USD $12 billed monthly (USD $96 a year), and Team $16.67 billed annually, or USD $20 billed monthly (USD $200 a year). That help article lists Business at $29.16 per user per month billed annually, one cent below the $29.17 on the USD pricing cards. I am quoting both official figures rather than combining them.

Who should pick which

These suggestions follow only what the official pages say. I have not tested accuracy, the extension, or the desktop or mobile apps.

Look at Tactiq if you want a stated bot-free browser extension for Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams, a lower yearly Pro list price, and you can work within a monthly transcript and AI-credit cap on Free, or an AI-credit cap on Pro. Look at Bluedot if you want official pages that describe capture without a bot across online, in-person, and phone-call recording, a pricing FAQ that charges only people who record, and you can accept a five-meeting lifetime free cap, or a one-hour audio cap until Pro. Japanese is shown as 日本語 in the language row on Bluedot’s homepage. I did not find a language list on Tactiq’s pricing page or on the upgrade help article I opened today, so I am not making a language claim for Tactiq here.

FAQ

Do Bluedot and Tactiq both record without a bot?

Bluedot’s homepage describes capture with no bots joining your calls. Tactiq’s site describes a bot-free Chrome extension for Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams. This page has not tested either product.

Which free plan records more meetings?

Bluedot Free allows 5 meetings for the lifetime of the account. Tactiq Free allows 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month. On those published caps, Tactiq Free is an ongoing monthly allowance; Bluedot Free is a lifetime cap of five meetings.

Which costs less, Bluedot or Tactiq?

On 21 November 2026, Bluedot Basic was $14 per user per month billed annually (audio only, one-hour cap). Tactiq’s help article lists Pro at $8 per user per month billed annually, or USD $12 billed monthly. On those first-paid yearly figures, Tactiq Pro is the lower list price. Tactiq Pro still caps AI credits at 10 a month. Check both live pages before you buy.

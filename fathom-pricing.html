Fathom Pricing and Free Plan (2026)

Last checked: 25 November 2026 (Japan time). I read Fathom’s pricing page that day, for both the Individuals and Teams views. I have not used Fathom on client calls, so this is not a report of summary quality. Fathom links on this page are official links, not affiliate links. Prices change; check the page before you buy.

Quick answer

Fathom Free is $0, “Free forever,” with unlimited recordings and transcriptions and instant AI call summaries. Paid plans on 25 November 2026: Premium $16 per user per month billed annually ($20 monthly), Team $15 annually ($19 monthly, 2-user minimum), Business $25 annually ($34 monthly, 2-user minimum), and Enterprise $35 per user per month, annual only.

What the Free plan covers

  • Unlimited recordings and transcriptions, and instant AI summaries, per the Free card.
  • The feature grid lists bot-free capture as a beta feature for Mac.
  • The grid marks some AI features, such as AI action items and Ask Fathom, as “Limited use” on lower tiers; I could not map every grid cell to a plan from the text I read, so check the live grid if those features matter.
  • Fathom integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Slack Huddles, according to its integrations menu and Zoom and Teams pages.

What a small team pays

Team has a 2-user minimum: 3 users × $15 = $45 a month billed annually, or 3 × $19 = $57 billed monthly. Business for 3 users: 3 × $25 = $75 a month annually. List prices, not a quote. The page also mentions 10 free seats for eligible nonprofits and up to 2 years free of Fathom Team for startups in its Qualified Portfolio Program; I have not checked eligibility rules.

FAQ

Is Fathom free?

Yes. As of 25 November 2026 Fathom lists an individual Free plan at $0, “Free forever,” with unlimited recordings and transcriptions and instant AI call summaries.

How much does Fathom cost?

As of 25 November 2026: Premium $16 per user per month billed annually or $20 monthly; Team $15 annually or $19 monthly with a 2-user minimum; Business $25 annually or $34 monthly with a 2-user minimum; Enterprise $35 per user per month, annual only.

Does Fathom work without a bot?

The Free card lists a choice of bot-free (in beta) or bot capture, and the feature grid describes bot-free capture as a beta feature for Mac.

Krisp vs Noota: bot-free meeting notes compared

Krisp and Noota can both take meeting notes without a bot in the participant list, but they get there differently. Krisp is a noise-cancellation app with a note taker built in, and its AI note-taker page says no bot joins your meeting. Noota usually joins as a named bot. It can also record quietly from your computer with its desktop app, but only on the Pro plan and above. Noota also emphasises EU hosting and ATS (applicant tracking) integrations for recruiters. This comparison uses the official pricing, help, and security pages, checked on 26 November 2026. It is not a hands-on test. Krisp prices in US dollars and Noota in euros, so confirm the live price before you buy.

Quick verdict

  • Krisp fits people who want call audio cleaned up and bot-free notes from the first plan. Krisp says noise cancellation is built into every plan. There is a 7-day trial, but no ongoing free plan.

  • Noota fits European teams that care where data is stored, and recruiters who want notes pushed into an ATS. You can start on a free plan, but bot-free recording needs Pro.

Noota’s free plan: the recording limit

Noota’s pricing page explains when Free plan recording is unlimited. You need a connected calendar with Join all meetings turned on, and the meeting summary is then shared automatically with all participants. Otherwise, live and standard recordings are limited to 300 minutes per month. Its help article lists the Free plan’s recording mode as bot only. For more, see Noota pricing and the Noota review.

Krisp has no free plan for the meeting assistant on its pricing page, only the 7-day trial. Its pricing FAQ says the assistant asks for a Google or Outlook calendar connection by default at sign-up. Plan details are on the Krisp pricing page.

Hosting and privacy

Noota’s security page says data and AI processing stay inside the European Union. For a sovereign environment, every provider in the chain is French, with hosting in Paris. Noota also says it does not train models on your content. Krisp’s pricing page lists SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA, and says meeting data is never used to train Krisp’s AI models. If EU data residency is a requirement, read Noota’s security page and ask both vendors for their current documentation. Integrations

Noota’s pricing FAQ names sixteen ATS integrations, including Bullhorn, Teamtailor, Workable, Recruitee, and SmartRecruiters, with Zapier and the API for others. Krisp’s pricing page lists integrations such as Salesforce and MCP. Check the vendor’s integrations page for the specific tool you use.

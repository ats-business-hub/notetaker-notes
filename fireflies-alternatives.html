Who should consider switching, and why

I am not telling every Fireflies user to leave. The points below come from official Fireflies pages on 20 November 2026.

You do not want a visible bot in the meeting. The join FAQ says Fireflies appears as Fireflies.ai Notetaker, may request permission, and cannot be hidden: it appears in the participant list and posts a message in the meeting chat. You can rename the bot on a paid plan as an add-on ($5 a month for a shared name across 5 users, or $5 a month per user for individual names), but that does not hide it. Official no-bot paths are a Google Meet Chrome extension, the mobile app, and desktop Take Notes. If your calls are not on Google Meet and you do not want to use the desktop path, a tool that leads with device capture may fit better.

The Free plan is hard to pin down. The pricing page calls Free “free forever” and marks transcripts and AI summaries as Limited, storage as 400 minutes per team, and the recording limit as 2 hours. Other official Fireflies pages describe those Free limits differently, including a 3-meeting figure and storage per seat rather than per team. That conflict is written out in the Fireflies review and in Free plans and trials. If you want a free allowance written in one place, Circleback’s pricing FAQ (unlimited meetings, 30-day history) or Laxis Basic (300 minutes a month) may be easier to read. I have not tested either free plan.

You are paying per seat, plus extras. Pro is $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 monthly. Business is $19 or $29. The pricing FAQ says teammates you add are billed as seats. Video recording starts on Business. Some features use AI credits, which the knowledge base says can be purchased as an add-on. If you only need a few people to record, Bluedot’s FAQ says it charges only users who record. If you want a lower published yearly entry price, Krisp Core is $8 per user per month billed annually, and MeetGeek Pro is $9.99 billed yearly (with a dated Black Friday offer, below).

Fireflies can still be the right tool if you want a calendar-connected bot, the pricing table’s 2-hour recording cap on Free and Pro, a large integration list on paid plans, or to keep using the Meet extension and desktop Take Notes. Stay if those points matter more than the visible bot.

What Fireflies’ plans say today

If you decide to stay, you can open Fireflies from this affiliate link. I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. On 20 November 2026, fireflies.ai/pricing and the pricing-plans help article listed:

  • Free at $0, labeled “free forever.” The comparison table marks transcripts Limited, storage 400 minutes per team, AI summaries Limited, and a 2-hour recording limit.
  • Pro at $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly. The table marks transcripts and AI summaries Unlimited, storage 8,000 minutes per seat, and a 2-hour recording limit.
  • Business at $19 per seat per month billed annually, or $29 monthly, with unlimited storage and a 3-hour recording limit. Video capture is listed on Business and Enterprise.
  • Enterprise at $39 per seat per month, billed annually only, with a 4-hour recording limit. The help article for Enterprise, checked as supporting context, also says AI credits are not included in the base price and can be added on.

The pricing FAQ says there are no monthly rate limits for the meeting bot or the Chrome extension on Pro and Business, and that API, dialers, and bulk uploads have separate limits. I did not find a Black Friday or Cyber Monday offer on the Fireflies pricing page or homepage on 20 November 2026.

Bluedot — best for leaving the bot behind

Bluedot is the clearest official contrast with Fireflies’ visible notetaker. The homepage, checked 20 November 2026, calls it an “Invisible, privacy-first AI note taker” and says it “never invites a bot to your meeting or asks for calendar access,” using a Chrome extension or a desktop app instead. The pricing page listed Free at $0 with 5 lifetime meetings; Basic at $14 per user per month on the annual row or $18 monthly (unlimited audio-only meetings, unlimited storage, 1-hour cap per recording); Pro at $20 or $25 (video and unlimited duration); and Business at $32 or $39 (CRM and ATS, SSO, SCIM). The FAQ says only users who record are billed. See the Bluedot review and Bluedot vs Fireflies. Skip it if you need a bot to join a meeting you cannot attend, or if five free meetings are not enough to try it.

Circleback — best for a free plan with unlimited meetings, and a choice of bot or desktop

Circleback may fit a team that wants to keep a bot option and also have a documented bot-free desktop app. On 20 November 2026 the pricing page listed Free at $0, with a bot or a bot-free desktop app, 30-day history, and a FAQ that says every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings. Pro is $15 per user per month (JSON-LD $180 a year), with 1 year of recording storage and automations that name HubSpot, Salesforce, Notion, and Attio. Business is $25, with unlimited free viewers and 2 years of storage. Enterprise is $35. I did not find a separate monthly list price in the HTML or JSON-LD. See the Circleback review and Circleback vs Fireflies.

Laxis — best for notes plus a voice keyboard on one bill

Laxis puts a meeting assistant and a voice keyboard on every plan, from the same monthly minute pool. On 20 November 2026 the pricing page listed Basic at $0 with 300 minutes a month; Premium at $9.99 per month billed yearly with 2,000 minutes; Business at $19.99 per month billed yearly with unlimited transcription and CRM automation; and a 14-day Premium trial with no credit card. The page has a Monthly/Yearly toggle (“Save up to 38%”), but the static HTML I downloaded listed $9.99 and $19.99 only, so I could not confirm a different monthly list price from that HTML. See the Laxis review and Laxis vs Fireflies.

Tactiq — best for leaving the bot behind with a caption-based Chrome extension

Tactiq official pages say a Chrome or Edge extension transcribes Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams with no bot joining the call, using the meeting platform’s captions. The download page lists a Mac app for Zoom desktop calls only, and a help article says the Microsoft Teams desktop application is not currently supported. As of 20 November 2026, the official pricing page listed Free at $0, with no credit card, 10 transcripts per month, and 5 AI credits per month; Pro at $8 per user per month billed annually, with unlimited transcripts and 10 AI credits; Team at $16.67 per user per month billed annually, with unlimited AI credits; and Business at $29.17 per user per month billed annually. A help article dated 30 June 2026 also lists monthly prices of $12, $20, and $40 for Pro, Team, and Business. Tactiq Pro annual, at $8 per user per month, is lower than Fireflies Pro annual at $10. Skip Tactiq if you need a bot to attend a meeting you skip, or if you cannot keep captions on. See the Tactiq review and Tactiq alternatives.

FAQ

Why would a small team leave Fireflies?

Fireflies’ join FAQ, checked on 20 November 2026, says the usual notetaker appears as Fireflies.ai Notetaker, may wait to be admitted, posts in the meeting chat, and cannot be hidden. Official pages also disagree on Free-plan limits: the pricing table marks transcripts and summaries as Limited and storage as 400 minutes per team, while other Fireflies help describes those limits differently (see the Fireflies review). Paid plans start at Pro $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 monthly, and some features use AI credits billed separately. Teams that do not want a visible bot, want a clearer free allowance, or want a different capture model look at the tools on this page. I have not tested switching.

Can I stay on Fireflies and avoid the bot?

Yes, according to official help. The join FAQ says you can record without the bot using the Chrome extension (Google Meet only) or the mobile app. A separate help article documents desktop Take Notes, which records without a bot joining as a participant. The pricing page also has a row for recording with the Chrome extension without the Fireflies bot. I have not tested those paths. If the bot is the only problem and Google Meet or the desktop app covers your calls, you may not need to switch.

Which alternative has the lowest yearly list price?

On 20 November 2026, among the tools on this page, Tactiq Pro and Krisp Core were both $8 per user per month billed annually (Krisp Core is $16 monthly; Tactiq Pro is $12 monthly in a help article dated 30 June 2026). MeetGeek Pro was $9.99 billed yearly or $15.99 monthly, and Laxis Premium was $9.99 per month billed yearly. Bluedot Basic was $14 on the annual row. Circleback Pro was $15 per user per month, matching a $180 annual offer in that page’s JSON-LD. Fireflies Pro remains $10 per seat per month billed annually. These plans are not the same product: storage, bots, and limits differ. MeetGeek also advertised a dated Black Friday discount on Pro and Business.

Is there a Black Friday offer on these tools?

As of 20 November 2026, MeetGeek’s official pricing page advertised 30% off new Pro and Business subscriptions for 12 months, with the code BLACKFRIDAY2026, valid for monthly and annual new subscriptions. A script on that page stores sale prices of Pro $11.19 monthly or $6.99 yearly, and Business $18.90 monthly or $11.90 yearly, and sets the offer window from 16 November 2026, 00:00 PST to 30 November 2026, 23:59 PST. On the same date I did not find a Black Friday or Cyber Monday offer on the official pricing pages of Fireflies, Bluedot, Circleback, Laxis, or Krisp. Promotions change. Check MeetGeek’s official pricing page before you buy. I have not confirmed the code at checkout.

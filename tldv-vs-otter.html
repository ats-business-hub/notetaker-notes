I have not used either product on client calls, so this is not a hands-on test of accuracy or reliability. Prices, plan limits, and the currency you are billed in can change. Quick pick

tl;dv may fit a team that wants official euro list prices, a free plan that the public pricing page describes with unlimited recordings and transcription (with capped AI notes), and paid plans that advertise unlimited AI notes (Pro) or no usage-based AI fees (Business). Otter may fit a team that wants to stay in US dollars, already uses Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or Google Meet with Otter’s notetaker, and is willing to recheck the live pricing page — the same official page shows more than one number for paid plans. I have not tested which product is more accurate.

The two vendors do not list prices in the same currency. tl;dv’s official structured data is in euros. Otter’s pricing page is labeled “Prices in USD.” Do not treat a euro figure and a dollar figure as directly comparable without a conversion you do yourself, and recheck checkout currency.

How they record meetings

Otter’s official pricing page names Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet on the Basic plan, and describes a notetaker that joins those meetings to write and share notes. I am not restating bot behavior beyond what that page writes down.

tl;dv’s official integrations material, already cited in tl;dv vs Fireflies.ai, says it can record and transcribe meetings from Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams. The official Zoom page has described a one-click recording with files stored in a personal library, and the pricing page mentions custom bot appearance on Pro. I did not find a single official sentence, in that earlier check, that clearly states whether a participant-bot always joins, never joins, or is optional. Read the current Zoom, Meet, and Teams setup guides before you promise a no-bot call to a client.

Whichever tool you use, tell participants they are being recorded and follow the consent rules that apply to your calls.

Who each tool may fit

Otter may fit if you already work in USD, want Japanese named on the official language list, and can live with Basic’s 300-minute monthly cap — or you will pay for Pro after rechecking which annual number applies to your payment card. Frequently asked questions

Is tl;dv cheaper than Otter?

Not in a single currency I can cite. On 22 November 2026, tl;dv’s official structured data listed Pro at €18 per seat per month billed annually, in euros. Otter’s official page listed prices in USD and showed more than one Pro figure, including $16.99 and a starred $8.49* annual number, while this site’s 20 November 2026 reading also recorded $8.33 on the annual tab. Convert for yourself and recheck checkout.

What is included on each free plan?

tl;dv’s public pricing page describes a €0 Free plan with unlimited video recordings and unlimited transcription in 100+ languages, with AI-note and Ask AI caps. Otter Basic is $0 with 300 monthly transcription minutes, a 30-minute per-conversation cap in the comparison table, and 3 lifetime file imports. Recheck both live pages.

Does Otter transcribe Japanese?

Otter’s official pricing comparison table lists Japanese among AI transcription languages, alongside English, Spanish, French, German, and Chinese. I have not tested accuracy.

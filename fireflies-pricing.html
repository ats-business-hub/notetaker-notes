What the free plan actually limits

Official pages do not describe the Free plan the same way. I am not choosing between them.

  • The pricing page calls Free “free forever,” marks transcripts and AI summaries “Limited,” and lists 400 minutes of storage per team and 20 AI credits. The comparison table lists a 2-hour recording limit in the Free column.
  • Free-plan help, which showed “Last updated 16 hours ago” when I read it, says 3 meetings are included on Free. Auto-join “allows unlimited transcriptions.” Storage is “400 minutes of storage per seat.” It also says the Free plan includes 20 AI credits per month, that you can upload MP3, MP4, WAV, or M4A files up to 100MB and 150 minutes per file (1 transcription credit per file), and that downloading transcripts requires a paid plan. It says all integrations are available on the Free plan, and that the Free plan includes three default channels plus up to three additional public channels.
  • The unlimited-transcriptions article says this feature is exclusive to Free plan users. Auto-join ON means no cap on the number of transcriptions; Auto-join OFF means the default of 3 meeting transcriptions. It says these limits apply to users who sign up on or after 1 September 2026, and that earlier accounts that already had unlimited free meetings are unaffected. The “Only when I invite fred@fireflies.ai” Auto-join option “does not support unlimited transcriptions.” The same article says unlimited transcription works across Google Meet (with the Chrome extension), shared bot meetings, and the mobile app, and that AI summaries, action items, and analytics still depend on your AI credit balance.
  • The same joins FAQ also says you can transcribe meetings for free if you are using the Chrome extension or the mobile app, or if you have enabled an “Unlimited Transcription” toggle and share recaps with all invitees. That wording is not identical to the Auto-join article.

Free-plan help also lists a separate pool of “transcription credits” at sign-up: 3 if you sign up from the Fireflies website (bot), 5 from the Chrome extension, 10 from the mobile app as a new user, or 5 from the mobile app as an existing user. Those credits are described as powering meeting summaries and uploaded-file transcriptions. I have not reconciled that table with Auto-join unlimited transcriptions, or with the AI-credits guide.

AI credits and other add-ons

AskFred and several other features use AI credits. Official pages disagree on whether the credits included with a plan are monthly or one-time.

  • Plan cards list 20 AI credits on Free and Pro, 30 on Business, and 50 on Enterprise.
  • Free-plan help says the Free plan includes 20 AI credits per month for AskFred, AI Soundbites, and AI Skills.
  • The AI-credits guide says every account includes one-time AI credits on sign-up, in those same 20 / 20 / 30 / 50 amounts, that they are pooled at the team level and do not scale with seats, and that they do not expire. If you upgrade, the new plan’s credits replace the old ones; they do not stack. It also says transcription, note-taking, and recap emails are not charged in AI credits.

Extra credits are a paid add-on, “available only on paid plans,” billed monthly or annually (annual pack prices are 20% lower). The guide’s pack table, as of 20 November 2026, was:

  • 50 credits: $5 a month, or $4 a month billed annually
  • 200 credits: $20, or $16
  • 1,000 credits: $90, or $72
  • 2,500 credits: $200, or $160
  • 5,000 credits: $375, or $300
  • 7,500 credits: $450, or $360
  • 10,000 credits: $600, or $480

Unused purchased credits do not carry over, even on annual billing. The monthly credit limit stays the same if you switch a pack to annual billing; only the price and renewal frequency change. The guide says that if plan credits run out and you are not yet on a pack, Fireflies automatically subscribes paid accounts to the lowest tier ($5 a month for 50 credits), and that you can pause auto-upgrade in billing settings. Free users “will see a paywall and be prompted to upgrade — no auto-charge applies.” There is also a 7-day AI credits trial, and a one-time 100-credit bonus for 14 days when you create your first Voice Agent.

Renaming the bot is a separate add-on, described in the custom bot-name article. It is available on Pro, Business, and Enterprise. Individual names are $5 a month or $60 a year (non-refundable). A shared name for a team is $5 a month for up to 5 users, then $5 extra per user. Different names are $5 a month or $60 a year for each unique name. Enterprise includes a custom bot name for all users at no extra charge. Without the add-on, the bot joins as “Fireflies.ai Notetaker” plus the first 7 letters of your first name. The joins FAQ still describes the default name as Fireflies.ai Notetaker, and says the bot cannot be hidden.

Which plan suits whom

These are readings of the official pages, not test results.

  • Free may fit someone who can live with the conflicting limits, wants to try Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams capture at $0, and can turn on Auto-join if they need more than 3 transcriptions (for accounts created on or after 1 September 2026). It is a weaker official fit if you need downloads, a clearly stated storage number, or AI features beyond a small credit balance.
  • Pro is the first paid seat at $10 a month on annual billing. The card adds downloads, 8,000 minutes of storage per seat, video recording, and the paid-plan AI apps. It may fit a solo consultant or a small team that has outgrown Free storage or needs downloads. Extra bot-name and AI-credit packs can add to that $10.
  • Business at $19 a month billed annually is the official path to unlimited storage, Multi-language Mode, team analytics, and the higher 30-credit starting balance. Free-plan help also points here for unlimited private channels.
  • Enterprise at $39 a month, annual only, is the official path to private storage, HIPAA with a BAA, SSO, SCIM, custom retention, and a free custom bot name for all users. Default storage and processing otherwise remain in the United States, according to the storage article.

A help article on pricing plans, last updated about a month before I read it, repeats the same list prices: Free $0, Pro $10 or $18, Business $19 or $29, and Enterprise $39 billed annually. It also says Fireflies Talk (unlimited voice dictation in the desktop app) is included on every plan, including Free.

Cheaper alternatives?

These tools are not all cheaper than Fireflies Pro at $10 per seat per month billed annually. They are alternatives with their own free plans and, in some cases, a lower yearly entry price or a different way to record. Prices below were checked on official pages on 20 November 2026.

  • Noota. The pricing page lists prices in euros: Free €0, Pro €19 per user per month billed annually or €29 monthly, and Business €39 or €49. That paid entry price is not cheaper than Fireflies Pro in euro terms. As of 20 November 2026, the same page still advertised 40% off the first year of a new annual Pro or Business plan, until 30 November 2026, 11:59 pm Paris time, with no code. See the Noota review and Noota vs Fireflies.
  • Bluedot. The pricing page listed Free at $0 with 5 lifetime meetings; Basic at $14 per user per month billed annually, or $18 monthly; Pro at $20 or $25; and Business at $32 or $39. Basic annual is higher than Fireflies Pro annual. The official contrast is bot-free capture, not a lower paid price. See the Bluedot review.
  • Circleback. The pricing page yearly cards show Free at $0, Pro at $15 per user per month, Business at $25, and Enterprise at $35. Price data in that same page lists Pro at $18 billed monthly or $180 a year, Business at $29 monthly or $300 a year, and Enterprise at $420 a year; I did not find a monthly Enterprise price in that data. The FAQ says every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings, with 30-day history on Free. Pro annual is higher than Fireflies Pro annual. See the Circleback review.
  • Laxis. The pricing page shows Premium at $9.99 per month billed yearly and Business at $19.99 per month billed yearly, with Basic at $0 (300 minutes a month and a 45-minute cap per recording). Billing help lists the same yearly figures ($9.99 a month, or $119.88 a year, on Premium; $19.99 a month, or $239.88 a year, on Business) and monthly list prices of $15.99 for Premium and $29.99 for Business. Yearly billing “saves up to 38% (37.5% on Premium, 33% on Business).” Premium yearly is just under Fireflies Pro’s $10 annual list price. New accounts get a 14-day Premium trial with no credit card, according to the pricing FAQ. See the Laxis review.

FAQ

How much does Fireflies cost?

As of 20 November 2026, the Fireflies pricing page listed Free at $0, Pro at $10 per seat per month billed annually or $18 billed monthly, Business at $19 or $29 on the same pattern, and Enterprise at $39 per seat per month with annual billing only. A custom bot-name add-on and AI-credit packs are priced separately. Check the current pricing page before you buy.

What are the Fireflies Free plan limits?

Official pages do not agree. The pricing page calls Free free forever, marks transcripts and AI summaries Limited, and lists 400 minutes of storage per team and 20 AI credits. Free-plan help says 3 meetings are included, Auto-join can remove the transcription cap, storage is 400 minutes per seat, and 20 AI credits are monthly. A separate article says Auto-join unlimited transcriptions apply to users who sign up on or after 1 September 2026, and that the Only when I invite fred@fireflies.ai option does not unlock unlimited transcriptions. The AI-credits guide says the 20 Free credits are one-time on sign-up, not monthly.

Does Fireflies charge extra for AI credits?

Plan cards list a starting credit amount: 20 on Free and Pro, 30 on Business, and 50 on Enterprise. The AI-credits guide says those plan credits are one-time, shared by the team, and do not increase with added seats. Extra credit packs are a paid add-on on paid plans only, starting at $5 a month for 50 credits, with annual pack prices 20% lower. Unused purchased credits do not carry over. The guide also says paid accounts can be auto-subscribed to the lowest pack if plan credits run out, unless auto-upgrade is paused. Free users see a paywall instead of an auto-charge.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Fireflies?

It depends on the plan and the currency. As of 20 November 2026, Tactiq Pro was $8 per user per month billed annually, which is below Fireflies Pro at $10 per seat per month billed annually. Laxis Premium was $9.99 per month billed yearly, also just under Fireflies Pro. Bluedot Basic was $14 per user per month billed annually, and Circleback Pro was $15 per user per month on the yearly card. Noota lists prices in euros, with Pro at €19 per user per month billed annually. These tools have different limits: Tactiq Pro still caps AI credits at 10 per month, and the seats are not interchangeable. See Fireflies.ai vs Tactiq and Tactiq pricing explained. Check each current pricing page before you buy.

Microsoft Teams-specific things to check

  • Lobby and external participants. A meeting bot joins like an outside guest. If your organization’s Teams settings hold guests in the lobby or block external participants, someone may need to admit the bot, or your IT admin may need to allow it. Check with your admin before relying on a bot.
  • Bot-free options. Circleback’s desktop app and tl;dv’s desktop app are described as recording without a bot; Tactiq describes a Chrome extension, which matters if you use the Teams desktop app rather than Teams in a browser.
  • Meeting length and history. Otter Basic caps a conversation at 30 minutes; Fireflies Free lists a 2-hour recording limit; Circleback Free keeps 30 days of history; tl;dv Free stores recordings for 3 months.
  • Consent. Tell participants you are recording or transcribing, especially in external Teams meetings.

FAQ

What is the best free AI note taker for Microsoft Teams?

There is no single best one. As of 25 November 2026, Circleback Free (unlimited meetings, 30-day history), Fireflies Free (2-hour recording limit, 400 minutes of storage per team), tl;dv Free (unlimited recordings, 3-month storage), Tactiq Free (10 transcripts a month), Otter Basic (300 minutes a month, 30 minutes per conversation) and Fathom Free all say they support Microsoft Teams. Pick by meeting length, history, and whether a bot is acceptable.

Can I take AI notes in Microsoft Teams without a bot joining?

Official pages describe bot-free options for Circleback (desktop app), tl;dv (desktop app), Tactiq (Chrome extension with live transcription) and Fathom (bot-free capture in beta for Mac). Fireflies and Otter describe a notetaker that joins the Microsoft Teams meeting.

Is Krisp free?

No standing free plan. As of 25 November 2026 Krisp offers a 7-day free trial, then Core at $8 per user per month billed annually or $16 monthly.

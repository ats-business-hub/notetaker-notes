Bot-free vs a bot in the meeting

Bluedot’s homepage says it never invites a bot and never asks for calendar access. Capture is a Chrome extension or a desktop app on Mac or Windows. The FAQ says you do not need to be the host, and that it works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Slack huddles, and phone calls because it captures from the browser or desktop. It does not announce itself the way a bot does. Bluedot recommends telling participants. This page is not legal advice.

Fireflies usually joins as Fireflies.ai Notetaker. Help says you cannot hide it, that it may wait to be admitted, and that it posts in chat. Two no-bot paths are documented. The Chrome extension records Google Meet only. Desktop Take Notes captures system audio on Windows or Mac, including Zoom, Meet, and Teams, and no Fireflies.ai Notetaker joins. That mode does not save a separate audio or video file. Speakers are labeled Speaker 1 and Speaker 2. You cannot switch from Take Notes to the bot in the middle of the same recording.

If a visible participant is the problem, Bluedot’s documented product is the closer fit. Fireflies can avoid the bot only on the narrower paths above. I have not tested either claim.

Pricing

These are list prices on 19 November 2026. Annual figures are the monthly amount when the year is paid up front, where the vendor says so.

  • Bluedot Free: $0. The card says “Start free, forever” and 5 meetings per lifetime. The homepage FAQ says Bluedot is not a free AI meeting note taker, and that after those 5 meetings a free member can view and share recordings or upgrade.
  • Bluedot Basic: $14 or $18 per user per month. Audio only, unlimited meetings, unlimited storage, max 1 hour per recording. The lower price is in the first card row, next to the selected Annual control. The FAQ says Bluedot charges only users who record, and its prorate example uses the $25 Pro price.
  • Bluedot Pro and Business: Pro is $20 or $25 and adds video and unlimited duration. Business is $32 or $39 and adds CRM and ATS, SSO, SCIM, and custom retention.
  • Fireflies Free: $0, called free forever. The pricing table, the free-plan article, and the AI-credits guide do not describe transcripts, storage, credits, or integrations the same way.
  • Fireflies Pro: $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly. That is the cheapest paid Fireflies plan. Business is $19 or $29. Enterprise is $39, annual only.

On the yearly numbers, Fireflies Pro at $10 is lower than Bluedot Basic at $14. They are not the same unit. Fireflies prices a seat. Bluedot says it prices users who record. Bluedot Basic is also audio only and capped at one hour. Fireflies Pro lists a 2-hour recording limit and video at 720p. Check Bluedot pricing and Fireflies pricing before you buy.

Privacy notes

Bluedot’s privacy policy, effective 8 January 2026, says customer content is stored and processed in the European Union by default. U.S. storage is an agreed arrangement. The policy says Bluedot does not use customer content to train AI models. Two official pages disagree on backups: the security page says deleted data is removed immediately and backups are cleared within 30 days. The privacy policy says active systems are cleared promptly and backup copies within 90 days. The homepage says Bluedot never asks for calendar access. The privacy policy says a connected Google Calendar can send event details.

Fireflies says the default is storage and processing in the United States on AWS and GCP. It says meeting content is not used to train Fireflies models. Encryption is described as 256-bit AES at rest and TLS 1.2 in transit. SOC 2 Type II and GDPR are on the pricing page. HIPAA with a BAA is Enterprise, plus private storage. Enterprise private storage can include the EU, and Fireflies says processing still happens in the US. The meeting owner can delete a meeting permanently. Bulk delete starts on Pro. Automatic deletion is Enterprise.

Neither set of pages was tested here. Read the privacy sections of the Bluedot review and the Fireflies review for the sentences these notes come from.

Who should pick which

Pick Bluedot if the official requirement is no bot in the participant list, and if a 5-meeting free cap and an audio-only Basic plan are acceptable. Pick Fireflies if a visible bot is acceptable, you want the $10 yearly Pro price, and you will read the free-plan conflicts before you rely on the $0 plan. If you need CRM or ATS on Bluedot, that is Business, not Basic.

FAQ

Does Bluedot or Fireflies put a bot in the meeting?

Bluedot's homepage says it never invites a bot. It records with a Chrome extension or a desktop app, and you do not have to be the host. Fireflies usually joins as Fireflies.ai Notetaker, and help says that presence cannot be hidden. Fireflies can record without a bot through a Google Meet Chrome extension or desktop Take Notes. Take Notes does not save a separate audio or video file. This page has not tested either product.

Which costs less, Bluedot or Fireflies?

On 19 November 2026 Fireflies Pro was $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly. Bluedot Basic was $14 or $18 per user per month. The lower Bluedot price sits with the Annual control. Bluedot says it charges only users who record. Fireflies prices a seat in the workspace. Free is not the same product: Fireflies calls its $0 plan free forever and its pages disagree on the limits. Bluedot allows 5 lifetime meetings, then view and share only.

Where do Bluedot and Fireflies store meetings?

Bluedot's privacy policy says customer content is stored and processed in the European Union by default, with U.S. storage by agreement. Its security page says backups are cleared within 30 days. Its privacy policy says 90 days. Fireflies says the default is storage and processing in the United States on AWS and GCP. Enterprise private storage can keep data in a dedicated location, including the EU, and Fireflies says processing still happens in the US.

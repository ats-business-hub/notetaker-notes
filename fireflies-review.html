Quick pick

Fireflies: Official pages checked 19 November 2026 describe a calendar-connected notetaker on Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams that appears as Fireflies.ai Notetaker. The same review notes narrower no-bot options, and list prices including Pro at $10 per seat per month billed annually.

Who it is for, and who should look elsewhere

Fireflies may fit a small team that wants a calendar-connected notetaker on Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, and that can accept a participant named Fireflies.ai Notetaker in the meeting. Sign-in on the pricing FAQ is Google or Outlook only. One calendar can be connected per account. Apple Calendar and Zoho are not supported directly; meetings on another calendar need a manual invite to fred@fireflies.ai.

It is a weaker fit if a visible bot is unacceptable and the team cannot use the narrower no-bot options below. It is also a weaker fit if you need one official description of the Free plan. The pricing page, the free-plan article, and the AI-credits guide do not describe storage, credits, transcripts, or integrations the same way.

How it works

Help says there are three ways to invite Fireflies: turn on auto-join, add fred@fireflies.ai to a Google or Outlook invite, or use “Add to live meeting” on the dashboard. If it is invited, it tries to join when the meeting starts. It appears as Fireflies.ai Notetaker. A host may have to admit it from a waiting room. On Google Meet, Fireflies says the bot can show under “potential risk”; Fireflies says that label is expected and is not a warning about the product.

The bot can record a meeting you do not attend if the platform allows a guest bot. It waits up to 10 minutes on Google Meet and 15 minutes on other platforms, then leaves. It stays 2 to 4 hours depending on the plan. The pricing table lists those caps as 2 hours on Free and Pro, 3 hours on Business, and 4 hours on Enterprise. You can remove it from the participant list; notes still cover the part that was recorded. You can also turn auto-join off or use bar rules so some meeting titles are skipped.

Recording without a bot is documented in two places. The joins FAQ says the Chrome extension records Google Meet only, with no bot, and that the mobile app is for in-person recording. The desktop article says “Take Notes” captures system audio on Windows or Mac, including Zoom, Meet, and Teams, and that no Fireflies.ai Notetaker joins. That mode does not save a separate audio or video file. It produces a transcript and summary. Speakers are labeled Speaker 1 and Speaker 2, not by name. You cannot switch from Take Notes to the bot in the middle of the same recording.

Plans and pricing

These are list prices on the Fireflies pricing page on 19 November 2026. Annual figures are the monthly amount when the year is paid up front. A seat is a teammate in the workspace. Sharing a recap link does not, by itself, add a paid seat. Check the current pricing page before you buy. A later walkthrough of the same list prices, free-plan conflicts, and add-ons, checked again on 20 November 2026, is in Fireflies.ai pricing explained.

  • Free: $0. The pricing page calls it free forever. The comparison table marks transcripts and AI summaries “Limited,” storage “400 mins/team,” and the AI-credits cell blank. Recording limit: 2 hours. Integrations cell: blank. API: 30 requests a minute and 500 a day.
  • Pro: $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly. The table marks transcripts and summaries unlimited, storage 8,000 minutes per seat, video 720p, AI credits 20, and integrations “All.” Recording limit: 2 hours. Bulk delete starts here.
  • Business: $19 per seat per month billed annually, or $29 billed monthly. Storage is unlimited. Recording limit: 3 hours. Video is also listed as 720p. AI credits: 30. The pricing FAQ says this tier adds higher pooled API limits, analytics, private channels, and video recording.
  • Enterprise: $39 per seat per month, annual billing only. Recording limit: 4 hours. Video: 1080p. AI credits: 50. Private storage, custom retention, SSO, SCIM, and automatic deletion of old meetings are listed here.

Renaming the bot is a paid add-on: $5 a month for one shared name across 5 users, or $5 a month per user for individual names. AI-credit packs are also separate. The smallest pack on the AI-credits guide is 50 credits for $5 a month, or $4 a month on annual billing. Unused purchased credits do not carry over.

Key features, as the official pages describe them

  • Live notes and transcription when the bot joins, and live transcript in desktop Take Notes.
  • Summaries. The free-plan article lists overview, detailed notes, and action items. The pricing table marks Free summaries “Limited” and paid summaries “Unlimited.” The unlimited-transcriptions article says summaries, action items, and analytics still depend on AI credits.
  • AskFred, described as an assistant that uses AI credits. The free-plan article lists it as something the monthly credits can try.
  • Search inside a meeting and across meetings, on the pricing table for every plan including Free.
  • Uploads of MP3, MP4, WAV, and M4A, up to 100 MB and 150 minutes, using 1 transcription credit. Speaker names are not detected on uploads.
  • Desktop, iOS, and Android apps are listed on the pricing page.
  • Slack can post a summary and an action-item checklist to a channel or DM. HubSpot’s product page describes notes, transcripts, action items, and tasks on contacts, companies, and deals. Which plans include those integrations is disputed below.
  • Languages: the language article lists more than 100 transcription languages, including Japanese (ja), plus auto-detect. Multi-language mode is marked beta in that list and, on the pricing table, is not checked for Free or Pro.

Limitations and things to watch

The bot is visible

Fireflies says you cannot hide the bot. It is in the participant list and it posts in chat. Help says that chat message alone may not meet consent laws, so tell people and get consent where it is required. A Meeting Compliance setting can show a disclaimer before the meeting. If someone opts out, help says the bot will not join. You can re-invite it, and Fireflies says it is better not to force a recording.

Desktop Take Notes can notify people with an audio message about 10 seconds after recording starts, a Mac-only chat message, and a camera watermark. Those options can be turned off. Fireflies says that if you turn them off, you are responsible for telling participants and for following recording laws. The desktop article also says some states require everyone’s consent for phone and in-person recordings. This page is not legal advice. Consent rules vary by state and by country.

Free plan, credits, and integrations do not match across pages

Transcription. The pricing table marks Free transcripts “Limited.” The free-plan article says 3 meetings are included and that auto-join allows unlimited transcriptions. The unlimited-transcriptions article says that applies to signups on or after 1 September 2026, that auto-join off means 3 transcriptions, and that the “only when I invite fred@fireflies.ai” filter does not unlock the unlimited path. The joins FAQ says free transcription is available with the Chrome extension or mobile app, or with the unlimited toggle and sharing recaps with all invitees. The unlimited-transcriptions article does not mention that sharing requirement.

Storage. The pricing page says 400 minutes per team. The free-plan article says 400 minutes per seat, and that you delete older files when you hit the limit. Pro is 8,000 minutes per seat on the pricing page. Business and Enterprise are unlimited there. The free-plan article says unlimited storage is on Business.

AI credits. The free-plan article says 20 credits per month for AskFred, soundbites, and AI skills. The AI-credits guide says every plan gets one-time credits on sign-up — 20 on Free — shared by the team, not increased when you add seats, and not expired. The pricing table shows 20 on Pro, 30 on Business, and 50 on Enterprise, and leaves Free blank. The guide says transcription and recap email are not charged in credits, while custom summary sections, voice agents, and AskFred on Live Assist are. It also says free users who run out see a paywall and are not auto-charged. A later FAQ answer describes an automatic $5-a-month subscription for 50 credits when plan credits run out, without repeating the free-user exception.

Integrations. The free-plan article says all integrations are on Free, including a “50+ apps” line that mentions CRMs. The pricing table marks Integrations blank on Free and “All” on Pro, Business, and Enterprise. The pricing FAQ says app and CRM integrations are on premium tiers, and that sending audio through Zapier starts on Pro. The Slack setup article still describes workspace settings for Free and Pro, and says the finer filters (including “external” meetings) are Business or higher. The HubSpot page describes the sync and does not name a plan.

Data and security, as Fireflies states it

The storage article says the default is storage and processing in the United States on AWS and GCP. Enterprise private storage can keep data in dedicated storage, including the EU, and Fireflies says processing still happens in the US. It says customers own their data, that meeting content is not used to train Fireflies models, and that AI vendors are contractually barred from training on it. Encryption is described as 256-bit AES at rest and TLS 1.2 in transit. The pricing page says SOC 2 Type II and GDPR, and HIPAA with a BAA on Enterprise plus private storage. Only the meeting owner can delete a meeting. Deletion is permanent. Bulk delete starts on Pro. Automatic deletion is Enterprise.

Language

Japanese (ja) is on the transcription list, and Japanese is also one of 16 interface languages. The interface language does not change the transcript. The pricing FAQ still says English is the primary transcription language and that Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, and 100+ other languages are in beta. I have not tested accuracy.

How to introduce a notetaker bot to clients

This is a practical script, not a Fireflies template and not legal advice. Recording-consent laws vary by state and by country. Say it before the bot joins, and skip the bot if the client says no. Fireflies says an opt-out means the bot will not join.

Before we start, I use a notetaker so I can stay in the conversation instead of typing. It joins as Fireflies.ai Notetaker, so you will see it in the participant list, and it posts a short note in the chat. It records the call and sends me a transcript and summary afterward. Is it all right if it joins? If not, I will take notes myself and it will not join.

If you use desktop Take Notes instead, do not tell the client a bot will appear. Tell them the computer is capturing audio. Fireflies says people know about Take Notes only if a notice is on, or if you tell them.

FAQ

Does Fireflies put a bot in the meeting?

Usually, yes. Fireflies help says the bot appears as Fireflies.ai Notetaker, may wait to be admitted, and posts in the meeting chat. The same article says that presence cannot be hidden. A Google Meet Chrome extension and a desktop Take Notes mode can record without the bot.

Is Fireflies free?

Fireflies publishes a Free plan at $0 and calls it free forever on the pricing page. Official pages do not agree on the Free limits. The pricing table marks transcripts and summaries Limited and storage as 400 minutes per team. Free-plan help says 3 meetings are included, auto-join can remove the transcription cap, storage is 400 minutes per seat, and 20 AI credits are monthly. The AI-credits guide says those 20 credits are one-time.

How much does Fireflies cost?

On 19 November 2026 the pricing page listed Pro at $10 per seat monthly when billed annually or $18 billed monthly, Business at $19 or $29 on the same pattern, and Enterprise at $39 per seat monthly with annual billing only. Check the current pricing page before you buy. A bot-name add-on and AI-credit packs are priced separately.

Does Fireflies support Japanese?

The supported-languages article lists Japanese, code ja, for transcription and summaries, and also lists Japanese as an interface language. The pricing FAQ still says English is the primary language and that other languages are in beta. This overview has not tested accuracy.

Where does Fireflies store meetings?

Fireflies says the default is storage and processing in the United States on AWS and GCP. Enterprise private storage can keep data in a dedicated location, including the EU, and Fireflies says processing still happens in the US. The meeting owner can delete a meeting permanently. Bulk delete starts on Pro. Automatic deletion is Enterprise.

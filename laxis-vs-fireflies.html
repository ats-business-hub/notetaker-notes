Laxis vs Fireflies for Sales Teams (2026)

The facts come from official pages as written up in the Laxis review and the Fireflies.ai review. I have not used either product on a sales call. What a sales team is buying

Laxis puts meeting notes and a voice keyboard on one subscription. They share one pool of transcription minutes, so dictation and live translation spend the same minutes as calls. Premium includes AI reports with 40+ templates. The billing page says those templates include sales frameworks such as MEDDIC and BANT. CRM automation for HubSpot, Salesforce, and Wealthbox is listed on Business. The integrations article says connecting a CRM requires Business, and you can connect one CRM at a time. Homepage structured data says Premium includes HubSpot and Salesforce CRM sync. I am not treating the structured data and the help center as the same fact.

Laxis also sells Prospect Research and an AI SDR as a separate Sales Agent subscription. Prospect Research Premium is $59 a month or $49 a month billed yearly. That price is not the meeting-assistant price. There is no self-serve Business trial for the meeting product. New accounts get 14 days of Premium with no card.

Fireflies documents HubSpot notes, transcripts, action items, and tasks, and a Slack summary. The pricing table marks integrations All on Pro and above, and leaves Free blank. The free-plan article says all integrations are on Free. The pricing FAQ says app and CRM integrations are on premium tiers. A sales team that needs HubSpot should read those three pages, not one of them. Renaming the Fireflies bot is a paid add-on, from $5 a month. Laxis puts a personalized assistant name on Business.

Bot or no bot on a sales call

The Laxis homepage says the note taker is bot-free. Help describes three paths: desktop capture with no bot, a bot that joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams, and a Chrome extension that captures browser captions without a bot. Bot meetings show a video player. Extension meetings are text only. The privacy policy says some options show participants that a notetaker is present and some do not, and that you are responsible for consent.

Fireflies usually joins as Fireflies.ai Notetaker. Help says that name cannot be hidden and that the chat message alone may not meet consent laws. The no-bot paths are the Google Meet extension and desktop Take Notes. Take Notes does not save a separate recording file. For a client who will notice a new participant, the Laxis desktop path and the Fireflies Take Notes path are the documented ways to avoid a bot. They are not the same feature. I have not tested them.

What the seats cost

  • Laxis Basic: $0. 300 minutes a month, 45 minutes per recording, 30 days of history. Older meetings are archived, not deleted.
  • Laxis Premium: $15.99 a month, or $9.99 a month billed yearly ($119.88 a year). 2,000 minutes, 2-hour recordings, templates, AI chat, and the voice keyboard’s agent mode. This is the notes-and-templates tier, not the CRM tier in the help center.
  • Laxis Business: $29.99 a month, or $19.99 a month billed yearly ($239.88 a year). Unlimited transcription, CRM automation, Zapier, team seats, and video replay. The billing owner buys seats. Five people with a Business seat, at the yearly web price, are 5 times $19.99 a month.
  • Fireflies Pro: $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly. Five seats at the yearly price are 5 times $10 a month. Business is $19 or $29. Enterprise is $39, annual only, and that is where HIPAA with a BAA and automatic deletion are listed.

If the requirement is templates without a CRM connection, Laxis Premium at $9.99 yearly is the lower yearly list price, just under Fireflies Pro at $10. If the requirement is the CRM the Laxis help center describes, the comparison is Laxis Business at $19.99 yearly against Fireflies Pro at $10 yearly, and Fireflies’ own pages still disagree about whether that CRM is on Pro. Check Laxis pricing, Laxis billing, and Fireflies pricing before you buy.

Privacy notes that matter on sales calls

The Laxis homepage says meeting and dictation data are never used to train models. The privacy policy, last updated 10 November 2026, says Laxis does not use that content to train models without consent, and that any training program would be opt-in. The homepage says data is stored on Google Cloud. The privacy policy does not name a provider or a region. Data residency is an Enterprise feature. Voice recognition is off by default. If you turn it on, Laxis stores a voiceprint, and the policy says you are responsible for consent.

Fireflies says meeting content is not used to train Fireflies models, and that the default location is the United States. A sales team that needs a BAA is pointed at Fireflies Enterprise, not at Pro. I did not find a HIPAA claim on the Laxis pages I read.

Who should pick which

Pick Laxis if the team wants MEDDIC or BANT templates on Premium, a voice keyboard in the same minute pool, and it will pay Business if the help-center CRM rule is the one you follow. Pick Fireflies if the team wants the $10 yearly seat, a calendar bot with a wide integration list, and someone will reconcile the free-plan and CRM pages before rollout. Japanese transcription is listed for Fireflies. Laxis help says 60 transcription languages and does not publish the list, so I cannot confirm Japanese transcription for Laxis from those pages. The app interface does include Japanese.

FAQ

Which plan includes a CRM for a sales team?

Laxis help says connecting HubSpot, Salesforce, or Wealthbox requires the Business plan, one CRM at a time. Business is $29.99 a month, or $19.99 a month billed yearly. Homepage structured data says Premium includes HubSpot and Salesforce sync. Those two Laxis pages do not agree. Fireflies documents HubSpot and Salesforce. Its pricing table marks integrations All on Pro, Business, and Enterprise, and blank on Free. Other Fireflies pages say integrations are included on Free, or that CRM starts on a premium tier. Read the plan gate on the current page before you buy.

Does Laxis or Fireflies put a bot in the meeting?

The Laxis homepage says the note taker is bot-free. Laxis help also documents a bot that joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams, plus desktop capture with no bot and a Chrome extension that captures captions without a bot. Fireflies usually joins as Fireflies.ai Notetaker, which help says cannot be hidden. Fireflies can skip the bot with a Google Meet extension or desktop Take Notes. This page has not tested either product.

What does a sales team actually pay?

If the team only needs notes and sales templates, Laxis Premium is $15.99 a month or $9.99 a month billed yearly, and it includes templates such as MEDDIC and BANT. CRM automation is the Business price above. Fireflies Pro is $10 per seat per month billed annually or $18 billed monthly, which is the first paid tier and the one whose pricing table says integrations are included. A five-person team is five times the per-seat price if each person has that seat. Laxis also sells Prospect Research as a separate subscription, starting at $59 a month or $49 billed yearly. That is not the meeting plan.

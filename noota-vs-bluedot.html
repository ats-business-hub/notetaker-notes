Noota vs Bluedot for Small Teams (2026)

How each one records

Bluedot’s homepage title is “Bluedot AI Note Taker | Bot-Free & Privacy-First.” The pricing cards list a Chrome extension and desktop and mobile apps on the Free plan. I am repeating that official wording; I have not watched a Bluedot recorder on a call.

Noota’s AI note-taker page says Noota takes notes of online meetings, phone calls, and in-person conversations, “with or without a bot.” The same page’s FAQ says Noota records Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Webex meetings, with a bot that joins the call or without one from the desktop app, and that in-person meetings and phone calls are covered by the mobile app and the built-in recorder. The pricing FAQ says Noota can join as a bot with the name you choose, or record quietly from your computer with the desktop app on the Pro plan and above, and that participants are notified that the meeting is recorded. The pricing comparison table marks recording mode on Free as “Bot only,” and on Pro, Business, and Enterprise as “Bot or invisible.”

Free plans

Bluedot’s pricing cards say “Start free, forever” at $0 per user per month, with “5 meetings / lifetime.” The homepage FAQ I saved says Bluedot is not a free AI meeting note taker in the sense of an uncapped free product, and that the Free plan lets you try core features for your first 5 meetings (a lifetime trial).

Noota’s Free card is €0, with no card needed, and lists unlimited meeting notes with an asterisk. The footnote, repeated in the FAQ, says unlimited recording on the Free plan requires a connected calendar with Join all meetings enabled, and that the meeting summary is automatically shared with all participants; otherwise live recordings and standard recordings are limited to 300 minutes per month. The Free card also lists one month of storage. The products are not equivalent, and I am not declaring a single winner from those different caps.

Paid prices, as of 21 November 2026

Bluedot’s pricing page has Annual and Monthly toggles. On the Annual cards I saved, Basic is $14 per user per month (“The essentials, audio only,” unlimited meetings with audio only), Pro is $20 (“No limits, with video”), and Business is $32 (“Advanced workflows & controls”). On the Monthly cards, Basic is $18, Pro is $25, and Business is $39. The FAQ says Bluedot only charges for users who record meetings, bills added paid users at a prorated price, and bills per workspace. On cancellation, the FAQ says you lose access to your recordings, you have one month to restore the subscription, and after that recordings are permanently deleted.

Noota’s pricing page says all prices are in euros (EUR). Pro is €19 per user per month billed annually, or €29 billed monthly. Business is €39 billed annually, or €49 billed monthly. A banner and the plan cards described Black Friday as 40% off any new annual Pro or Business plan until Monday, November 30, which brought the first-year annual rates on the cards I saved to €11.40 per user per month (€136.80 for the first year instead of €228) and €23.40 (€280.80 instead of €468). The page says there is no discount on the monthly plan. I have not converted euros to dollars.

Who should pick which

These suggestions follow only what the official pages say. I have not tested accuracy, the bot, or the desktop or Chrome recorder.

Look at Bluedot if you want a product whose official homepage calls itself bot-free, prices in U.S. dollars, and a Free plan with a 5-meeting lifetime cap rather than a monthly minute cap, and you can accept that only recorders are billed, as the FAQ states. Look at Noota if you want prices and hosting described in European terms (the pricing and AI note-taker pages say meeting data is hosted in Europe, with a France option, and that data is not used to train AI models; the pricing page also lists ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II), a first-year Black Friday annual rate that was still on the page as of this check, and you can accept a bot-only Free plan, or pay for Pro when you want the desktop “invisible” mode.

FAQ

Can I record without a bot?

Bluedot’s homepage presents the product as bot-free, and the pricing cards list a Chrome extension plus desktop and mobile apps on Free. Noota’s pricing table marks bot-free (“invisible”) recording on Pro and above; Free is marked bot-only. This page has not tested either product.

Which free plan records more meetings?

Bluedot’s Free card is 5 meetings for the life of the account. Noota’s Free unlimited line depends on a connected calendar, Join all meetings, and auto-sharing the summary; otherwise the cap is 300 minutes a month. I am not declaring a single winner from those different conditions.

Which costs less, Noota or Bluedot?

They publish prices in different currencies, and I have not converted them. As of 21 November 2026, Noota Pro was €19 per user per month billed annually, or €29 billed monthly, with a first-year Black Friday annual rate of €11.40 still on the page through the dated offer ending Monday, November 30. Bluedot Pro was $20 per user per month on the Annual cards, or $25 on the Monthly cards. Check both live pages before you buy.

Circleback vs Fireflies for Small Teams (2026)

Last checked: November 19, 2026. This page compares Circleback and Fireflies from their official pages, as written up in the Circleback review and the Fireflies.ai review. I have not used either product on a client call. The four-tool table is Best AI meeting note takers for small teams (2026).

How each one records

Circleback’s homepage says you can use a bot or the bot-free desktop app for macOS and Windows. The desktop FAQ says the app records meeting video by default and that what is on screen goes into the notes. The same is true of saved bot video. Calendars named on the homepage are Google and Outlook. Platforms named there are Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Slack huddles, and GoTo, plus in-person recording.

Fireflies usually joins as Fireflies.ai Notetaker. Help says the bot cannot be hidden. The no-bot options are a Chrome extension for Google Meet and desktop Take Notes, which produces a transcript and summary without a separate audio or video file and without named speakers. Auto-join, a calendar invite to fred@fireflies.ai, or “Add to live meeting” are the bot paths.

Free plans

Circleback Free is $0. The card includes notes, action items, Ask Circleback, a bot or the desktop app, mobile and Apple Watch, speaker-labeled transcripts, limited automations, 30-day meeting and recording history, and API, MCP, and CLI access. The pricing FAQ says every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings. Full integration automations, including the HubSpot and Salesforce line, are on Pro, not on Free.

Fireflies Free is $0 and the pricing page calls it free forever. That is where the official pages split. The pricing table marks transcripts and summaries Limited and storage as 400 minutes per team. Free-plan help says 3 meetings are included, auto-join can remove the transcription cap, and storage is 400 minutes per seat. The AI-credits guide says the 20 Free credits are one-time, while free-plan help calls them monthly. I am not resolving that here. The Fireflies review quotes each page.

Paid prices

  • Circleback Pro: $15 per user per month on the Yearly view. Unlimited meeting history, 1 year of recording storage, and automations with the integrations the card names, including Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, and webhooks. The Monthly tab’s prices were not in the HTML.
  • Circleback Business: $25 per user per month on that same view. Unlimited free viewers, custom retention, and 2 years of recording storage. Enterprise is $35, with HIPAA listed on that card.
  • Fireflies Pro: $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly. The pricing table marks integrations All on Pro. Other Fireflies pages still disagree about Free. Business is $19 or $29. Enterprise is $39, annual only.

On yearly list prices, Fireflies Pro at $10 is lower than Circleback Pro at $15. Circleback’s Free plan is the one that states unlimited meetings. Fireflies’ Free plan is the one its own pages describe in more than one way. Viewers who only read notes are a paid Circleback seat until Business. Check Circleback pricing and Fireflies pricing before you buy.

Privacy notes

Circleback’s privacy policy, effective 15 July 2024, says the services are hosted and operated in the United States and that personal data will be hosted on U.S. servers, and possibly in other countries. The homepage says Circleback does not use customer data to train models. The trust center lists SOC 2 Type 2, HIPAA, and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and it does not name a storage region. HIPAA is also a line on the Enterprise pricing card. The cancellation FAQ does not say what happens to recordings.

Fireflies says default storage and processing are in the United States, that meeting content is not used to train Fireflies models, and that deletion by the meeting owner is permanent. HIPAA with a BAA is Enterprise. The free-plan conflicts above are about limits, not about the storage region.

Who should pick which

Pick Circleback if you want a stated unlimited-meeting free plan, a desktop app without a bot, and you can accept 30 days of history until you pay, plus screen content in the notes. Pick Fireflies if you want the lower yearly Pro price, a calendar bot, and you will read the free-plan pages before you depend on them. Japanese is named on Circleback’s language list and on Fireflies’ transcription list. I have not tested accuracy in either product.

FAQ

Can I record without a bot?

Circleback's homepage says yes: a desktop app for macOS and Windows records without a bot. By default it records meeting video, and what is on screen goes into the notes. Circleback can also send a bot. Fireflies usually sends a bot named Fireflies.ai Notetaker. Its no-bot options are a Google Meet Chrome extension and desktop Take Notes, which does not save a separate audio or video file. This page has not tested either product.

Which free plan records more meetings?

Circleback's pricing FAQ says every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings. The Free card limits history to 30 days and says automations are limited. Fireflies publishes a $0 plan and calls it free forever. Its pricing table, free-plan article, and AI-credits guide do not describe the meeting cap, storage, or credits the same way. I am not declaring a winner from pages that disagree.

Which costs less, Circleback or Fireflies?

On 19 November 2026, with Circleback's Yearly tab selected, Pro was $15 per user per month. A Monthly price was not in that page's HTML. The 50-user annual FAQ matches $15 times 12. Fireflies Pro was $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly. On the yearly figures, Fireflies Pro is the lower list price. Circleback Business, at $25 on that same Yearly view, is where unlimited free viewers start.

A help FAQ asks why some people only see Team options instead of Pro. To upgrade to Pro, it says, you need to leave the current team first, then open Billing.

AI credits, transcript limits, and cancellation

The pricing FAQ says one AI credit covers a single AI action: generating a meeting summary, drafting action items, or asking Tactiq AI a question. Free includes 5 credits per month, Pro includes 10, and Team and above include unlimited AI credits. Unlimited transcripts start on Pro.

If you hit a transcript or AI-credit limit, the FAQ says Tactiq keeps transcribing, but AI features pause until the limit resets at the start of the next month, or until you upgrade, and that existing transcripts stay accessible. You can change or cancel later from billing settings. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. The FAQ also says Tactiq does not offer a separate time-boxed free trial, because the Free plan has no credit card and no time limit.

What the plan-comparison table marks as included

The pricing page also has a longer comparison table. I have not reproduced every row. A few cells matter if you are choosing a plan from the official page alone:

  • The Chrome extension for Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams transcriptions, Ask Tactiq AI, in-meeting AI, share and export, upload meeting files, and workflow integrations are marked included on every plan, including Free, within the transcript and credit caps above.
  • Managing in-meeting transcript notifications, and “Always ask about transcribing,” are marked not included on Free, and included on Pro and above.
  • Auto-share transcripts with teams, and admin-only team invites, are marked included from Team and above, not on Free or Pro.
  • AI Meeting Agent, SAML SSO, advanced data retention, and a customer success manager are marked included from Business and above.
  • SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, ISO 27001, and HIPAA are marked included on every plan, including Free.

The Team card’s short bullets highlight priority customer support, and the comparison table marks that row as included on Pro and above, and not included on Free. I am reporting both placements, not choosing between them.

Education pricing is offered, but the amount is not published

The pricing page has a “Tactiq for Education” section that says teachers and students can get special pricing by applying for an education plan. It does not publish that price. A help article on upgrading your plan says individual university students and teachers can apply with a school-issued email after signing up, and that the discount cannot be combined with other promotional offers. I have not applied, so I do not know the discounted amount.

Questions, answered from the official pages

How much does Tactiq cost?

As of 20 November 2026, Free was $0 with no credit card; Pro was $8 per user per month billed annually, or $12 monthly in a help article dated 30 June 2026; Team was $16.67 or $20; Business was $29.17 on the pricing page or $29.16 in that help article, and $40 monthly; and Enterprise was custom for 200 or more users. Check the live page before you buy.

Is the Free plan really free?

The pricing page calls it free forever, with no credit card. It includes 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits per month. There is no separate time-boxed trial, according to the pricing FAQ, because Free has no time limit.

What is a Tactiq AI credit, and which plan has unlimited credits?

One credit is one AI action, such as a summary, action items, or a question to Tactiq AI. Free has 5 credits a month, Pro has 10, and Team and above have unlimited AI credits. Unlimited transcripts start on Pro.

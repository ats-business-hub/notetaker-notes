Quick verdict by use case

There is no single winner. These are readings of the official pages, not test results.

  • No bot in the participant list, from the default setup. Tactiq’s homepage says no bot joins the call. Otter’s usual Notetaker joins as a visible guest and, according to help, cannot join anonymously. Otter does document a botless path through its desktop app.
  • A phone app, or recording playback of the meeting audio. Otter’s pricing page lists iOS and Android apps, live transcription, and audio recording playback. Tactiq’s download page lists a Chrome extension and a Mac app, and the homepage FAQ says Tactiq does not record or store meeting audio.
  • A cheaper annual Pro seat, if you can work with a monthly AI-credit cap. Tactiq Pro is $8 per user per month billed annually, with unlimited transcripts and 10 AI credits a month. Otter’s unstarred annual Pro list price on 20 November 2026 was $8.33, with 1,200 in-app recording minutes and a 90-minute meeting cap. Otter’s page also showed other starred Pro figures. These are different limits, not interchangeable seats.
  • Languages beyond Otter’s published six. Tactiq’s homepage says it supports over 60 languages. Otter’s pricing comparison table lists English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and Chinese.
  • A notetaker that can join a meeting you do not attend. Otter’s Notetaker overview describes a guest participant that joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams once a calendar is connected. Tactiq’s official pages describe capture from your browser or, for Zoom desktop, from a Mac app, with you in the meeting.

Bots, captions, and recording without a bot

Tactiq’s homepage says you get live transcriptions without an AI bot joining the meeting. The install help article says Tactiq uses the meeting platform’s captions, and to keep captions turned on. A Microsoft Teams help article says the Teams desktop application is not currently supported, and tells you to continue in the browser or the Teams PWA.

Otter’s Notetaker overview, updated 21 August 2026, says Notetaker automatically joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams to record and transcribe in real time once a calendar is connected. It answers “Can Notetaker join a meeting anonymously or without other participants seeing it?” with no. The same article’s tip for botless recording is to use the Otter Desktop app. The desktop-app help, updated 6 November 2026, repeats that you can record without an AI bot joining as a participant, and says you are responsible for notifying participants and obtaining any required consent.

I have not tested whether Tactiq’s caption-based transcript, or Otter’s visible Notetaker or desktop app, is obvious to the other person on the call. Both vendors still describe notice in some form. This page is not legal advice.

Who might pick which, on the official pages alone

Pick Tactiq if you want a browser extension that official pages say does not join as a bot, you can keep captions on, and you are happy with text transcripts rather than stored audio. It also fits if you want a $0 plan with no card, or an $8 annual Pro seat with unlimited transcripts, and you can work within the published AI-credit caps. Longer notes are in the Tactiq review.

Pick Otter if you want a phone app, audio playback, a notetaker that can join when you are not in the meeting, or a desktop botless path that official help documents on both macOS and Windows. Read the starred Pro prices carefully if a footnote limits the offer. Teams already on Otter who are weighing a switch can also use Otter.ai alternatives.

Questions, answered from the official pages

Is Tactiq or Otter free?

Both publish a $0 plan. Tactiq Free is $0 with no credit card, 10 transcripts per month, and 5 AI credits per month. Otter Basic is free, with 300 monthly transcription minutes and a 30-minute per-conversation cap on the comparison table.

How much do Tactiq and Otter cost?

As of 20 November 2026, Tactiq Pro was $8 per user per month billed annually, or $12 monthly in a help article, Team was $16.67 or $20, and Business was $29.17 on the pricing page or $29.16 in that help article, and $40 monthly. Otter Pro was $16.99 monthly or $8.33 annual, and Business was $30 monthly or $19.99 annual, plus the starred and short-term figures quoted above. Check both live pages before you buy.

Does Tactiq or Otter join the call as a bot?

Tactiq’s official pages say no bot joins, and that transcription runs from a Chrome or Edge extension, using the meeting platform’s captions. Otter’s Notetaker joins as a visible guest and cannot join anonymously. Otter’s help points to the desktop app for botless recording, on macOS 13.0 or higher or Windows 10 or higher.

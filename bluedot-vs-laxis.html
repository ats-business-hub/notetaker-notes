Bluedot vs Laxis for Small Teams (2026)

Longer write-ups are in the Bluedot review, Bluedot pricing, Laxis review, and Laxis pricing. A three-way overview is in bot-free meeting recorders.

  • Laxis: Official pages describe a bot-free meeting assistant, plus a voice keyboard that shares the same monthly transcription minutes. How each one records

Bluedot’s homepage title is “Bluedot AI Note Taker | Bot-Free & Privacy-First.” The pricing cards list a Chrome extension and desktop and mobile apps.

Laxis’s homepage says “Laxis transcribes every meeting into polished AI notes, tasks, and reports — bot-free, in 100+ languages.” The pricing page says you can “Record online & in-person meetings,” and that every plan includes both the AI meeting assistant and the AI voice keyboard, which “draw from the same pool of monthly transcription minutes.” I am repeating that official wording; I have not watched either recorder on a call.

Free plans and the Laxis trial

Bluedot’s Free card is $0 per user per month, with 5 meetings / lifetime. The homepage wording I saved calls that a lifetime trial of core features for the first 5 meetings.

Laxis lists a Basic plan at $0, with 300 minutes a month, 30-day conversation history, and, in the comparison table, a 45-minute cap per recording. The pricing FAQ says every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of Premium, with no credit card required, and that when the trial ends you move to the free Basic plan and keep all your notes. A Business team trial is arranged through sales. The FAQ also says that when Basic history is older than 30 days, meetings “are archived, not deleted.” I am not declaring a winner between a 5-meeting lifetime cap and a 300-minute monthly cap.

Paid prices, as of 21 November 2026

Bluedot’s Annual cards list Basic at $14 per user per month (audio only, unlimited meetings with audio only, unlimited storage), Pro at $20 (video), and Business at $32. The Monthly cards list $18, $25, and $39. The FAQ says only recorders are billed, added seats are prorated, payment is per workspace, and cancelled subscriptions lose access to recordings, with one month to restore before permanent deletion.

Laxis’s public pricing page, with the Yearly control and “Save up to 38%” label I saved, listed Premium at $9.99 a month and Business at $19.99 a month. The page’s structured data describes Premium and Business as billed yearly. The official billing help article lists both cadences in one table: Premium $15.99 a month, or $9.99 a month billed yearly ($119.88 a year); Business $29.99 a month, or $19.99 a month billed yearly ($239.88 a year). The same help table repeats 2,000 minutes a month and a 2-hour cap on Premium, and unlimited minutes with a 4-hour cap on Business. Website purchases are billed in USD through Stripe, the pricing FAQ says; amounts charged inside the iOS or Android apps can differ because of app-store regional tiers and tax. Check both the pricing page and the billing help article before you buy.

Who should pick which

These suggestions follow only what the official pages say. I have not tested accuracy or the apps.

Look at Laxis if you want a stated 14-day Premium trial with no credit card, a $0 Basic plan with a monthly minute pool that also covers the voice keyboard, and 100+ languages as described on the homepage. Look at Bluedot if you want annual and monthly list prices per recording user, a Chrome extension plus desktop and mobile apps on the Free card, and you can accept a 5-meeting lifetime cap on Free rather than a monthly minute cap. Plans are not equivalent: Bluedot’s Basic card is labeled audio only, and Laxis minutes are shared across meetings, dictation, and live translation, according to the pricing FAQ.

FAQ

Are both of these bot-free?

Bluedot’s homepage calls the product bot-free. Laxis’s homepage uses the same “bot-free” wording, with 100+ languages. This page has not tested either recorder.

Which free plan records more meetings?

Bluedot Free is 5 meetings for the life of the account. Laxis Basic is 300 minutes a month, with a 45-minute cap per recording and 30-day history, plus a 14-day Premium trial for new accounts. I am not declaring a single winner from those different conditions.

Which costs less, Bluedot or Laxis?

As of 21 November 2026, Laxis Premium was $9.99 a month billed yearly ($119.88 a year), or $15.99 billed monthly, in the billing help table. Bluedot Pro was $20 per user per month on the Annual cards, or $25 monthly; Bluedot Basic was $14 annually or $18 monthly, and is labeled audio only. Check both live pages before you buy.

How to choose

Start with the constraint you cannot bend. The official pages do not support a single winner.

  • No bot in the room. Bluedot’s pages describe only device capture. Circleback, Laxis, and Fireflies each document a no-bot path and a bot path. Noota’s pricing table marks bot-free (“invisible”) recording from Pro; Free is bot only. Read the no-bot limits: Fireflies Take Notes does not save a separate audio or video file and labels speakers as Speaker 1 and Speaker 2. Circleback’s desktop app records video by default, and what is on screen goes into the notes. Laxis’s Chrome extension meetings are text only.
  • Keep recording on a free plan. Circleback says unlimited meetings on Free, with 30 days of history. Laxis Basic is 300 minutes a month and a 45-minute cap per recording. Fireflies calls Free free forever, and its pricing page, free-plan article, and AI-credits guide do not describe the limits the same way. Bluedot stops new recordings after 5 lifetime meetings. Noota Free is €0 with one month of storage; official pages disagree on when recording is unlimited versus capped at 300 minutes a month, and the unlimited path described on the pricing page uses a bot.
  • Lowest published yearly price. Among the USD tools, Laxis Premium is $9.99 per month billed yearly. Fireflies Pro is $10 per seat per month billed annually. Those are not the CRM tiers. Circleback’s cheapest paid price on the Yearly view is $15. Bluedot’s cheapest paid price is $14 or $18 per recording user. Monthly billing is higher where a monthly price is published. Noota’s cheapest paid list price is Pro at €19 per user per month billed annually, or €29 billed monthly, checked 20 November 2026. This page does not convert that euro price into dollars.
  • CRM after the call. Laxis help says HubSpot, Salesforce, and Wealthbox require Business. Circleback’s Pro card names HubSpot and Salesforce. Fireflies documents both, and its own pages disagree about whether Free includes integrations. Bluedot puts CRM and ATS on Business. Noota’s pricing table marks work tools (CRM and ATS) from Pro, and Zapier and the API from Business. A logo on a homepage is not a plan.

Fireflies

Fireflies usually joins as Fireflies.ai Notetaker. Help says that presence cannot be hidden. A Google Meet Chrome extension and desktop Take Notes can record without the bot. The cheapest paid plan on 19 November 2026 was Pro at $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly. The free plan is $0. Official pages do not agree on transcripts, storage, credits, or which integrations are included. Default storage and processing are in the United States. The full conflict list is in the Fireflies.ai review.

Bluedot

Bluedot’s homepage says it never invites a bot. Recording is a Chrome extension or a desktop app, and you do not have to be the host. The cheapest paid plan is Basic, audio only, at $14 or $18 per user per month. The pricing page shows both, with the lower set next to the selected Annual control. Free is 5 lifetime meetings, then view and share. Customer content is stored in the EU by default. The homepage says Bluedot never asks for calendar access, and the privacy policy describes calendar data if you connect Google Calendar. Details are in the Bluedot review.

See Bluedot plans

Circleback

Circleback documents a bot and a bot-free desktop app for macOS and Windows. The desktop app records video by default, and screen content can enter the notes. Free includes unlimited meetings and 30 days of history. The cheapest paid plan on the Yearly view is Pro at $15 per user per month, which is where the card names HubSpot, Salesforce, and other automations. A Monthly price was not in the pricing HTML. The privacy policy says the service is hosted in the United States. Details are in the Circleback review.

See Circleback plans

Laxis

Laxis sells meeting notes and a voice keyboard on one subscription. They share one pool of minutes. The homepage says the note taker is bot-free. Help also documents a bot for Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams. Premium, the cheapest paid plan, is $9.99 per month billed yearly or $15.99 billed monthly, and it includes sales templates such as MEDDIC and BANT. CRM automation is Business, at $19.99 yearly or $29.99 monthly, and the help center says connecting a CRM requires Business. Homepage structured data lists HubSpot and Salesforce on Premium. The homepage says data is never used to train models. The privacy policy says not without consent. Details are in the Laxis review. For the sales comparison, see Laxis vs Fireflies.

See Laxis plans

Noota

Noota records online meetings with a bot, or, on Pro and above, from the desktop app without a bot. The pricing table marks Free as “Bot only.” List prices, checked 20 November 2026, are in euros: Free is €0 with no card and one month of storage; Pro is €19 per user per month billed annually, or €29 billed monthly. Official pages do not agree on the Free recording limit, or on whether several features marked included on Free on the pricing table start on Pro in the help center. Default hosting is in the European Union. The security page says conversations are not used to train AI models, including on Free, and that Noota itself does not hold a HIPAA certification. Details, including the conflicts, are in the Noota review.

Tactiq

Tactiq is a Chrome or Edge extension for Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams. Official pages say it transcribes in the browser, with no bot joining the call, and that meeting audio is not recorded or stored. Help says Tactiq uses the meeting platform’s captions, and to keep captions turned on. The download page also lists a Mac app for Zoom desktop calls only, and a help article says the Microsoft Teams desktop application is not currently supported. I did not find an iPhone or Android app on the download page. As of 20 November 2026, the official pricing page listed Free at $0, with no credit card, 10 transcripts per month, and 5 AI credits per month; Pro at $8 per user per month billed annually, with unlimited transcripts and 10 AI credits; Team at $16.67 per user per month billed annually, with unlimited AI credits; and Business at $29.17 per user per month billed annually. A help article dated 30 June 2026 also lists monthly prices of $12, $20, and $40 for Pro, Team, and Business. Details are in the Tactiq review, Tactiq vs Otter.ai, and Tactiq alternatives.

FAQ

Which paid plan is the cheapest?

On 20 November 2026 the lowest published yearly price among the USD tools on this page was Tactiq Pro at $8 per user per month billed annually, then Laxis Premium at $9.99 per month billed yearly, then Fireflies Pro at $10 per seat per month billed annually. Bluedot Basic is $14 or $18 per user per month. Circleback Pro is $15 per user per month on the Yearly view, and that page did not include a separate monthly price in the HTML. Laxis and Fireflies also publish higher monthly prices: Laxis Premium $15.99 and Fireflies Pro $18. Noota’s cheapest paid list price, checked 20 November 2026, is published in euros: Pro at €19 per user per month billed annually, or €29 billed monthly. This page does not convert currencies. These are list prices for a user or a seat, not for a whole company.

Which tool records without a bot?

Bluedot's homepage says it never invites a bot and records with a Chrome extension or a desktop app. Circleback documents a bot-free desktop app and a bot. The Laxis homepage says bot-free, and its help center also documents a bot for Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams. Fireflies usually joins as Fireflies.ai Notetaker. Its no-bot options are a Google Meet Chrome extension and desktop Take Notes. Noota’s pricing table marks Free as bot only, and bot-free (invisible) recording from the desktop app on Pro and above. Tactiq’s official pages say no bot joins, and that a Chrome or Edge extension transcribes from the meeting’s captions. This page has not tested any of them.

Which free plan lets a small team keep recording?

Circleback's pricing FAQ says every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings, with 30 days of history on the Free card. Laxis Basic is $0 with 300 minutes a month, and new accounts get 14 days of Premium. Fireflies publishes a free plan and calls it free forever, but its own pages do not agree on the limits. Bluedot's free card is 5 meetings for the life of the account, then view and share only. Noota’s Free plan is €0 with no card and one month of storage. Its official pages do not describe the Free recording limit the same way: a 300-minute cap appears next to an unlimited bot path that the pricing footnote ties to calendar auto-join and auto-shared summaries. Tactiq Free is $0, with no credit card, 10 transcripts a month, and 5 AI credits a month.

Which one fits a sales team?

Laxis puts sales templates such as MEDDIC and BANT on Premium, and its help center says HubSpot, Salesforce, and Wealthbox require Business. Circleback's Pro card names HubSpot and Salesforce automations. Fireflies documents HubSpot and Salesforce, and its pricing table marks integrations All starting on Pro, while other Fireflies pages describe the free plan differently. Bluedot puts CRM and ATS integrations on Business. Noota’s pricing table marks work tools (CRM and ATS) from Pro, and Zapier and the API from Business. Read the plan gate before you treat a homepage logo as included.

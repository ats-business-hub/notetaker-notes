Free trial, Basic limits, and what happens when minutes run out

The pricing FAQ says every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of Premium, with no credit card required. When the trial ends, it says you move to the free Basic plan and keep all your notes. For a Business team trial, it says to contact sales. There is no self-serve Business trial described on the pages I opened.

The FAQ says meetings, Voice Keyboard dictation, and live translation all draw from the same monthly pool, that minutes reset at the start of each billing month, and that once the pool runs out those features pause until the reset — or you can upgrade for a bigger pool. Everything already recorded stays available. On Basic, meetings older than 30 days “are archived, not deleted.” The FAQ says archived meetings stay in your list, and upgrading unlocks every note again instantly.

The comparison table, and the FAQ, also list a length limit per recording: up to 45 minutes on Basic, 2 hours on Premium, and 4 hours on Business. The FAQ says that when you reach the limit the recording stops and saves automatically, and that you can start a new recording to continue in the same note. All recordings still draw from the monthly minutes.

One subscription covers meetings and the voice keyboard

The pricing page states that every plan includes both the AI note taker for meetings and the AI voice keyboard, and that they draw from the same pool of monthly transcription minutes. The FAQ repeats that one subscription covers both. I am reporting that packaging as the official pages describe it, not as a test of either feature.

Non-profit and education pricing is offered, but the amount is not published

The pricing FAQ says Laxis offers specialized pricing for non-profits and educational institutions, and tells you to contact the partnership team to apply. It does not publish a percentage or a discounted price. I have not applied, so I do not know the amount.

Questions, answered from the official pages

How much does Laxis cost?

As of 21 November 2026, Basic was $0; Premium was $9.99 per month billed yearly ($119.88 a year), or $15.99 a month on the billing help page; Business was $19.99 per month billed yearly ($239.88 a year), or $29.99 a month; and Enterprise was custom. Check both official pages before you buy.

Does Laxis have a free plan or a free trial?

Yes, both. Basic is $0, with 300 minutes a month and 30-day history. Every new account starts with a 14-day Premium trial and no credit card, then moves to Basic, according to the pricing FAQ.

Does one subscription cover both meeting notes and the voice keyboard?

Yes. Official pages say every plan includes both, and that meetings, dictation, and live translation share the same monthly minutes. When the pool runs out, those features pause until the reset or an upgrade.

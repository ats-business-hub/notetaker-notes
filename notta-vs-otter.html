How they record meetings

Notta’s official homepage, checked 22 November 2026, describes bot-free recording: the Notta desktop app records Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex, and the same public copy mentions bot-free calls for Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, and Slack huddles, on Mac and Windows. I have not tested that desktop app, and I am not claiming it is the only capture method Notta offers.

Otter’s official pricing page names Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet on the Basic plan, and describes a notetaker that joins those meetings to write and share notes. I am not restating bot behavior beyond what that page writes down.

Whichever tool you use, tell participants they are being recorded and follow the consent rules that apply to your calls.

Frequently asked questions

Is Notta cheaper than Otter?

On 22 November 2026, Notta’s official pricing page listed Pro at $8.17 per month billed annually ($97.99 a year), with a $10 monthly figure also on the page. Otter’s official page listed prices in USD and showed more than one Pro figure, including $16.99 and a starred $8.49* annual number, while this site’s 20 November 2026 reading also recorded $8.33 on the annual tab. Those plans are not identical. Recheck both live pages.

How do the free plans differ?

Notta Free, as listed on 22 November 2026, is $0 with 120 transcription minutes a month and a 3-minute cap per conversation. Otter Basic is $0 with 300 monthly transcription minutes and a 30-minute per-conversation cap in the comparison table, plus 3 lifetime file imports. Recheck both live pages.

Does Otter transcribe Japanese?

Otter’s official pricing comparison table lists Japanese among AI transcription languages, alongside English, Spanish, French, German, and Chinese. I have not tested accuracy.

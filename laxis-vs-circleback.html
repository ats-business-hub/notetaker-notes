How each one records

Laxis’s homepage says the product transcribes every meeting into polished AI notes, tasks, and reports — “bot-free, in 100+ languages.” I am repeating that official wording; I have not watched a Laxis recorder on a call.

Circleback’s pricing cards say you can use “a bot or the bot-free desktop app for online meetings,” and list mobile and Apple Watch apps for in-person meetings. The homepage FAQ “Can I use Circleback without a bot?” says yes: you can download the Circleback desktop app for macOS and Windows to record meetings without a bot joining your calls, and that by default it records meeting video, with what’s shown on screen making it into your notes. The same FAQ section says Circleback supports in-person meetings and all meeting platforms, including Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams.

Free plans

Laxis’s pricing FAQ says every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of Premium, with no credit card required, and that when the trial ends you move to the free Basic plan and keep all your notes. The Basic card is $0 and lists 300 minutes of transcription a month, 30 days of conversation history, and recording of online and in-person meetings. The comparison table lists a 45-minute cap per recording on Basic. The pricing FAQ and the billing help article both say meetings older than 30 days on Basic are archived, not deleted. The billing help article also says meeting transcription, Voice Keyboard dictation, and live translation share the same monthly minutes.

Circleback’s Free card is $0 per user per month. It lists AI meeting notes and action items, a bot or the bot-free desktop app, and 30-day meeting and recording history. The pricing FAQ says every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings. The two free offers use different caps — a monthly minute pool versus unlimited meetings with a 30-day history window — and I am not declaring a single winner.

Paid prices, as of 21 November 2026

Laxis’s pricing page has Monthly and Yearly toggles. On the Yearly cards I saved, Premium is $9.99 per month and Business is $19.99 per month. The official billing help article lists Premium at $15.99 a month or $9.99 yearly ($119.88 a year), and Business at $29.99 a month or $19.99 yearly ($239.88 a year). The pricing FAQ says website purchases are billed in USD through Stripe, while in-app purchases use Apple or Google regional price tiers.

Circleback’s pricing page has Monthly and Yearly toggles. On the Yearly cards I saved, Pro is $15, Business is $25, and Enterprise is $35, each labeled per user per month. The same page’s billingPlanPrices list, in US cents, has Pro at $18 billed monthly or $180 a year, Business at $29 billed monthly or $300 a year, and Enterprise at $420 a year, with no monthly interval for Enterprise in that list. The pricing FAQ says that on annual billing, 50 users cost $9,000 a year on Pro, $15,000 on Business, and $21,000 on Enterprise, and that Business and Enterprise include unlimited free viewers. I am quoting the visible cards, the source list, and the FAQ rather than collapsing them into one figure.

Who should pick which

These suggestions follow only what the official pages say. I have not tested accuracy, the bot, or the desktop recorder.

Look at Laxis if you want a product whose official homepage calls itself bot-free, a 14-day Premium trial with no credit card, and a $0 Basic plan with a shared monthly minute pool, as the official pages state. Look at Circleback if you want a meeting notetaker whose official pages offer both a bot and a bot-free desktop app, a $0 Free plan with unlimited meetings and a 30-day history cap, and per-seat list prices, including unlimited free viewers on Business and Enterprise.

FAQ

Can I record without a bot?

Laxis’s homepage presents the product as bot-free. Circleback’s official pages offer a bot or a bot-free desktop app, including on the Free plan. This page has not tested either product.

Which free plan records more meetings?

Circleback Free has unlimited meetings and 30-day history. Laxis Basic has 300 minutes a month, a 45-minute cap per recording, and 30 days of history (older meetings are archived, not deleted). I am not declaring a single winner from those different conditions.

Which costs less, Laxis or Circleback?

As of 21 November 2026, Laxis Premium was $9.99 per month on the Yearly cards, or $15.99 monthly in the billing help article. Circleback Pro was $15 per user per month on the Yearly cards, or $18 billed monthly in the page-source list. The billing rules differ (shared minutes versus per-user seats).

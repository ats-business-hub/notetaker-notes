Otter vs Fireflies vs tl;dv for small teams (2026)

Why these three matter for a 2–10 person team

For a small team, choosing an AI meeting note taker comes down to how plans handle several people: how many members fit, who needs a paid seat, and what happens to shared storage or credits as you grow. Otter, Fireflies, and tl;dv answer those questions differently.

I read each vendor’s official pricing and help pages on 24 November 2026 (Japan time), including a same-day recheck of platforms, integrations, and tl;dv’s pricing-page currency. I have not used these tools on client calls, so this compares plan terms, not hands-on results. Prices and promotions change, so re-check the official page before you buy. Pairwise pages already cover Otter vs Fireflies, tl;dv vs Fireflies, and tl;dv vs Otter; this page adds a three-way, seat-count view.

Who each tool fits (based on official plan positioning)

  • Otter labels Pro “For individuals and small teams” and Business “For medium-sized teams” (otter.ai/pricing). Basic and Pro allow up to 5 members, Business up to 25. A team of five fits on Pro; a team of six to ten would need Business.
  • Fireflies sells every paid tier per seat. Pro is positioned “For professional individuals and small teams” and Business “For fast growing businesses” (fireflies.ai/pricing). Each paid person costs one seat.
  • tl;dv lets you mix Free and Pro members in one workspace. Its help center says you can have “as many Free and Pro members… as you want” and that you only pay for members who need the extra features (tl;dv help: which team members do I pay for). This suits teams where only some people need paid features.

Otter

Otter’s pricing page (otter.ai/pricing) lists four plans: Basic (Free), Pro, Business, and Enterprise. For a deeper look at the numbers, see the Otter pricing guide.

  • Basic (Free): 300 monthly transcription minutes, 3 lifetime file imports, 30 minutes per conversation, and up to 5 members.
  • Pro: $16.99 per user per month billed monthly, or $8.33 on annual billing (“save 51%”). It includes 1,200 in-app recording minutes, 10 monthly file imports, and meetings up to 90 minutes. The cap stays at up to 5 members: five people fit, a sixth does not.
  • Business: $19.99 per user per month on annual billing. It includes unlimited meetings and in-app recordings, up to 4 hours per meeting, 3 concurrent meetings, and up to 25 members.

Offers that are not list prices. Monthly Business shows $30 as the regular price next to a “20% off for 3 months” offer at $24. The page doesn’t say what you pay after the three months. The page also showed Pro discounts valid only with an India-issued credit card, and a “first-time subscribers only” footnote that doesn’t say which price it covers. None of these are list prices, so check the official page. This site does not currently use an Otter affiliate link; the pricing links go to the official page.

Fireflies

Fireflies sells Free, Pro, Business, and Enterprise (fireflies.ai/pricing). The help center’s pricing overview lists the same numbers:

  • Pro: $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly.
  • Business: $19 per seat per month billed annually, or $29 billed monthly.
  • Enterprise: $39 per seat per month, billed annually only.

The Fireflies pricing breakdown goes further.

Free plan. Official pages word it differently:

  • Transcription: the pricing page says “Unlimited transcription*”. The help center says 3 transcriptions by default, unlimited once you turn on Auto Join (Free tier features).
  • Storage: the pricing page says 400 mins “/team”, while help articles say “per seat” or “per user”. For a team that difference matters, so check the official page. Storage is a total, not monthly; at the limit you delete older meetings.
  • Recording limit: 2 hours per meeting.

See the Fireflies free plan guide.

Paid tiers. Pro adds 8,000 mins of storage per seat and video recording, with the same 2-hour limit. Business has unlimited storage and a 3-hour limit. Integrations show “All” on paid plans and no entry on Free.

AI credits are billed separately. One help article says credits are shared within the team and don’t increase with teammates (AI credits). The Business article mentions 30 one-time credits per seat. Check the official page on how credits scale.

tl;dv

tl;dv’s pricing page is tldv.io/app/pricing/. Its structured data lists prices in EUR:

  • Pro: €29.00 per seat per month billed monthly, or €18.00 per seat per month billed annually (€216.00 per year).
  • Business: €39.00 monthly, or €29.00 per month billed annually (€348.00 per year).
  • Enterprise: custom pricing.

The page doesn’t show USD prices. The official blog says “the euro and dollar prices are the same number rather than a conversion” (tl;dv blog), and a help article says “All from just $18 a month.” This guide does not convert EUR to USD or quote a USD list price, so check the official page for your currency. The tl;dv pricing page has more context.

Free plan (per the free plan limits article):

  • Unlimited recordings and transcripts, stored for 3 months.
  • Full AI notes for your first 10 meetings, then only the first 10 minutes of each meeting.
  • 10 AI prompts, 10 AI Reports, 20 Zapier integration credits, and 4 clips. These are lifetime limits and don’t reset monthly.
  • No overlapping recordings. Upload counts conflict (one article says 5 files, a newer one says 3), so check the official page.

The tl;dv free plan guide covers this in detail.

Team angle. Free and Pro members can share a workspace, so you pay only for people who need paid features. Pro keeps recordings “for as long as you stay with us,” Pro and Business have no upload limit, and every plan caps meetings at 3 hours.

Decision guide for small teams

This guide doesn’t pick a tested winner. It maps official plan terms to common team situations.

  • Your team is exactly 2–5 people and wants Otter’s paid features: Otter Pro’s 5-member cap fits. If you expect to grow past five, the official cap means you’d need Business (up to 25 members).
  • Your team is 6–10 people and is looking at Otter: go straight to Otter Business. By the official limits, Basic and Pro stop at 5 members.
  • Only some of your team needs paid features: tl;dv’s official policy of mixing Free and Pro members means you pay only for those seats. Just remember the lifetime limits on Free members’ AI features.
  • Everyone needs a paid seat and you want US-dollar per-seat pricing: compare Fireflies Pro ($10 annual / $18 monthly) with Otter Pro ($8.33 annual / $16.99 monthly) for teams of up to five, using the arithmetic in the table.
  • Your meetings run long: per-meeting caps are Otter 30 min / 90 min / 4 hours (Basic / Pro / Business), Fireflies 2 / 2 / 3 hours (Free / Pro / Business), and tl;dv 3 hours on every plan.
  • You rely on shared storage or AI credits: Fireflies’ official pages word these differently, so check the official page first.

FAQ

Do all three have a free plan?

Yes. Otter has Basic (Free), Fireflies has Free ($0, “Free forever”), and tl;dv has a Free plan at €0. Each comes with different limits; see the sections above or the free plans overview.

Can a 7-person team use Otter Pro?

Not according to Otter’s official comparison table. Basic and Pro list “Up to 5 members.” Business lists “Up to 25 members” (otter.ai/pricing).

Does everyone on tl;dv need a paid seat?

No. tl;dv’s help center says you can have Free and Pro members at the same time and only pay for members who need additional features (source).

Is Fireflies’ free storage shared by the team or given per person?

Official pages disagree. The pricing page says “/team,” and help articles say “per seat” or “per user.” Check the official page.

Is Otter Business $24 a month?

No, $24 is a “20% off for 3 months” promotion. The page shows $30 as the regular monthly price and $19.99 on annual billing. Check the official page.

Are tl;dv’s prices in euros or dollars?

The pricing page’s structured data is in EUR. The official blog says euro and dollar prices are the same number. This guide does not convert EUR to USD. Check the official page for your billing currency.

Conclusion

For a 2–10 person team, the most practical next step is to count who actually needs paid features:

  • Everyone, and the team is five or fewer: Otter Pro and Fireflies Pro are both straightforward per-seat options.
  • Everyone, and the team is six or more: Otter means Business, while Fireflies and tl;dv bill per seat.
  • Only a few people: tl;dv’s mixed Free/Pro model is worth a look.

Then run the 5-seat arithmetic above with your real headcount. Before you buy, re-check each official pricing page (Otter, Fireflies, tl;dv). Prices, promotions, and limits can change after 24 November 2026. This overview is researched from official pages, not a hands-on test.

The free plan question, in detail

  • Trial, not a free tier: the $0 card is labeled “Free Trial … Free for 7 days.” I did not find an ongoing free Meeting AI plan on the pricing page.
  • Noise cancellation only: the FAQ says there is no separate noise-cancellation-only plan, because noise cancellation is built into every Krisp plan, and you can disable the Meeting Assistant if you only want clean audio.
  • Calendar at signup: the FAQ says signup connects a Google or Outlook calendar by default, with a zero-calendar-permissions option, and to contact sales to skip the calendar step during a trial.
  • If you need a standing $0 plan: Circleback, Fireflies, tl;dv, Tactiq, Otter, and Fathom publish one. See Free plans and trials and Krisp vs Circleback.

Core or Advanced?

Core covers the note taker, noise cancellation, and general integrations (the comparison table lists Slack, Notion, Zapier, Teams, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Affinity). Advanced is the tier the table marks for Salesforce and ConnectWise, admin controls such as link-sharing access and note-taking mode enforcement, and 4 hours a day of speaker-side accent conversion. If you do not need those, Core is the cheaper list price.

Other pricing-page details: volume discounts for buying more than 50 members (contact Krisp); payment by major credit and debit cards, with invoices for Enterprise; and you can run different plans for different teams in one workspace.

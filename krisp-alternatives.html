Last checked: 25 November 2026 (Japan time). Prices and plan limits below come from each vendor’s official pricing page or help article read that day. This compares published plans, not transcription accuracy; I have not run every tool on the same meetings.

Quick pick

People usually look for a Krisp alternative for one of two reasons: Krisp has no ongoing free plan (only a 7-day trial, then Core at $8 per user per month billed annually or $16 monthly, as of 25 November 2026), or they need something Krisp’s plans do not cover. If you want a standing $0 plan with unlimited meetings, start with Circleback Free. If you want bot-free capture, Circleback’s desktop app, Tactiq’s Chrome extension, and Fathom’s Mac beta are the published options.

When to stay with Krisp

Krisp is the only tool here whose plans all include noise cancellation for your own microphone, and its pricing FAQ says there is no noise-cancellation-only plan. If loud surroundings are the real problem, an alternative note taker will not fix that. Krisp also lists accent conversion and a bot-free note taker on Core. FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Krisp?

Krisp has no ongoing free plan, only a 7-day trial (as of 25 November 2026). Circleback Free offers unlimited meetings with 30-day history; Fathom, Fireflies, tl;dv, Tactiq, and Otter also publish $0 plans with different limits.

Is there a bot-free alternative to Krisp?

Yes. Circleback offers a bot-free desktop app, Tactiq works as a Chrome extension without a bot, and Fathom lists bot-free capture in beta for Mac, according to their official pages on 25 November 2026.

Which Krisp alternative also cancels background noise?

None of the note takers compared here list Krisp-style noise cancellation on their pricing pages. If noise is your main problem, Krisp remains the fit.

Circleback vs Fathom for Small Teams (2026)

Last checked: 25 November 2026 (Japan time). I read the official pricing pages of Circleback and Fathom, Circleback’s homepage FAQ, Fathom’s features page and Fathom’s Free vs. Premium help article, that day. I have not run both tools on the same meetings, so this compares published plans and features, not note quality or accuracy. Prices change; check both pages before you buy.

Quick pick

  • Circleback if you want automations that push notes into Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio or webhooks, bot-free recording on Windows as well as Mac, or Japanese. Free is $0 with unlimited meetings and 30-day history; Pro is $15 per user per month on the yearly cards.

What each product is

Circleback is an AI note taker built around what happens after the meeting. Its pricing page lists AI notes and action items, Ask Circleback, email drafts, speaker-labelled transcripts, automatic tagging and sharing, and automations. On Pro and above, automations work “with all integrations, including Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, webhooks, and many more apps.” It records with a bot, with the bot-free desktop app, or with mobile and Apple Watch apps for in-person meetings.

Fathom is an AI note taker with a strong free plan and a set of team and sales features. Its pages list recordings, transcripts, AI summaries and action items, Ask Fathom, clips and playlists, and, on team plans, Trackers, shared folders, Deal View, CRM field sync and coaching scorecards. It records with the Fathom Notetaker bot, bot-free capture on Mac (beta), or the iOS app.

What it costs on list prices

One person, free. Both are $0. The main difference is history: Circleback Free keeps 30 days of meetings and recordings, while Fathom Free lists unlimited recordings and storage. Fathom Free limits advanced summaries to 5 calls a month; Circleback Free limits automations.

One person, paid. Circleback Pro is $15 per user per month on the yearly cards. Fathom Premium is $16 a month billed annually or $20 monthly.

Five seats. Circleback Pro: 5 × $15 = $75 a month. Fathom Team annual: 5 × $15 = $75 a month ($95 on monthly billing). At Business level both are $25 per user on annual billing, so 5 × $25 = $125 a month. Circleback’s own FAQ example: 50 users on annual billing cost $9,000 a year on Pro, $15,000 on Business and $21,000 on Enterprise. List prices before tax or discount; not quotes.

One cost difference that matters for teams: Circleback Business and Enterprise allow unlimited free viewers, so people who only read shared notes do not need a seat.

Automations and integrations

This is where the two differ most on paper. Circleback lets you set automations that run after meetings that match a condition. Its homepage example is “After every meeting where tags include Customers,” it generates customer details, posts them to a Slack channel and adds rows to a table. On Free, automations are limited. On Pro and above they work with all integrations. Circleback’s homepage names, among others, Claude, ChatGPT, Slack, Notion, Linear, HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, Google Calendar, Gmail, Outlook, Asana, monday.com, Zoho CRM, Intercom, Zapier, Make, MCP, API, CLI and webhooks.

Fathom names HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Notion, Asana, Zapier, Make, ChatGPT and Claude through its MCP server, plus a public API. CRM sync is capped at 3 users per email domain on Free, Premium and Team. Business removes that cap and adds CRM field sync, which writes meeting insights into specific CRM fields. Zapier and Make are marked “limited use” on the lower plans.

If you want notes to land in Notion or a CRM without building a Zapier flow, Circleback Pro covers that at $15. If you want automatic CRM field updates for a sales team, Fathom puts that on Business at $25.

Bot or no bot

Both can send a bot. Both also offer bot-free recording, but not on the same computers. Circleback’s FAQ says its desktop app for macOS and Windows records without a bot, and by default records video so on-screen slides make it into your notes. Fathom’s bot-free capture runs in its Mac desktop app (macOS 14.2 or later), is labelled beta, works with Zoom, Google Meet and Slack Huddles, and needs a Fathom user in the meeting. Fathom says Windows support is coming.

For in-person meetings, Circleback lists mobile and Apple Watch apps. Fathom lists an iOS app. Circleback can also import past meetings from Fathom, Fireflies, Granola and Read AI, according to its FAQ.

Whichever you use, tell people when you record.

Team and sales features

Fathom has more named sales features. Trackers flag topics such as competitor mentions or objections across future calls and send them to Slack, email or the app. Deal View collects every call on a deal. Business adds coaching metrics and AI scorecards. Circleback’s answer is custom AI insights (Pro and above) and its automations, plus admin roles and a usage dashboard on Business. I have not compared how well either works in practice.

Languages

Circleback says it supports over 100 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch and Japanese. Fathom’s features page lists seven languages for transcripts and summaries (English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese), with translation into 29 more through Ask Fathom. A Fathom help article (edited 4 June 2026) says “unlimited transcriptions in 38 languages,” so Fathom’s pages do not agree with each other. For meetings in Japanese, Circleback is the only one of the two that names it.

Privacy and security

Circleback’s FAQ says it does not use customer data to train models. Its pricing page lists HIPAA compliance, SSO and SCIM on Enterprise, and custom data retention on Business. Fathom lists HIPAA compliance with a standard BAA on Team and Business, and SSO, SCIM, custom data retention and custom BAA eligibility on Enterprise. For anything more, read Circleback’s security page and Fathom’s trust center. I have not audited either.

FAQ

Is Circleback or Fathom cheaper?

Both have a $0 Free plan. On paid list prices as of 25 November 2026, Circleback Pro is $15 per user per month on the yearly cards and Business is $25. Fathom Premium is $16 per month billed annually ($20 monthly) for individuals, Team is $15 per user per month billed annually ($19 monthly) and Business is $25 ($34 monthly), both with a 2-user minimum.

What do the free plans include?

Circleback Free lists unlimited meetings, AI notes and action items, Ask Circleback, a bot or the bot-free desktop app, limited automations and 30-day meeting and recording history. Fathom Free lists unlimited recordings, transcriptions and storage, instant AI summaries, and advanced summaries for the first 5 calls each month.

Can Circleback and Fathom record without a bot?

Circleback’s desktop app for macOS and Windows records without a bot joining. Fathom’s bot-free capture is in beta in its Mac desktop app (macOS 14.2 or later), with Windows coming.

Does Circleback or Fathom support Japanese?

Circleback says it supports over 100 languages, including Japanese. Fathom’s features page names seven languages for transcripts and summaries, and Japanese is not one of them.

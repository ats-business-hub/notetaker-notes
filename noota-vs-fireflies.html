Quick verdict by use case

There is no single winner. These are readings of the official pages, not test results.

  • Bot-free recording on a free plan. Fireflies documents two no-bot paths that are not marked as paid-only: a Chrome extension that records Google Meet audio without the bot, and a desktop Take Notes mode that captures system audio, including Google Meet, without the bot joining as a participant. Noota’s pricing comparison table marks Free as “Bot only,” and “Bot or invisible” as a Pro, Business, and Enterprise feature. The pricing FAQ places quiet recording from the desktop app on Pro and above.
  • A clearly stated free allowance. Neither vendor’s free limits sit on one page. Noota Free is €0, no card, and one month of storage, but the pricing footnote and a credits help article describe the 300-minute cap differently, including whether bot recordings are unlimited. Fireflies Free is $0 and called free forever, but the pricing table, free-plan article, and unlimited-transcriptions article do not agree on minutes, meetings, or storage per team versus per seat.
  • European hosting versus default US storage. Noota’s pricing and security pages say meeting data is hosted in the European Union, and that conversations are not used to train AI models. Fireflies’ storage article says the default is storage and processing in the United States on AWS and GCP. Enterprise private storage can keep data in a dedicated location, including the EU, and Fireflies says processing still happens in the US. Fireflies also says it does not use meeting data to train AI models.
  • Languages, as the official pages state them. Fireflies lists Japanese (ja) among 100+ transcription languages, and Japanese as a display language. Its pricing FAQ still says English is the primarily language supported, and that other languages are in beta. Noota’s marketing page names English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, and Dutch. “What is Noota?” says 80+ languages. A 15 April 2024 languages article lists Japanese. Neither page gives an accuracy number I can repeat as a test.
  • Currency and a current Noota sale. Noota lists prices in euros. Fireflies lists prices in US dollars. As of 20 November 2026, Noota’s pricing page still advertised 40% off the first year of a new annual Pro or Business plan, until Monday, 30 November 2026, 11:59 pm Paris time, with no code. Details are also on the Black Friday & Cyber Monday 2026 deals page. Offers change. Check the live pricing page before you buy.

Who should pick which

Pick Noota if you want meeting data described as hosted in the EU, a published no-training claim, and list prices in euros. It also fits if you want a bot that the pricing FAQ says can join with the name you choose, and you can pay for Pro when you need recording without a bot in the participant list. If you are buying a new annual Pro or Business plan before the stated deadline, the official 40% first-year offer is the other reason to look now. Longer notes are in the Noota review.

Pick Fireflies if you want list prices in US dollars, a Google Meet Chrome extension that records without a bot, or desktop Take Notes on a $0 plan. It also fits if you want the longer language list, knowing the pricing FAQ still calls non-English languages beta, or if you already live in a Fireflies-documented workflow such as Slack or a CRM connection. Official Free limits are split across several pages, which I walk through in Fireflies.ai pricing explained and the Fireflies.ai review.

If the client must not see any bot, Noota’s Free plan is not the official fit. Fireflies’ Chrome extension and desktop Take Notes, and Noota’s desktop path on Pro and above, are the documented no-bot options. A help article still tells Noota users to inform participants when the desktop app or Chrome extension records, because those modes do not send an automatic Noota notification. I have not tested whether those recordings are obvious to the other person.

FAQ

Is Noota or Fireflies free?

Both publish a $0 or €0 plan. Noota Free is €0 per user per month, with no card needed and one month of storage. Official Noota pages disagree on the Free recording limit: the pricing footnote says unlimited recording requires a connected calendar with Join all meetings enabled, and that the summary is automatically shared with all participants; otherwise live and standard recordings are limited to 300 minutes per month. A help article says bot recordings are unlimited on Free, and that the 300-minute cap applies to live, desktop app, and Chrome extension recordings. Fireflies Free is $0 and called free forever. Its pages also disagree: the pricing table marks transcripts Limited and storage 400 minutes per team, while free-plan help says 3 meetings are included unless Auto-join is on, and storage is 400 minutes per seat.

Do Noota and Fireflies put a bot in the meeting?

Both can. Noota’s pricing comparison table marks the Free plan as Bot only, and Pro, Business, and Enterprise as Bot or invisible. The pricing FAQ says Noota can join as a bot with the name you choose, or record from your computer with the desktop app on Pro and above. Fireflies usually joins as a visible Fireflies.ai Notetaker and says that presence cannot be hidden, unless you buy a custom bot-name add-on. Fireflies also documents a Google Meet Chrome extension that records audio without the bot, and a desktop Take Notes mode that captures system audio, including Google Meet, without the bot joining as a participant.

Does Noota or Fireflies support Japanese?

Fireflies’ supported-languages article lists Japanese, code ja, among 100+ transcription languages, and lists Japanese as an interface language. The Fireflies pricing FAQ still says English is the primarily language supported, and that Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, and 100+ other languages are in beta. Noota’s AI note-taker page names English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, and Dutch. A separate knowledge-base article, What is Noota?, says 80+ supported languages. A Supported Languages article dated 15 April 2024 lists Japanese. This page has not tested accuracy, and it has not confirmed that the 2024 Noota list is still complete.

How much do Noota and Fireflies cost?

As of 20 November 2026, Noota’s pricing page listed all prices in euros. Pro was €19 per user per month billed annually, or €29 billed monthly. Business was €39 billed annually, or €49 billed monthly. Enterprise is custom, for 50 or more seats. The same page advertised 40% off the first year of a new annual Pro or Business plan until Monday, 30 November 2026, 11:59 pm Paris time, with no code and no discount on monthly plans. Fireflies listed Pro at $10 per seat monthly when billed annually or $18 billed monthly, Business at $19 or $29 on the same pattern, and Enterprise at $39 per seat monthly with annual billing only. A Fireflies bot-name add-on and AI-credit packs are priced separately. Check both official pricing pages before you buy.

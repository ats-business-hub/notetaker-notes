tl;dv Free Plan Limits (2026)

This page uses official tl;dv help and marketing pages checked on 22 November 2026 (Japan time). I have not used the Free plan myself. This is not a hands-on test. Official pages do not describe every Free limit in the same words, so I quote them separately rather than inventing a single combined figure.

tl;dv publishes a standing Free plan. Official structured data on the pricing page lists it at €0.00 per seat per month. Paid list prices are in tl;dv pricing.

What the official help article lists

The help article What are the limits of the Free plan? is dated 24 November 2025, and I re-read it on 22 November 2026. It says the Free plan gives unlimited recordings, transcripts, and manually timestamped notes, with these limits, and it adds: “These limits are for the lifetime of the account, they do not renew monthly.”

  • Recordings are stored for 3 months. You are notified 4 weeks before the scheduled deletion date and can download them in that window. Outside that 4-week window, the article says recordings made on the Free plan cannot be downloaded, even by a user on the Pro plan.
  • After 3 days, recordings are moved to long-term “archived” storage. The transcript and notes remain accessible, but you must unarchive the video to watch it, which “can take up to an hour and a half.”
  • Your first 10 meetings get automatic AI notes for the entire meeting. After that, only the first 10 minutes of each meeting get automatic AI notes.
  • You get 10 free AI prompts, “to generate custom content based on one meeting, or many.”
  • You get 10 free AI Reports, scheduled and sent to your inbox.
  • You get 20 “Integration credits” for Zapier and for HubSpot, Salesforce, or Salesloft workflow triggers.
  • You can create and share 4 clips for free. “Clips cannot be downloaded, even by a user on the Pro plan.”
  • You can manually upload 5 files to be transcribed for free.
  • You can auto-publish meetings to your organization (same email domain), but not to your “Team” (users sharing the same paid plan).
  • You cannot record simultaneous or overlapping meetings.
  • If servers are under heavy load, “customers with the Pro plan will be prioritised,” and a Free meeting “may not be recorded.”
  • On all plans, “there is a max limit of 3 hours for recorded or uploaded meetings.”

The same article says subscribing to Pro or Business unlocks unlimited AI summaries for single meetings, viewing analytics, integrations with over 5,000+ productivity tools, and instant access to older storage, “All from just $18 a month.”

What a newer official blog says

The official article AI Note Taker Apps: I Tested the 5 Best Free Options in 2026 (dateModified 19 November 2026; the body says plan limits were re-checked on 10 November 2026) describes the Free plan as including:

  • Unlimited video recordings on Google Meet, Zoom, and MS Teams
  • Unlimited meetings and viewers
  • Unlimited transcription in 100+ languages with automatic speaker recognition
  • Unlimited AI moment summaries
  • 10 meetings with full AI notes included (a lifetime allowance, not monthly)
  • 10 Ask tl;dv AI queries included (lifetime allowance)
  • 10x multi-meeting AI reports

On limitations, that article also says recordings archive after 3 days and are stored for up to 3 months, with a 4-week notice before deletion; each recording can run up to 3 hours; there are no simultaneous meetings (it says Pro allows 2 and Business 3); and beyond the first 10 AI-notes meetings, Free gives notes for the first 10 minutes of each meeting only. It also says bot-based recording requires host approval, and points to the desktop app for bot-free capture on Google Meet.

Where official pages do not fully agree

  • Language count: The official homepage (checked 22 November 2026) says tl;dv “transcribes and writes notes in 100+ languages.” The official languages page (dateModified 15 November 2024) says transcriptions, notes and reports are supported in “over 30 languages,” and its FAQ says you can “transcribe in over 30 different languages.” The same languages FAQ says the product interface is offered in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, and German. I have not counted a complete official language list as 30 or 100. The desktop app page prints a named “30+” list that includes Japanese, among other languages; that list also repeats Swedish and spells Egyptian as “Egypcian,” so I am not treating it as a clean inventory.
  • Multi-meeting AI questions: The help article says 10 free AI prompts for one meeting or many, and 10 free AI Reports. The November 2026 blog says 10 Ask AI queries and 10x multi-meeting AI reports. The official honest-review article (visible “Last updated August 2026,” dateModified 10 October 2026) says “Free and Pro both give you five queries across multiple meetings,” and that asking questions of your whole library is a Business feature. I am not choosing one of those figures as the true cap.

Platforms and bot vs no-bot, as official pages describe them

The homepage FAQ says “The notetaker joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, plus Slack huddles and WhatsApp calls. For in-person meetings there’s the mobile app, and the desktop app records without a bot in the call.” The official Google Meet page describes two ways: a Chrome extension whose bot “joins your calls automatically,” and a desktop app “to capture directly from your device, with no bot added to the call.” The desktop app page offers Mac (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows downloads, and says you can “Record Google Meet, MS Teams, Zoom, and all your favorite platforms without a bot.” Help article Recording without a bot (dateModified 5 November 2026) says bot-free recording needs the latest desktop app on a Mac or Windows computer, and that recording notifications may not appear automatically, so it is your responsibility to inform participants.

FAQ

Is tl;dv Free really $0 or €0?

Official structured data on the pricing page, checked 22 November 2026, lists Free at €0.00 per seat per month. Official blog pages also call the plan Free Forever at $0. I did not find a sentence on those pages that says a credit card is required, or not required, to open Free.

How long does tl;dv keep Free recordings?

The official help article dated 24 November 2025, re-read on 22 November 2026, says Free recordings are stored for 3 months, are moved to archived storage after 3 days, and can be downloaded in a 4-week window before deletion. A November 2026 official blog describes the same 3-day archive and 3-month storage.

Do Free AI notes cover every meeting?

Both the help article and the November 2026 blog say your first 10 meetings get full automatic AI notes, and after that only the first 10 minutes of each meeting get automatic AI notes. The help article says those limits are for the lifetime of the account and do not renew monthly.

Best AI Note Takers for Sales Teams (2026)

I read official pricing, sales, and help pages on 20 November 2026 to see which AI note takers document CRM updates, deal or call intelligence, or outreach for sales teams, and which plan those features sit on. I have not used these tools on sales calls, so this is not a ranking of accuracy, win rates, or how well a CRM write-back works in practice. “Best” here is a search phrase, not a test score. Start with the constraint you cannot bend: the plan that includes CRM, a visible bot, or a currency you can pay in. How I compared the official pages

Sales teams usually care about three things the marketing pages also emphasize: whether notes can be written back to a CRM, whether a bot joins the customer call, and what that CRM-capable plan costs. I used each vendor’s own wording for those points. I did not convert Noota’s euro prices into US dollars, and I did not repeat vendor time-saved claims as if I had measured them.

Fireflies

Fireflies publishes a dedicated sales page. It says Fireflies for Sales “preps you before meetings, updates your CRM, drafts follow-ups and flags deal risk.” The same page lists 18+ sales templates, including BANT, MEDDIC, SPICED, Discovery, Demo, and Renewal; Skills for qualification, customer insights, coaching, and competitor mentions; and Autofill, which it says “automatically updates Salesforce, HubSpot, and 45 more.” I have not used Autofill or those templates.

The sales-page pricing block, labeled “Fireflies for Sales Pricing,” lists prices per seat, billed annually, with monthly billing available on Pro and Business: Free at $0; Pro at $10 per seat per month, “for individual reps,” with transcripts, summaries, AskFred, and AI Skills; Business at $19, with “Deal Intelligence. Autofill CRM. Everything in Pro.”; and Enterprise at $39, plus SCIM, a HIPAA BAA, and custom retention. Those annual figures match the pricing page I opened the same day, which also listed Pro at $18 and Business at $29 on monthly billing. Conversation intelligence is listed on the Business plan in the pricing comparison. Official pages still disagree on Free-plan limits; that conflict is walked through in Fireflies.ai pricing explained.

If you do not want a bot in the customer call, the pricing comparison includes “Record using Chrome extension without Fireflies bot,” and Fireflies also documents a desktop Take Notes mode in help. See the Fireflies review.

Noota

Noota lists CRM and work-tool sync on the Pro plan. As of 20 November 2026, Pro was €19 per user per month billed annually or €29 monthly, and Business was €39 or €49. The same pricing page still advertised 40% off the first year of a new annual Pro or Business plan, until 30 November 2026, 11:59 pm Paris time, with no code, and said there is no discount on monthly plans. The discounted first-year annual figures shown were €11.40 per user per month for Pro and €23.40 for Business.

The Sales Agent page is easy to over-read. It says the agent is “coming early 2027,” that the screens are a preview of a product still in development, and that “pricing will be announced at launch.” The same FAQ says Noota can already record and summarise calls, prepare a brief, draft a follow-up email, score calls, and push them to HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive. I have not used those workflows. Quiet desktop recording, according to the pricing FAQ, is on Pro and above. List prices are in euros. See Noota pricing explained, the Noota review, and Noota vs Fireflies.

Circleback

Circleback is the lowest-priced CRM-capable plan in this group, on the official list prices I saw. The pricing page puts HubSpot, Salesforce, and Attio automations on Pro. The Yearly tab showed Pro at $15 per user per month, Business at $25, and Enterprise at $35. The FAQ says that on annual billing, 50 users cost $9,000 a year on Pro, $15,000 on Business, and $21,000 on Enterprise, and that every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings, with 30-day history on Free. The same page’s plan-price data lists Pro at $18 per user per month on monthly billing ($180 a year on annual billing) and Business at $29 monthly ($300 a year). Enterprise is listed as annual only. Business and Enterprise include unlimited free viewers, according to the FAQ.

The pricing page also lists “a bot or the bot-free desktop app for online meetings,” including on Free. See the Circleback review and Circleback vs Fireflies.

Laxis

Laxis publishes a sales-teams page that describes four stages: outreach, pre-call research, bot-free call capture, and CRM updates afterward. The FAQ on that page says Laxis “automatically pushes call summaries, action items, and next steps into CRMs including HubSpot and Salesforce,” and describes a bot-free Mac app. It also describes a Laxis AI SDR for multi-channel outreach. I have not used the SDR, the dossiers, or the CRM sync, and I am not repeating the page’s time-saved figures as measured results.

On the pricing page, CRM Automation and “7000+ Apps Integrations” sit on Business, not on Premium. As of 20 November 2026, the page and its schema listed Premium at $9.99 per month billed yearly and Business at $19.99 per month billed yearly. Basic is $0, with 300 minutes per month and 30 days of conversation history. New accounts, the FAQ says, start with a 14-day Premium trial and no credit card. Bluedot

Bluedot is the bot-free-only option in this group. The pricing page lists CRM and ATS integrations on Business, not on Basic or Pro. As of 20 November 2026, Business was $32 per user per month billed annually, or $39 monthly. Pro, which does not list CRM, was $20 annually or $25 monthly. Free is $0, with 5 meetings for a lifetime. The FAQ says Bluedot charges only for users who record meetings, not for every workspace member. See the Bluedot review and Bluedot vs Fireflies.

Questions, answered from the official pages

Which AI note taker has CRM sync for sales teams?

All five approved-affiliate tools above document some form of CRM update, but not on the same plan. Circleback and Noota list CRM automations or sync on Pro. Fireflies, Laxis, and Bluedot list the named CRM features on Business. Check the live pricing page for the plan you would actually buy.

Can sales teams record calls without a meeting bot?

Bluedot describes capture with no bot. Circleback lists a bot or a bot-free desktop app, including on Free. Laxis’s sales-teams page describes a bot-free Mac app. Noota places quiet desktop recording on Pro and above. Fireflies documents a no-bot Chrome extension and a desktop Take Notes mode. I have not tested how visible any of those modes are to the customer.

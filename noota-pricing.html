Black Friday 2026 offer, as stated on the pricing page

On 20 November 2026 the pricing page still advertised a “Black Friday 2026” offer, “until Monday, November 30, 11:59 pm (Paris time).” The headline is “40% off annual plans.” The page says that discount applies to Pro and Business, for the first year, on any new annual subscription: new customers, returning customers, and monthly subscribers who switch to annual. “No code needed: the discount is applied at checkout.” It also says: “No discount on monthly plans.”

The discounted first-year annual figures shown were €11.40 per user per month for Pro (€136.80 for the first year instead of €228) and €23.40 per user per month for Business (€280.80 instead of €468). Offers change. Recheck the live pricing page before you buy. A same-day roundup of official November 2026 offers is on the Black Friday & Cyber Monday 2026 deals page.

Free-plan limits, as the page itself states them

The FAQ “Is the Free plan really free?” answers yes, with no card. It says the plan records your meetings with one month of storage, and includes transcription and summaries, the email agent on your monthly AI credits, and the web, mobile, and desktop apps.

A footnote on the same page adds a condition to the “unlimited” wording: “Unlimited recording on the Free plan requires a connected calendar with Join all meetings enabled, and the meeting summary is automatically shared with all participants. Otherwise, live recordings and standard recordings are limited to 300 minutes per month.” I have not tested how that switch behaves. If that condition matters to you, read the footnote on the live page, not only the plan-card headline.

Bot or bot-free recording

The FAQ “Do other meeting participants see a bot during the call?” says: “Only if you want. Noota can join as a bot with the name you choose, or record quietly from your computer with the desktop app on the Pro plan and above. Participants are notified that the meeting is recorded.” Quiet desktop recording is therefore described as a Pro, Business, and Enterprise feature, not a Free-plan feature. The FAQ “Which plan should a small team pick?” makes the same cut: start on Free to try it on every meeting, and move to Pro when you want bot-free recording, a longer history, and your CRM connected.

Seats, CRM, and phone calls

The FAQ “How does it work when the team grows?” says you invite your team to a shared workspace, “seats are unlimited on every plan, and each person pays for the plan they need.” Business, it says, adds team analytics, company templates and branding, shared rules, and the API.

CRM and work-tool sync is listed on the Pro card. Phone-call recording is listed on the Business card. I did not treat those rows as a hands-on test of every named integration.

The Sales Agent is not in today’s list prices

The pricing page also describes “power-ups that scale your value,” including a Sales Agent and a Recruitment Agent, and says to “bolt these onto any paid plan.” The dedicated Sales Agent page, which I also opened on 20 November 2026, is more cautious. It says the Sales Agent is “coming early 2027,” that “the screens on this page are a preview of a product still in development,” and that “pricing will be announced at launch.” The same FAQ says Noota can already record and summarise calls, prepare a brief, draft a follow-up email, score calls, and push them to HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive. I have not used those workflows. Do not budget for the Sales Agent as if it were a live, priced add-on today.

Hosting and trust claims on the pricing page

The pricing page says Noota is GDPR compliant, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certified, with EU data residency, encryption at rest and in transit, and a SecNumCloud-compatible architecture. The FAQ “How does Noota protect our data?” says meeting data is hosted in Europe, that Noota is ISO 27001 certified and GDPR compliant, and that “your data is never used to train AI models.” It also describes two hosting modes: “Noota Europe,” hosted in the European Union, and “Noota Sovereign France,” described as using French sub-processors only. I did not open gated trust-center reports.

Questions, answered from the official pages

How much does Noota cost?

As of 20 November 2026, Free was €0 with no card; Pro was €19 per user per month billed annually or €29 monthly; Business was €39 or €49 on the same pattern; and Enterprise was custom for 50 or more seats. A 40% first-year discount on new annual Pro or Business plans was advertised until 30 November 2026, 11:59 pm Paris time, with no code and no discount on monthly plans. Check the live page before you buy.

Is the Free plan really free?

The pricing FAQ says yes, with no card. Read the footnote about the 300-minute cap when “Join all meetings” is not enabled, or when the summary is not shared with all participants.

The pricing FAQ places quiet recording from the desktop app on Pro and above.

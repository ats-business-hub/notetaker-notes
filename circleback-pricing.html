Viewers, 50-user examples, and seat math

The pricing FAQ asks whether teammates who are not in meetings need a paid seat. It says no, on Business and Enterprise you can add unlimited viewers for free. Viewers can access meetings that have been shared with them but cannot use Circleback for their own meetings. That unlimited-viewer line is on the Business and Enterprise cards, not on Free or Pro.

The same FAQ says that on annual billing, 50 users cost $9,000 a year on Pro, $15,000 on Business, and $21,000 on Enterprise. Those totals match the Yearly card rates times 50 times 12. It also repeats that every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings, and that Business and Enterprise include unlimited free viewers.

Five people who each need their own meetings, at the Yearly Pro price, are 5 times $15, or $75 a month before any discount. I am only multiplying the published per-user figure. Circleback’s own FAQ is the source for the 50-user annual totals.

Enterprise volume tiers in the page source

The Enterprise object in the same billingPlanPrices list includes volume tiers, still as annual unit amounts in US cents: $420 a year up to 50 users, then $360, $300, $240, $210, and $180 a year as the seat count rises. The public FAQ does not spell out those cutovers in prose. The 50-user Enterprise example of $21,000 a year matches 50 times $420, so that example does not show a discount at 50 users. I am quoting the unit amounts as they appear in the page source, not as a discount I calculated from a visible table.

Non-profit and education discounts

The pricing FAQ says Circleback has a 30% non-profit discount: sign up with your non-profit email, then contact support to have it applied. It also says there is a 50% education discount for students: sign up with your school email to have it applied automatically, or contact support with proof of being a student. I have not applied, so I have not seen a discounted invoice.

Questions, answered from the official pages

How much does Circleback cost?

As of 21 November 2026, Free was $0; Pro was $15 per user per month on the Yearly cards, or $18 monthly in the page-source list; Business was $25 or $29; and Enterprise was $35 per user per month on the Yearly cards, or $420 a year in that list, with volume tiers as the seat count rises. Check the live page before you buy.

Does Circleback have a free plan?

Yes. The Free card is $0 and includes notes, action items, a bot or the bot-free desktop app, and 30-day history. The pricing FAQ says every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings.

Do teammates who only read notes need a paid seat?

The pricing FAQ says no, on Business and Enterprise you can add unlimited free viewers. Viewers can open shared meetings but cannot use Circleback for their own meetings.

Minute caps and other limits in the comparison table

The comparison table on the same page is the clearest official list of caps. As of 20 November 2026 it showed the following, in four columns that line up with Basic, Pro, Business, and Enterprise:

  • Meeting and recording transcription monthly limit (no rollover): 300 minutes per user, 1,200 minutes per user, unlimited, and unlimited.
  • Imported file transcription monthly limit (no rollover): 300 minutes per user, 1,200 minutes per user, 6,000 minutes per user, and 6,000 minutes per user.
  • Max transcription time per conversation: 30 minutes, 90 minutes, 4 hours, and 4 hours.
  • Pre-recorded audio or video files for import: 3 lifetime per user, 10 monthly per user, unlimited, and unlimited.
  • Conversation history: 25 most recent, then unlimited on Pro, Business, and Enterprise.
  • Max concurrent meetings: 1, 2, 3, and 3.
  • Workspace size: up to 5 members on Basic and Pro, up to 25 members on Business, and unlimited members on Enterprise.
  • Otter AI Chat queries: 20 per user a month (3 per conversation) on Basic, 50 per user on Pro, and 200 per user on Business and Enterprise.

A footnote under the table says these transcription limits are a “shared pool across all transcription types.” Another line in the table reads: “Unlimited in-app recordings. Limit applies only to imported files.” I am quoting that sentence as the page prints it, next to the imported-file caps above, rather than collapsing the two rows into one number. The AI transcription line names English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and Chinese.

The Basic (free) plan, in one place

Basic is $0. If you only need a trial of Otter itself, these are the caps the pricing page wrote down on 20 November 2026: 300 monthly transcription minutes, 30 minutes per conversation, 3 lifetime file imports, the 25 most recent conversations, one concurrent meeting, up to 5 workspace members, and 20 AI Chat queries per user a month. The plan card also names Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet. Minutes do not roll over, according to the table heading.

Student and teacher discount

A FAQ on the same pricing page says Otter offers a 20% discount on Otter Pro Monthly and Otter Pro Annual plans for individual students and teachers using an email address ending in .edu. The discounted prices listed there are Otter Pro Annual at $6.67 per month, billed $79.99 annually, and Otter Pro Monthly at $13.59 per month. The FAQ says you sign up for Otter Basic (or log in) with a .edu address, then use the “Student & Teacher Discount” link on the upgrade page. That discount is not the list price in the table above.

How Otter records meetings

This is not a pricing fact, but it is the main product limit people ask about when they compare plans. The Notetaker overview, updated 21 August 2026, says Otter Notetaker “automatically joins your Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams meetings,” joins as a guest participant, appears as “[Your Name]’s Notetaker (Otter.ai),” and cannot join anonymously or without other participants seeing it. It also says the Otter Desktop app can record without Notetaker joining as a separate participant, and that you should obtain any required consent. I have not tested either path.

FAQ

How much does Otter.ai cost?

As of 20 November 2026, the Otter pricing page listed Basic at $0, Pro at $16.99 per user per month billed monthly or $8.33 per user per month on the annual tab (labeled save 51%), Business at $30 monthly with a first-time $24 figure for “20% off for 3 months,” or $19.99 per user per month billed annually (save 33%), and Enterprise at custom pricing. The page also shows starred Pro prices that a footnote limits to an India-issued credit card. Check the current pricing page before you buy.

What are the Otter.ai free plan limits?

The Basic plan is free. The plan card lists 300 monthly transcription minutes and 3 lifetime audio or video file imports. The comparison table lists 30 minutes per conversation, the 25 most recent conversations in history, up to 5 workspace members, 1 concurrent meeting, and 20 Otter AI Chat queries per user a month (3 per conversation). A footnote says transcription limits are a shared pool across all transcription types, with no rollover.

What are Otter’s minute caps on paid plans?

The comparison table listed a meeting and recording transcription monthly limit, with no rollover, of 1,200 minutes per user on Pro and unlimited on Business and Enterprise. An imported-file transcription monthly limit is listed as 1,200 minutes per user on Pro and 6,000 minutes per user on Business and Enterprise. Max transcription time per conversation is 90 minutes on Pro and 4 hours on Business and Enterprise. A footnote says these limits are a shared pool across all transcription types.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Otter?

It depends on the plan and the billing period. As of 20 November 2026, Otter Pro’s annual tab showed $8.33 per user per month, which is lower than Laxis Premium at $9.99 a month billed yearly, Fireflies Pro at $10 per seat per month billed annually, Bluedot Basic at $14, and Circleback Pro at $15 on the yearly card. Otter Pro billed monthly, at $16.99, is higher than several of those yearly entry prices. Noota lists prices in euros. These tools are not all cheaper than Otter, and they have different free allowances and recording methods.

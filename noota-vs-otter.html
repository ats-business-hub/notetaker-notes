Quick verdict by use case

There is no single winner. These are readings of the official pages, not test results.

  • Prices in US dollars, with a clearly stated monthly minute cap on the free plan. Otter is the clearer page on both points. Basic is $0, and the comparison table lists 300 monthly transcription minutes and 30 minutes per conversation. Noota lists every price in euros, and its “unlimited” Free headline is qualified by a footnote.
  • European hosting and a published no-training claim on the pricing page itself. Noota’s pricing page says meeting data is hosted in Europe, that Noota is ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant, and that your data is never used to train AI models. I did not find an equivalent hosting statement on Otter’s pricing page when I opened it on 20 November 2026.
  • Bot-free desktop recording on a paid plan. Both document this path. Noota’s pricing FAQ places quiet recording from the desktop app on Pro and above. Otter’s Notetaker overview says the Notetaker cannot join a meeting anonymously or without appearing to other participants, and points to the Otter Desktop app for botless recording. I have not tested whether either “quiet” or “botless” mode is obvious to the other person. Both vendors still tell you to get consent.
  • A current first-year discount on a new annual plan. As of 20 November 2026, Noota’s pricing page still advertised 40% off the first year of a new annual Pro or Business plan, until 30 November 2026, 11:59 pm Paris time, with no code. Otter’s pricing page showed other promotions, including starred Pro prices limited to an India-issued credit card, and a Business monthly “20% off for 3 months” figure. Offers change.
  • CRM notes on a mid-tier plan, versus a named sales notetaker. Noota’s Pro card lists syncs with your CRM and work tools. Otter’s Pro card lists Salesforce*, HubSpot*, and Zapier integrations, with a footnote that Salesforce and HubSpot integrations are subject to user limits. Otter Sales Notetaker and custom CRM or dialer integrations are listed on Enterprise.

Bots, and recording without one

Otter’s Notetaker overview, updated 21 August 2026, says Notetaker automatically joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams to record and transcribe in real time once a calendar is connected. It answers “Can Notetaker join a meeting anonymously or without other participants seeing it?” with no. The same article’s tip for botless recording is to use the Otter Desktop app. The desktop-app help, updated 6 November 2026, repeats that you can record without an AI bot joining as a participant, and says you are responsible for notifying participants and obtaining any required consent.

Noota’s pricing FAQ describes a similar fork: a bot with the name you choose, or quiet recording from the desktop app on Pro and above, with participants notified that the meeting is recorded. I have not compared how those notices appear on a live call.

Who might pick which, on the official pages alone

Pick Noota if you want list prices and hosting described in European terms, a published no-training claim on the pricing page, and you can pay in euros. It also fits if you want bot-free desktop capture and CRM sync on the mid-tier Pro plan, and if you are buying a new annual Pro or Business plan before the stated 30 November 2026 deadline. Longer notes are in the Noota review and in Noota pricing explained.

Pick Otter if you want prices in US dollars, a free plan whose 300-minute monthly cap is written on the comparison table, and a desktop botless path that official help does not mark as paid-only. Read the starred Pro prices carefully if you do not have an India-issued credit card. Otter Sales Notetaker is listed on Enterprise, not on Pro or Business. Teams already on Otter who are weighing a switch can also use Otter.ai alternatives.

Questions, answered from the official pages

Is Noota or Otter free?

Both publish a zero-price plan. Noota Free is €0 with no card, but the unlimited-recording headline has a footnote. Otter Basic is free, with 300 monthly transcription minutes and a 30-minute per-conversation cap on the comparison table.

How much do Noota and Otter cost?

As of 20 November 2026, Noota Pro was €19 per user per month billed annually or €29 monthly, and Business was €39 or €49, with a 40% first-year annual offer until 30 November 2026, 11:59 pm Paris time. Otter Pro was $16.99 monthly or $8.33 annual, and Business was $30 monthly or $19.99 annual, plus the starred and short-term figures quoted above. Check both live pages before you buy.

Can Noota or Otter record without a bot?

Both document a desktop path. Noota places it on Pro and above. Otter’s help points to the desktop app for botless recording, and lists macOS 13.0 or higher and Windows 10 or higher.

Krisp vs Fireflies: which AI meeting note taker fits you?

Krisp and Fireflies both turn meetings into transcripts and AI notes, but they start from different places. Krisp is a noise-cancellation app with a note taker built in, and its AI note-taker page says no bot joins your meeting. Fireflies is built around a searchable, shared meeting archive. It usually joins as a bot, and its desktop app can also record without one. This comparison uses the official pricing and help pages, checked on 26 November 2026. It is not a hands-on test, and prices can change, so confirm on the vendor page before you buy.

See Krisp plans See Fireflies plans

Quick verdict

  • Krisp fits people who want call audio cleaned up on their own microphone and speaker, and notes without a visible bot. Krisp says noise cancellation is built into every plan. There is a 7-day free trial, but no ongoing free plan on the pricing page.

  • Fireflies fits teams that want one shared archive of meetings, with a free plan to start. The free plan’s storage is small (see below), and paid plans are priced per seat.

Bot-free recording vs a meeting bot

Krisp runs on your own computer and works with your microphone and speaker, so other participants do not see a note-taker bot in the participant list. Krisp’s pricing FAQ says the AI Meeting Assistant asks for a Google or Outlook calendar connection by default at sign-up.

Fireflies’ usual setup is a bot that joins calendar meetings. Its desktop app help says the app can record meetings with or without a bot. If bot-free capture is your main need, compare more options in bot-free AI meeting note takers.

With either approach, tell people before you record. See how to tell participants you’re using an AI note taker.

Free plan and trial details

Krisp: the pricing page lists a Free Trial for 7 days with no credit card. There is no ongoing free plan for the meeting assistant on that page.

Fireflies: the Free plan is $0. The pricing page lists 400 mins of storage per team. Fireflies’ help article on transcription credits, storage, and rate limits labels the same allowance as storage per user. We could not resolve which applies on 26 November 2026, so check your account before relying on it. The details are in Fireflies Free plan limits. The same help article lists an upload limit of 3,000 minutes per source per month (multiplied by teams and users, capped at 150,000), with overage at $0.01 per minute.

Which costs less?

On annual billing, Krisp Core ($8 per user) lists lower than Fireflies Pro ($10 per seat). On monthly billing, Krisp Core is $16 and Fireflies Pro is $18. Storage is counted differently, though: Krisp lists gigabytes, Fireflies lists minutes. Fireflies has a free plan, while Krisp has only a trial. For full plan tables, see Krisp pricing and Fireflies pricing.

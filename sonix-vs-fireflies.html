Sonix vs Fireflies for Small Teams (2026)

I read Sonix’s pricing page, features page, and AI meeting recorder page, and Fireflies’s pricing page, pricing-plans help article, Free-plan help article, and Chrome extension help article, on 21 November 2026 (Japan time). I have not used either product on a client call. Longer write-ups are in the Sonix review, Sonix pricing, Fireflies review, and Fireflies pricing.

Quick pick

  • Fireflies: Official pages describe a meeting Notetaker bot, plus a Chrome extension, desktop app, and mobile apps. The public pricing cards list a $0 Free plan and Pro at $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly. Free-plan limits are worded differently on the pricing cards and in the Free-plan help article, so I quote both in the Free plans section below.

  • Sonix: Official pages describe an upload-based transcription and translation platform, plus Sonix Agent, which the meeting-recorder page says joins Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams on Pro and Enterprise plans. How each one records

Sonix’s AI meeting recorder page says: “Sonix Agent joins your Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams call and writes the transcript while people talk.” The same page says speakers are named from the call, 52 languages work live, and the video is saved with the transcript when you leave. It also says the Agent is “Included with Pro and Enterprise plans. The free trial covers uploaded recordings.” For Webex and other platforms, the page describes saving a recording in that app and uploading the file to Sonix. I did not treat the Agent as being included on Core, Advanced, or Pay As You Go, because that page named only Pro and Enterprise.

Fireflies’s pricing page lists Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, “+more,” a Chrome extension, a desktop app, and mobile apps, and a FAQ item on customizing the Fireflies Notetaker bot name. The comparison table includes a row labeled “Record using Chrome extension without Fireflies bot.” The Chrome extension help article says you can record audio with the extension, use Live Assist during calls, or invite the Notetaker when you want full video recording, and that you can turn a setting off if you prefer to capture meetings manually or invite the Notetaker (bot). I have not tested either capture method.

Free trial and Free plan

Sonix’s pricing page advertises a “30 min free trial,” “No credit card required,” and “Cancel anytime.” The meeting-recorder page says that free trial covers uploaded recordings, not the live Agent.

Fireflies lists a $0 Free plan that is “Free forever” on the public cards I saved. Those cards listed “Unlimited transcription*” (the asterisk is on the card), limited AI summaries, “400 mins of storage/team,” and 20 AI credits, plus transcription in 100+ languages. A separate help article, “What’s included in the Fireflies Free plan,” last updated one day before this check, says auto-join “allows unlimited transcriptions on the Free plan,” gives “400 minutes of storage per seat,” includes 20 AI credits a month, and describes a pool of sign-up transcription credits that varies by source (for example, 3 credits when you sign up on the Fireflies website with the bot, 5 with the Chrome extension, and 10 for a new mobile-app user). The same article says downloading transcripts requires a paid plan, and that Fireflies Talk (unlimited voice dictation in the desktop app) is included on every plan, including Free. Because the storage wording (“/team” on the pricing card versus “per seat” in the help article) and the transcription limits are not written the same way, I am quoting both and not collapsing them into one figure.

Paid prices, as of 21 November 2026

Fireflies’s pricing page and the pricing-plans help article agree on these list prices: Pro is $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 billed monthly; Business is $19 annually or $29 monthly; Enterprise is $39 per seat per month, billed annually only. The public Pro card I saved also listed unlimited transcription, unlimited AI summaries, 8,000 minutes of storage per seat, and 20 AI credits.

Sonix’s pricing page lists all prices in USD. Pay As You Go is $10 per hour. Core is $25 a month or $275 a year, with 5 hours a month or 60 hours a year of transcription and translation, and a separate 5 hours a month or 60 hours a year of AI workspace usage, 25 GB of storage, and one user included. Advanced is $50 a month or $550 a year (20 hours / 240 hours a year of transcription and translation; 25 hours / 300 hours a year of AI workspace usage). Pro is $80 a month or $880 a year (40 hours / 480 hours a year of transcription and translation; 100 hours / 1,200 hours a year of AI workspace usage). The page says additional hours on any subscription plan are billed at $10 per hour, and extra seats are $25 a month or $275 a year and do not add more hours. The features page names 54+ languages for translation; the meeting-recorder page names 52 live languages for the Agent.

Who should pick which

These suggestions follow only what the official pages say. I have not tested accuracy, the Agent, or the Fireflies bot.

Look at Fireflies if you want a per-seat meeting-notetaker plan, a $0 Free tier (with the limits quoted above), and a documented option to record from a Chrome extension without inviting the Notetaker bot, as the comparison table and the extension help article describe. Look at Sonix if you want hour-based pricing for file uploads, a 30-minute no-card trial that the meeting-recorder page says covers uploaded recordings, and you can accept that Sonix Agent is documented on Pro and Enterprise rather than on every plan. The two products are not equivalent: one is sold mainly as a meeting notetaker, the other mainly as a transcription and translation platform that can also send an Agent to a call on named plans.

FAQ

Does Sonix join the meeting as a bot?

The AI meeting recorder page says Sonix Agent joins Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams and writes the transcript live, and that this is included with Pro and Enterprise plans. The free trial, that page says, covers uploaded recordings. Fireflies describes a renameable Notetaker bot and a Chrome extension that can record without inviting the bot. This page has not tested either product.

What can I try for free?

Sonix advertises a 30-minute free trial with no credit card, covering uploaded recordings according to the meeting-recorder page. Fireflies lists a $0 Free plan; the pricing cards and the Free-plan help article do not use the same wording for transcription and storage limits, so I quote both in the Free plans section rather than picking one figure.

Which costs less, Sonix or Fireflies?

They are billed on different metrics, and I am not declaring a cheaper winner. As of 21 November 2026, Fireflies Pro was $10 per seat per month billed annually, or $18 monthly. Sonix Core was $25 a month or $275 a year for 5 hours of transcription a month, with extra hours at $10 per hour. Check both live pages before you buy.

Stop Otter Notetaker from auto-joining meetings

Otter’s official help says Otter Notetaker can automatically join Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams meetings through synced calendar events. If you want that to stop, the same help says to change the default auto-join setting and then check individual upcoming events. The steps below are taken from those official articles. Menu labels can change; use Otter’s current help if a label does not match.

Turn off automatic joining

Otter documents two places to set the default:

  • On the homepage Meetings tab, open AI Notetaker settings and select Meetings I manually select.
  • In account settings, go to Integrations → Meetings, open Default auto-join settings, and select Meetings I manually select.

On mobile, Otter’s help says to tap Account → Account Settings → Meeting settings → Auto-join meetings, then select Manual.

Otter lists other default options in a separate settings article, including joining every calendar event that has a Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams URL, joining only meetings you host, and (for some workspace accounts) joining only external or only internal meetings. “Meetings I manually select” is the option the stop-auto-join article tells you to use if you do not want automatic joins.

Review events you already toggled

This is easy to miss. Otter’s official help says events you previously toggled on or off keep that individual setting. Changing the default only applies to meetings you have not changed by hand. After you change the default, review the homepage calendar (or the Calendar tab on mobile) and confirm Notetaker is off for upcoming meetings you do not want recorded.

A related Otter article says that to have Notetaker join automatically, you should update the auto-join setting at least 30 minutes before the start time; changes made later than that may prevent it from joining on time. The same article says enabling auto-join once on a recurring event enables it for the rest of the series.

Otter also notes that you can still turn auto-join on for a specific event, or add Notetaker to a meeting manually, after the default is set to manual selection.

Remove Notetaker from a meeting that already started

Otter’s official help, updated 7 May 2026 and rechecked 26 November 2026, says any meeting participant — including people without an Otter account — can remove Notetaker from a live Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams meeting.

  • Type stop otter in the meeting chat. Otter says all Notetakers in that meeting are removed immediately. If more than one Notetaker is present and you only want to remove one, use another method below instead of the chat command.
  • If the Otter account has the Notetaker chat setting enabled, participants can open the conversation from the Notetaker link in the chat, click Stop Notetaker, and confirm Yes, turn it off.
  • Otter users can stop Notetaker from the live conversation page or the homepage calendar.
  • A host or co-host can also remove the Notetaker participant through the meeting platform’s own controls. Otter points you to Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams documentation for those controls, and notes that its own screenshots may be out of date.

After you remove it from one meeting, it can still join later meetings unless you change the default auto-join setting and review individual events, as above. Otter also says you can disconnect your calendar to remove future events from Otter.

If you would rather not have a bot in the call

Stopping auto-join is a setting in Otter; it does not, by itself, switch you to a bot-free recorder.

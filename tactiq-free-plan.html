Quick pick

Tactiq Free: As of 22 November 2026, official pages call Free “free forever,” with no credit card, for 1 user. The standing caps are 10 transcripts per month and 5 AI credits per month. The homepage says no bot joins the call, and that Tactiq does not record or store meeting audio.

What the Free plan includes

The pricing page labels Free “For 1 user” and “Free forever. No credit card.” The Free card lists 5 AI credits / mo, 10 transcripts / mo, AI-generated insights, share and export transcripts, and in-meeting AI. The comparison table and the 30 June 2026 help article use the same transcript and AI-credit caps. The pricing FAQ says the Free plan lets you try Tactiq with no credit card and no time limit.

The same FAQ defines one AI credit as a single AI action: generating a meeting summary, drafting action items, or asking Tactiq AI a question. Free includes 5 credits per month, Pro includes 10, and Team and above include unlimited AI credits. Unlimited transcripts start on Pro.

What happens when you hit a cap

The pricing FAQ answers “What happens when I hit my transcript or AI credit limit?” with this wording: Tactiq keeps transcribing, but AI features pause until your limit resets at the start of the next month — or until you upgrade. Existing transcripts always stay accessible.

How Tactiq captures a meeting, including Google Meet

The homepage describes Tactiq as an AI note taker for Google Meet, Zoom, and Teams, and says you get “live transcriptions without an AI bot joining the meeting.” A homepage FAQ, “Does Tactiq record the audio?”, answers: “No. Tactiq does not record or store the audio from your meetings. The transcript is happening in real-time and saved as a text.” The pricing page also mentions a free Chrome extension for Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams. For a wider Google Meet comparison, see Best free AI note takers for Google Meet.

How paid prices were shown on 22 November 2026

The Free plan itself is $0 or ¥0. Paid prices on the same page depend on how the page is localized. The pricing page says “Prices shown in your local currency.” Visible annual cards, fetched from Japan on 22 November 2026, showed Pro at ¥1,249, Team at ¥2,541, and Business at ¥4,800 per user per month, with annual billing. Structured data on that same page also lists USD figures: Free $0, Pro $8, Team $16.67, and Business $29.17. I am quoting both. I am not converting yen to dollars.

The help article dated 30 June 2026 lists USD prices for both billing intervals: Pro $8 per user per month billed annually or $12 monthly; Team $16.67 or $20; Business $29.16 or $40; Enterprise custom. That Business annual figure is one cent below the $29.17 in the pricing page’s structured data. The help article also says localized pricing may be shown at checkout, and that you can view current prices at checkout. Full paid-plan context is in Tactiq pricing explained.

Quick verdict by use case

There is no single winner. These are readings of the official pages, not test results.

  • A clearly stated free allowance. Otter is the clearer page. Basic is 300 minutes per user each month, 30 minutes per conversation, and the 25 most recent conversations. Fireflies’ free plan is $0, but its pricing table, free-plan article, and AI-credits guide do not agree on minutes, meetings, or credits.
  • A longer free meeting. Fireflies’ pricing table lists a 2-hour recording limit on Free. Otter’s comparison lists 30 minutes per conversation on Basic. If that 2-hour row is what the product enforces, Fireflies fits longer client calls on the free plan. I have not checked which product actually stops the recording.
  • Japanese, as the help centers state it. Otter’s language article, updated 10 November 2026, lists Japanese as a transcription language. Fireflies lists Japanese (ja) among 100+ languages, and its pricing FAQ still calls non-English languages beta. Otter’s own Chrome extension article is narrower and still says Spanish, French, or English. Neither page gives an accuracy number I can repeat as a test.
  • A visible bot with your name on it. Otter joins as “[Your Name]’s Notetaker (Otter.ai)” and says it cannot join unseen. Fireflies joins as Fireflies.ai Notetaker and says that name cannot be hidden unless you buy a rename add-on. For introducing the tool to a client, Otter’s default name is easier to explain as yours. The consent script on the Fireflies review is written for the Fireflies name; change the product name if you use Otter.
  • Slack action items and a documented CRM. Fireflies’ Slack article describes a summary plus an action-item checklist in a channel or DM. Otter’s pricing page names Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zapier on the Pro card, and the comparison grid shows CRM sync for 1 user on Pro, 5 users on Business, and unlimited on Enterprise. Fireflies’ plan gate for CRM is disputed across its own pages.

Otter prices, including the ones that do not match

A later, line-by-line walkthrough of Otter’s plans, monthly versus annual figures, and minute caps, checked again on 20 November 2026, is in Otter.ai pricing explained.

On 19 November 2026 the Otter pricing page showed more than one annual Pro price. The plan card says Pro is $16.99 per user per month, and $8.49 per user per month with “save 50%.” A separate annual price attribute on the same page says $8.33 per user per month with “save 51%.” I am not choosing between $8.33 and $8.49.

The Business monthly card shows $30 and $24 per user per month with the words “20% off for 3 months.” The page does not say, in the text I read, what the price is after those three months. Business annual is labeled $19.99 per user per month with “save 33%.” Enterprise is custom. A student and teacher discount is separate: the FAQ says Pro annual is $6.67 per month, billed $79.99 a year, and Pro monthly is $13.59, for an individual .edu address. That discount is not the list price in the table above.

Who should pick which

Pick Otter if you want the free limits written in one comparison table: 300 minutes, 30-minute conversations, and 25 saved conversations, and you would rather not reconcile Fireflies’ free-plan pages. Otter is also the clearer official fit if Japanese transcription matters and you can select the language before the meeting. Its November 2026 language article lists Japanese without calling Japanese beta. The bot’s default name includes your name, which is easier to explain on a client call than a vendor name. Pro’s published minute cap is 1,200 a month and 90 minutes a meeting, which is a known ceiling rather than Fireflies’ “unlimited transcriptions” path.

Pick Fireflies if you want the pricing table’s 2-hour cap on the free plan, or the help-center path that says auto-join removes the free transcription cap for signups on or after 1 September 2026, and you can live with the conflicting pages. It also fits if you want the Slack article’s action-item checklist in a channel, or the longer language list, knowing the pricing FAQ still calls non-English beta. Desktop Take Notes covers Zoom, Meet, and Teams without a bot in the room. Otter’s desktop app is also botless, but the Chrome extension still adds a visible notetaker.

If the client must not see any bot, neither product’s main notetaker qualifies. Use the desktop path on either one, and still tell the client. I have not tested whether those desktop recordings are obvious to the other person.

Introducing the bot

Otter’s notetaker article says the bot cannot join anonymously, that it is a guest participant, and that you should get any required consent and follow recording laws and Otter’s terms. Fireflies says the same kind of thing: the bot cannot be hidden, the chat message alone may not meet consent laws, and you should tell people. A sample script for the Fireflies name, plus a not-legal-advice note, is in How to introduce a notetaker bot to clients. For Otter, use the same idea and say the participant will be named with your name and “Notetaker (Otter.ai).”

FAQ

Is Otter or Fireflies free?

Both publish a $0 plan. Otter Basic is 300 transcription minutes per user each month, 30 minutes per conversation, and the 25 most recent conversations. Fireflies calls Free free forever, but its pages disagree: the pricing table marks transcripts Limited and storage 400 minutes per team, while free-plan help says 3 meetings unless auto-join is on and 400 minutes per seat.

Do Otter and Fireflies put a bot in the meeting?

Yes, in the usual setup. Otter says Notetaker cannot join unseen and appears as a guest, named with your name plus Notetaker (Otter.ai). Fireflies says its bot appears as Fireflies.ai Notetaker and cannot be hidden. Both also document a desktop option that records without a bot joining as a participant.

Does Otter support Japanese?

Otter’s supported-languages help, updated 10 November 2026, lists Japanese as a transcription language. The pricing page also names Japanese. A Chrome extension article still says the extension transcribes Spanish, French, or English and hopes to add more. Otter says some languages are in beta without naming Japanese. Fireflies lists Japanese (ja) among 100+ languages, and its pricing FAQ still calls non-English languages beta.

How much do Otter and Fireflies cost?

On 19 November 2026, Otter’s pricing page showed Pro at $16.99 per user monthly. The same page shows an annual Pro price as both $8.49 per user per month (save 50%) and $8.33 (save 51%). Business monthly shows $30 and $24 with the words “20% off for 3 months,” and Business annual shows $19.99 per user per month (save 33%). Fireflies listed Pro at $10 per seat monthly when billed annually or $18 billed monthly, Business at $19 or $29, and Enterprise at $39 annual only. Check both pricing pages before you buy.

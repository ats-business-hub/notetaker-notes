Sonix vs Bluedot for Small Teams (2026)

I read Sonix’s pricing page and AI meeting recorder page, and Bluedot’s pricing page and homepage, on 21 November 2026 (Japan time). I have not used either product on a client call. Longer write-ups are in the Sonix review, Sonix pricing, Bluedot review, and Bluedot pricing. Related comparisons include Sonix vs Circleback and Bluedot vs Laxis.

Quick pick

  • Bluedot: Official pages present a bot-free note taker, with a $0 Free plan that covers 5 meetings for the life of the account. Annual cards list Basic at $14, Pro at $20, and Business at $32 per user per month; the Monthly cards list $18, $25, and $39. The Basic card is labeled audio only.

    See Bluedot plans

  • Sonix: Official pages center on file uploads, with a 30-minute free trial that does not require a credit card. Sonix Agent, which joins Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams, is included with Pro and Enterprise; the free trial covers uploaded recordings, not the agent. Pay as You Go is $10 an hour; Core, Advanced, and Pro start at $25, $50, and $80 a month.

How each one records

Bluedot’s homepage title is “Bluedot AI Note Taker | Bot-Free & Privacy-First.” The homepage FAQ “What makes Bluedot different?” says “no bot joins your call,” and the FAQ “Does Bluedot work with Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams?” says yes: Bluedot captures audio or video from your browser or desktop rather than joining as a bot, and is compatible with any platform, including Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, and Slack huddles or phone calls. The pricing cards list a Chrome extension and desktop and mobile apps. I have not watched either capture path on a call.

Sonix’s AI meeting recorder page says Sonix Agent joins your Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams call and writes the transcript while people talk. It says speakers are named from the call, 52 languages work live, and the agent is included with Pro and Enterprise plans, while the free trial covers uploaded recordings. A comparison on that page lists “Sonix Agent in the call” on Pro and Enterprise, and “Upload the recording” on all plans and the free trial. The FAQ says the agent does not join Webex or other meeting apps, so those recordings should be uploaded instead, and that the free trial and Pay as You Go cover uploaded files, not the live agent. The FAQ also says Sonix Agent appears in the participant list under that name.

Free plans and trials

Bluedot’s Free card is $0 per user per month, with 5 meetings / lifetime. The homepage FAQ “Can I use Bluedot for free?” says the Free plan lets you try all core features for your first 5 meetings (lifetime trial), and that after that you can continue as a free member to view and share recordings, or upgrade for unlimited recording and transcription.

Sonix’s pricing page offers a 30-minute free trial, with no credit card required, and says you can cancel anytime. It does not present a standing $0 monthly plan like Bluedot Free. The AI meeting recorder FAQ, “Is the AI meeting recorder part of the free trial?”, answers no: the meeting recorder comes with Pro and Enterprise, and the free trial and Pay as You Go cover uploaded files. A 5-meeting lifetime cap and a 30-minute upload trial are not the same kind of offer, and I am not declaring a winner.

Paid prices, as of 21 November 2026

Bluedot’s Annual cards list Basic at $14 per user per month (audio only, unlimited meetings with audio only, unlimited storage), Pro at $20 (video), and Business at $32. The Monthly cards list $18, $25, and $39. The FAQ says only recorders are billed, added seats are prorated, payment is per workspace, and cancelled subscriptions lose access to recordings, with one month to restore before permanent deletion.

Sonix’s pricing page lists Pay as You Go at $10 an hour, with AI workspace usage not available, 5 GB of storage, and a single-user account. Core is $25 a month or $275 a year, with 5 hours a month or 60 hours a year of transcription and translation, plus a separate 5 hours a month or 60 hours a year of AI workspace usage, and 25 GB of storage. Advanced is $50 a month or $550 a year, with 20 / 240 transcription hours, 25 / 300 AI workspace hours, and 50 GB of storage. Pro is $80 a month or $880 a year, with 40 / 480 transcription hours, 100 / 1,200 AI workspace hours, and 100 GB of storage. Extra hours on any subscription plan are $10 an hour. Extra seats are $25 a month or $275 a year and, the page says, do not add more hours. The AI meeting recorder page says the recorder is part of Pro at $80 a month and of every Enterprise plan. I have not turned Sonix hours into Bluedot seats.

Who should pick which

These suggestions follow only what the official pages say. I have not tested accuracy, the extension, the desktop app, or Sonix Agent.

Look at Bluedot if you want a product whose official homepage calls itself bot-free, annual and monthly list prices per recording user, a Chrome extension plus desktop and mobile apps on the Free card, and you can accept a 5-meeting lifetime cap on Free. Plans are not equivalent: Bluedot’s Basic card is labeled audio only. Look at Sonix if you mainly upload audio or video, want a 30-minute no-card trial of that upload path, and can move to Pro when you want Sonix Agent in Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams, as the official recorder page states.

FAQ

Can I record a live meeting?

Bluedot’s homepage calls the product bot-free and says it captures audio or video from your browser or desktop rather than joining as a bot. Sonix Agent joins Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams and is included with Pro and Enterprise; the free trial covers uploads, not the agent. This page has not tested either product.

What can I try for $0?

Bluedot Free is a standing $0 plan with a 5-meeting lifetime cap. Sonix offers a 30-minute free trial of uploaded recordings, not a standing free monthly plan and not the live agent. I am not declaring a winner from those different offers.

Which costs less, Sonix or Bluedot?

They price on different metrics. As of 21 November 2026, Bluedot Pro was $20 per user per month on the Annual cards, or $25 monthly; Bluedot Basic was $14 annually or $18 monthly, and is labeled audio only. Sonix Core was $25 a month or $275 a year, and Sonix Pro, which includes the agent, was $80 a month or $880 a year. Check both live pages before you buy.

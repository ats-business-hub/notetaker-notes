Who might look at an alternative

I am not telling every Tactiq user to leave. The reasons below come from Tactiq’s own pages on 20 November 2026, not from a test of the product.

You need a bot to attend a meeting you cannot join. Tactiq’s official pages describe capture from your browser, or from a Mac app for Zoom desktop calls, with no bot in the participant list. If you want a guest notetaker to join when you are away, look at a tool that documents that path.

You join Microsoft Teams from the desktop app, or Zoom from a Windows desktop app. A Tactiq help article says the Microsoft Teams desktop application is not currently supported, and tells you to continue in the browser or the Teams PWA. The download page says the Mac app transcribes Zoom desktop calls only, and I did not find a Windows desktop app listed there.

You want a phone app, or stored meeting audio. Tactiq’s download page lists a Chrome extension and a Mac app. I did not find an iPhone or Android app on that page. The homepage FAQ says Tactiq does not record or store meeting audio, and that the transcript is saved as text.

You are hitting the published AI-credit caps. Free is 5 AI credits a month. Pro is still 10 AI credits a month, even though transcripts become unlimited. Unlimited AI credits start on Team, at $16.67 per user per month billed annually, or $20 monthly in a help article dated 30 June 2026. The pricing FAQ says one credit covers a single AI action, such as a summary, action items, or an Ask Tactiq question, and that AI features pause when you hit the cap.

You cannot depend on meeting captions. The install help article says Tactiq uses the meeting platform’s captions to create the transcript, and tells you to keep captions turned on.

What Tactiq’s plans say today

As of 20 November 2026, Tactiq’s pricing page listed Free at $0, with no credit card, 10 transcripts per month, and 5 AI credits per month; Pro at $8 per user per month billed annually, with unlimited transcripts and 10 AI credits; Team at $16.67 per user per month billed annually, with unlimited AI credits, for 1 to 20 users; Business at $29.17 per user per month billed annually, for 20 to 200 users; and Enterprise as custom, for 200 or more users. A help article dated 30 June 2026 also lists monthly prices of $12, $20, and $40 for Pro, Team, and Business, and lists the Business annual rate as $29.16, one cent below the pricing page. A longer walkthrough is in the Tactiq review.

Alternatives, with an honest “best for”

These five tools have approved affiliate links on this site. “Best for” means the official pages support that use, not that I ranked accuracy. List prices for Fireflies, Noota, Bluedot, Circleback, and Laxis below are the same figures already published on this site from official pages checked on 20 November 2026.

Bluedot — best for bot-free capture that also names a Windows desktop app

Bluedot is the closest like-for-like switch if you want capture that does not invite a bot, and you also want a documented desktop app on Windows. The homepage, checked 20 November 2026, says it never invites a bot and uses a Chrome extension or a desktop app instead. The FAQ names Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, and Slack huddles or phone calls. As of that date, the pricing page listed Free at $0 with 5 meetings for the life of the account; Basic at $14 per user per month on the annual row or $18 monthly, with unlimited audio-only meetings and a 1-hour cap per recording; Pro at $20 or $25, adding video and unlimited recording duration; and Business at $32 or $39, adding CRM and ATS integrations. Skip it if five lifetime free meetings are not enough to try it, or if you need a bot to attend a meeting you skip. See the Bluedot review and the bot-free comparison.

Fireflies — best for a calendar bot that can join when you are away

Fireflies is the clearest official contrast if the missing bot is why Tactiq does not fit. Help says it can automatically join, record, and transcribe Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. The bot appears as Fireflies.ai Notetaker and cannot be hidden. Official pages also document no-bot paths: a Google Meet Chrome extension, and desktop Take Notes that captures system audio. As of 20 November 2026, the pricing page listed Free at $0, Pro at $10 per seat per month billed annually or $18 monthly, Business at $19 or $29, and Enterprise at $39 annual only. Official Fireflies pages still disagree on Free-plan limits; that conflict is walked through in Fireflies.ai pricing explained. See the Fireflies review.

Circleback — best for a bot or a bot-free desktop app, including on Free

Circleback documents both a bot that can join from a connected calendar or a pasted link, and a bot-free desktop app for macOS and Windows. Official pages say recording with the bot or the desktop app is included on every plan, including Free. As of 20 November 2026, the pricing page yearly cards showed Free at $0, Pro at $15 per user per month, Business at $25, and Enterprise at $35. The FAQ says every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings, with 30-day history on Free. That free allowance is broader than Tactiq Free’s 10 transcripts a month, if you accept a 30-day history cap. See the Circleback review.

Laxis — best for a $0 plan with published minute caps, plus a phone-friendly path on official pages

Laxis help says the meeting bot joins Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams, and that a Chrome extension can add live transcripts to those platforms in the browser. Desktop help describes recording without the bot joining as a participant. As of 20 November 2026, the pricing page listed Basic at $0 with 300 transcription minutes a month and a 45-minute cap per recording; Premium at $9.99 a month with 2,000 minutes; and Business at $19.99 a month with unlimited transcription. The page’s meta description labels the Premium and Business figures as billed yearly. A FAQ says every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of Premium, then moves to Basic. Noota — best for bot or desktop recording, with prices in euros

Noota publishes dedicated pages for Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet. As of 20 November 2026, list prices were Free €0, Pro €19 per user per month billed annually or €29 monthly, and Business €39 or €49. Questions, answered from the official pages

Why would a small team look at a Tactiq alternative?

Tactiq’s official pages describe a Chrome or Edge extension that transcribes Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams without a bot, using the meeting platform’s captions. The download page lists a Mac app for Zoom desktop only, and a help article says the Microsoft Teams desktop app is not currently supported. I did not find an iPhone or Android app on the download page. Free is 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month; Pro still lists 10 AI credits a month. Teams that need a bot to attend a meeting they skip, a phone app, a Windows desktop recorder, or unlimited AI summaries on a mid-price plan often look at other tools.

What does Tactiq cost?

As of 20 November 2026, Tactiq’s pricing page listed Free at $0, Pro at $8 per user per month billed annually, Team at $16.67, Business at $29.17, and Enterprise as custom. A help article dated 30 June 2026 lists monthly prices of $12, $20, and $40 for Pro, Team, and Business, and lists Business annual as $29.16. Check the current pricing page before you buy.

Which Tactiq alternative also records without a bot?

Bluedot’s homepage says it never invites a bot and records with a Chrome extension or a desktop app, including on Windows. Circleback, Fireflies, Noota, and Laxis all document a bot-free desktop or browser path in addition to a bot. I have not tested any of these paths. See also the bot-free comparison on this site.

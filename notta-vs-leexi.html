Notta vs Leexi: what the official pages show and how to choose

If you are comparing Notta and Leexi, you are comparing two AI meeting-notes tools that both list sales calls among their use cases. This page does not pick a universal winner. It separates what the official pages show from what still needs checking on your side, and gives a short decision checklist.

How this page was made: it uses the vendors' public official pages as displayed on 28 November 2026 (Japan time). No hands-on test of either tool was run. Prices, plan limits and features change often, so check the official pages before you subscribe. Notta and Leexi are linked to their official sites and are not affiliate links; the Fireflies link at the end is an affiliate link.

Quick answer

  • Check Notta first if you want a free plan that needs no credit card to try on a few meetings, or a recorder that does not join as a bot. Notta's pricing page lists a Free plan and a "Bot-free Meeting Recorder (record on your device, no bot joins)" option. Which plans include which option is shown on that page.
  • Check Leexi first if data hosting in Europe and formal security standards matter to your team, or your workflow runs through a CRM or ATS. Leexi's home page states European data hosting, GDPR compliance and ISO 27001 certification, and lists HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho CRM and RecruitCRM among its integrations.
  • Not sure: use the free plan or trial, run it on two or three real meetings of your own, and compare the notes.

Five questions that decide it

1. What kind of meetings are you recording? Both vendors list sales use cases, so compare how each one fits your own meeting types. See best AI note taker for sales and best AI note taker for interviews.

2. Which languages are spoken? If your meetings mix Japanese and English, check each tool's supported languages and how it handles a language switch in the middle of a meeting. See AI note takers for Japanese and English meetings.

3. Do you need recorded files, live meetings, or both? Some tools are stronger at live meetings, others at uploaded recordings. See best AI note takers for recorded files.

4. How will participants feel about a bot joining? Whatever you choose, tell participants that notes are being taken. See how to tell participants and bot-free meeting recorders.

5. What happens to the notes afterwards? If notes need to end up in Google Docs, a CRM or a task tracker, check the export and integration options. See exporting notes to Google Docs and turning notes into action items.

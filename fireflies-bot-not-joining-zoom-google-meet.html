Fireflies bot not joining Zoom or Google Meet

Fireflies joins a meeting as a participant, usually labelled Fireflies.ai Notetaker or Fred. Official help says that if Fred does not appear, start with the meeting status in the Fireflies app, then match that status to the cause. The steps below are taken from those official articles. Some Zoom or Google Workspace settings may need an admin. Menu labels can change; use Fireflies’ current help if a label does not match.

First: check Notifications → Status

Fireflies’ troubleshooting guide says to log in, click the bell icon, open the Status tab, and find the meeting. Official examples of what you may see include:

  • Denied Entry — the host or a participant rejected Fred’s request to join.
  • In Waiting Room or Not allowed in — Fred waited and was not admitted.
  • Connection Failed — the meeting link, platform settings, or an integration problem blocked the connection.
  • Meeting requires sign-in or a similar login error — login, password, encryption, CAPTCHA, or domain restrictions blocked Fred.
  • Meeting was too short — the meeting ended in under 3 minutes, so no recap was created. Fireflies also says it does not process meetings shorter than 3 minutes, including when Fred is removed early.

The same guide says a “Completed” row with “Joined on behalf of” a host or teammate means Fred joined from someone else’s account; your auto-join preferences or Meeting Rules may have stopped it from joining under yours.

Check auto-join and the calendar invite

From the Fireflies Home page, official help says to open Settings and review Auto-join calendar meetings. If this is set to Only when I invite fred@fireflies.ai and you do not add that address, Fireflies says the bot will not join and no transcript is generated. Other official options include all meetings with a web-conference link, only meetings you own, and only meetings with teammates.

The same official guide also lists invite problems that stop Fred:

  • The calendar is not connected to Fireflies, or the event was created with a different email than the connected account.
  • The invite does not contain a valid Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams URL, or the link is incomplete.
  • Fred was invited only after the meeting had already started — official help says that is too late for auto-join; use Add to Live Meeting instead and paste the full meeting URL.

Meeting Rules, when they exist, “always override” auto-join, according to the same guide. Bar Rules can block meetings with keywords or domains you have excluded.

Zoom-specific checks

Fireflies’ Zoom setup article says Fred joins through the Zoom web client as a guest. Official items to verify — some only an admin can change — include:

  • Turn off “Only authenticated users can join from Web client.”
  • Turn off end-to-end encryption (E2EE). Fireflies says E2EE prevents it from recording and transcribing.
  • Turn off the Waiting Room, or be ready to admit Fred. If the waiting room stays on and nobody lets Fred in, the bot will not record.
  • Turn on “Allow participants to join before host,” so Fred is not stuck if it arrives before you.
  • Computer audio must be available. Fireflies says it cannot hear participants who join by phone only, in the context of this setting.

Important official caveat: Fireflies says Zoom settings such as guest access and the waiting room are embedded when the meeting link is created. If you change Zoom settings after the invite already exists, Fred may still fail to join. The official advice is to fix the settings, then create a new meeting link.

If the Zoom meeting has a password, Fireflies says the password must be in the invite details or in the URL (the pwd= parameter). A link without that parameter can block automatic entry.

If you are not the host, Fireflies says Zoom may ask the host to admit Fred.

Google Meet-specific checks

Fireflies official FAQs say Fred may appear in Google Meet as a participant with a “potential risk” label. The same FAQs describe that label as part of a Google Meet security update, not as a finding that the bot is unsafe. If you do not see an Admit button, official help says to look under a With potential risks section in the participants panel, click the three dots next to Fireflies Notetaker, and select Admit entry.

For Google Workspace, the troubleshooting guide says to check Meet Safety Settings and, under Domain, allow “All users (including users not signed in with a Google account)” if Fred needs to join as a guest. That setting is an admin control; I am repeating the official wording, not advising you to weaken a workplace policy.

The same official FAQs say Fireflies will wait up to 10 minutes in the lobby or waiting room for Google Meet, and up to 15 minutes on other platforms, then leave. If the meeting starts later, add Fred again with Add to Live Meeting.

If Fred joined but you still have no notes

That is a different official problem from “did not join.” Fireflies says transcription can take 5–10 minutes after the meeting ends; check Notifications → Status if it is still in progress. Meetings under 3 minutes are not processed. If you attended but were not the host, a restricted recap-privacy setting can hide the transcript until the host shares it.

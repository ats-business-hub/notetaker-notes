Circleback Alternatives (2026): Free and Cheaper Options

Last checked: 25 November 2026 (Japan time). Figures come from official pricing pages and help articles read that day. Circleback’s monthly-billing prices were not readable on its page, so only the yearly-card prices are used. This compares published plans, not accuracy.

Quick pick

Circleback already has a free plan with unlimited meetings, so most people looking for an alternative want either a lower paid price than Pro at $15 per user per month (yearly), longer free history than 30 days, or noise cancellation. Krisp Core is $8 per user per month billed annually and adds noise cancellation, but has only a 7-day trial. Fireflies Pro is $10 annual. Fathom Free is “free forever” with unlimited recordings.

When Circleback is still the better pick

If you want to stay at $0 without a meeting cap, Circleback Free is one of the few plans here with unlimited meetings (its FAQ says every plan includes them). It also lists a 30% non-profit discount and a 50% student discount, and supports Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, WebEx, Slack huddles and GoTo. See the Circleback review and Circleback pricing.

FAQ

Is there a cheaper alternative to Circleback?

On list prices as of 25 November 2026, Krisp Core is $8 per user per month billed annually and Fireflies Pro is $10 annual, versus Circleback Pro at $15 per user per month on the yearly cards. Circleback Free remains $0 with unlimited meetings.

Does Circleback have a free plan?

Yes. Free is $0 with unlimited meetings and 30-day meeting and recording history, per its pricing page and FAQ on 25 November 2026.

Which Circleback alternative keeps free recordings longest?

Of the plans compared, tl;dv Free keeps recordings for 3 months per its help article, and Fathom Free is described as free forever with unlimited recordings. Check each page for current limits.

FAQ

Is there a free AI note taker for Google Meet?

Yes. Official pages checked on 22 November 2026 publish a $0 or €0 plan and also document Google Meet support for Fireflies, Tactiq, Noota, Circleback, Laxis, and Bluedot, and for Otter and Fathom. The free allowances differ: some are ongoing monthly caps, some are lifetime meeting counts, and some official pages disagree on the details. Check each vendor’s current pricing page before you rely on a free allowance.

Can I get Google Meet notes without a bot joining the call?

Several official pages describe a no-bot path that is available on a free plan, or at least documented as a product mode. Tactiq’s homepage says no bot joins the call and that it does not record or store audio. Fireflies documents a Google Meet-only Chrome extension that records audio, not video. Bluedot’s FAQ says it captures audio or video from your browser or desktop rather than joining as a bot, including on Google Meet; its Free plan is 5 lifetime meetings. Circleback documents a bot or a bot-free desktop app. Laxis help says the Chrome extension captures captions in Google Meet without a bot. Noota’s Google Meet page describes desktop recording with no bot in the participant list, but the pricing table marks Free as bot only.

Which free AI note taker is best for Google Meet?

Official pages do not support a single winner, and this page is not a hands-on test or a ranked score. Fireflies has the most detailed Google Meet help, including a Meet-only Chrome extension, but official pages disagree on Free-plan limits. Tactiq publishes the clearest standing monthly caps on a no-bot, no-audio-storage extension: 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month, with no credit card. Circleback’s pricing FAQ says every plan, including Free, has unlimited meetings, with 30-day history on Free. Choose by the official limit and capture method you can live with, then recheck the live pricing page.

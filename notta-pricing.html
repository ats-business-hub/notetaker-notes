Notta pricing at a glance

Notta sells four tiers: Free, Pro, Business, and Enterprise. The Pro and Business prices on its English pricing page (checked 26 November 2026) are annual-billing prices. Pro is listed as "$8.17 USD/month Total $97.99 · Billed annually," and Business as "$16.67 USD/month Total $199.99 · billed annually." A "Monthly" toggle sits at the top of the page, but the page text we checked showed no monthly-billing rate for Pro or Business. The rate may only load in the browser, so treat the monthly price as unconfirmed and check it at checkout. Enterprise is quoted individually.

Plan Published price Who it is for
Free $0, no credit card required Trying Notta on short recordings
Pro $8.17/month, $97.99 billed annually Individuals with regular meetings
Business $16.67/month, $199.99 billed annually Teams that need unlimited minutes and admin controls
Enterprise Custom pricing, from 51 seats Large organizations needing SSO and audit logs

The page also advertises "40% OFF" for annual billing on Pro and Business. Two more offers appear on it: a 7-day Business trial, and a 50% education discount. The FAQ says the discount is for the Pro plan, and you sign up with an educational email.

Notta lists all prices in USD. Local currency, taxes, and in-app purchase pricing on iOS or Android can differ, so check the final total at checkout.

Notta plans explained

Free

The Free plan costs nothing and needs no card at signup. Its transcription quota (no carryover) is "120 minutes per month". The more important limit is recording length: the Free plan card says "Up to 3 minutes per conversation," and the comparison table shows the same 3-minute maximum per recording.

So the 120 monthly minutes are spread across very short recordings. Free is good for checking transcription quality, speaker labels, and the interface. It cannot hold a full meeting transcript. The Free plan includes transcription of web meetings and speaker identification.

Other Free quotas on the pricing page: 10 AI summaries per month, 50 file transcriptions per month, 1 seat, and 3 custom vocabulary entries.

For a wider look at free tiers across tools, see Free plans and trials: Fireflies vs MeetGeek vs Krisp (November 2026).

Pro

Pro is Notta's individual paid plan. It raises the monthly quota to 1,800 transcription minutes (no carryover) and allows "Up to 5 hours per recording." AI summaries go up to 100 per month and file transcriptions to 100 per month. Pro has one seat.

Compared with Free, Pro adds exporting transcripts, transcript translation, and custom vocabulary. Pro allows 200 custom vocabulary entries. The comparison table labels custom vocabulary "English & Japanese".

Pro is the only plan with the 50% education discount mentioned above.

Business

Business is aimed at teams. Its transcription quota is listed as "Unlimited," with the same 5-hour cap per recording as Pro. AI summaries rise to 200 per month and file transcriptions to 200 per month. Custom vocabulary allows 1,000 entries.

On top of Pro, Business adds web meeting video recording, advanced data security controls, a usage report, and CRM and Zapier integration. If you need meeting notes pushed into Salesforce, HubSpot, or another CRM, Business is the lowest tier where the pricing page lists that.

Business also includes a shared workspace. Notta's pricing FAQ describes the workspace as a central place to access, organize, and search recordings and transcripts, where Business and Enterprise members can view and work on each other's content. The comparison table lists IP restriction for up to 20 IPs on Business.

The pricing page does not give a fixed seat count for Business. The plan card says "seat(s)" with no number.

Enterprise

Enterprise has custom pricing and is listed as "Starting from 51 seats." The transcription quota is "Customized," and AI summaries and file transcriptions are unlimited.

On top of Business, Enterprise adds SAML SSO, full data access control, audit logs, flexible payments, priority support, and customized transcription. The comparison table lists IP restriction for up to 1,000 IPs. The pricing FAQ says Enterprise orders can be paid by bank transfer.

Three points to note:

  1. Minutes do not roll over. On Free and Pro, unused minutes are lost at the end of the month.
  2. AI summaries have their own quota. Staying under your minute limit does not guarantee a summary for every meeting. On Pro, 100 summaries a month is the cap.
  3. File uploads are counted by number, not minutes. The file transcription limit counts files per month.

Notta add-ons and AI credits

The pricing page lists three add-ons and says they are "available exclusively to Pro, Business, and Enterprise plan users."

  • Monolingual Translation: $6 USD/month ($72 billed annually), or $10 USD/month on a monthly plan. Pro and Business can use it up to 100 times per account per month. Enterprise has no limit.
  • Bilingual Transcription & Translation: $9 USD/month ($108 billed annually), or $15 USD/month on a monthly plan. Pro and Business can use it up to 200 times per account per month. Enterprise has no limit.
  • Notta Brain: $93.59 USD/year for 8,000 AI Credits/month.

Notta Brain is Notta's AI workspace on top of your meetings. The homepage says it offers cross-meeting Q&A, meeting infographics, document building, follow-up email drafts, and Slack messages. The comparison table lists 1,000 AI credits a month on every plan, and the add-on increases that allowance.

According to the pricing FAQ, credits are used only when a result is actually delivered. A Knowledge Base question costs 100 credits, an image 200, a PPT file 300, and an Excel or Word file 200. If you plan to generate many documents or slides from meetings, use these rates to estimate whether the add-on is worth it.

How Notta captures meetings

You can capture meetings on any plan in several ways. The homepage describes an online meeting recorder that records "via a bot that joins your call," and a bot-free recorder that runs on your own device, where "no bot joins." It also lists:

  • A Chrome extension that can capture audio from any browser tab
  • iOS and Android apps
  • Transcription of audio files, video files, or YouTube links
  • A web app, a desktop app for Mac and Windows, and screen recording
  • Notta Memo, a separate hardware device that attaches to a phone to record calls and meetings

Notta supports Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex. Its homepage FAQ describes the integrations with Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet as native.

On languages, the homepage says Notta supports "58 different languages for transcription and translation." The comparison table also gives every plan, including Free, up to 4 free uses a month of Bilingual Transcription & Translation and up to 2 free uses a month of monolingual real-time translation. The add-ons above are for heavier use.

The capture method affects how a meeting feels to attendees. If you don't want a bot to join calls, see Bot-Free AI Meeting Note Takers: Bluedot, Circleback, Laxis, and Tactiq (2026).

  • CRM: Freshsales, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesflare, Salesforce, Zendesk Sell, Zoho CRM
  • Video conferencing: Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Zoom

The integrations page itself does not say which plan includes each integration. The pricing page does say that CRM and Zapier integration are added on Business. If you need CRM or Zapier, budget for Business or higher.

Security and compliance

Notta's security page says the company "has successfully obtained SOC 2 Type II certification through an independent audit." The page also says Notta:

  • Follows ISO 27001 guidelines (the pricing FAQ calls Notta "SOC2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified")
  • Is compliant with GDPR and CCPA
  • Follows HIPAA guidelines for medical records and health information

On technical controls, data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.2 and data at rest with AES-256. All software is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS), and user data is regularly backed up to AWS S3 and Aurora. The page also mentions vulnerability scanning, periodic penetration testing, an activity audit log, and role-based permissions.

On pricing, SAML SSO, audit logs, and full data access control are listed only on Enterprise. IP restriction starts on Business.

Billing, plan changes, and cancellation

The pricing FAQ covers:

  • Payment methods: credit or debit cards (American Express, Discover, Mastercard, Visa, JCB), in-app purchases through Apple or Google accounts, and bank transfers for Enterprise orders.
  • Switching plans: You can switch from monthly to annual in the billing section. Moving from Pro to Business, or from annual to monthly, means canceling and subscribing to the new plan. Your current plan stays active until the next renewal date.
  • Cancellation: Paid features stay available until the end of the billing period. You can cancel through Notta Web (Billing > Cancel Subscription), the App Store, or Google Play.
  • Data after downgrading: Transcripts are kept, but Free users can view only the first 3 minutes of each one. Notta recommends exporting transcripts before you downgrade.

The last point matters most for budgeting. If you might drop back to Free, export everything first, because your older long recordings will be cut off at 3 minutes in the Free view.

Which Notta plan fits which user?

Based only on the published limits:

  • Free: Use it to test transcription quality and the interface. The 3-minute cap per conversation means it won't hold real meetings.
  • Pro: Fits an individual who records fewer than 1,800 minutes (30 hours) of meetings a month, needs no more than 100 AI summaries, and wants exports and translation. Students with an educational email can get 50% off Pro.
  • Business: Fits heavy users or teams who need unlimited transcription minutes, a shared workspace, video recording, CRM or Zapier sync, and usage reports.
  • Enterprise: Fits organizations of 51+ seats that need SAML SSO, audit logs, and custom transcription.

If translation matters to you, note that every plan gets only a few free translation uses a month (4 bilingual, 2 monolingual real-time). If you need more, add the add-on cost to the base plan price.

FAQ

Does Notta have a free plan? Yes. The Free plan includes 120 transcription minutes per month (no carryover), but each conversation is capped at 3 minutes. It also includes 10 AI summaries and 50 file transcriptions per month.

Tactiq vs Laxis: plans, capture style, and official limits

This comparison uses Tactiq’s and Laxis’s official pricing, feature and help pages, checked on 27 November 2026. It is not a hands-on test and does not rank accuracy. The “See plans” buttons are affiliate links, and I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Confirm live prices before you buy. #ad

Tactiq and Laxis both advertise bot-free meeting notes, but they're built around different ideas. Tactiq is a browser extension that transcribes video calls as they happen. Laxis is a wider product suite: a note taker that also records in-person conversations, a voice keyboard and recording hardware. This comparison sticks to what each vendor publishes: plan names, prices, transcript and minute caps, and how each one captures audio.

No hands-on testing was done for this page. For deeper single-tool coverage, see Tactiq Review for Small Businesses (2026) and Laxis Review for Small Businesses (2026).

Capture style: browser extension vs multi-device recorder

Tactiq runs in your browser. Its features page describes real-time transcription with no bot, delivered through a bot-free extension for Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams (tactiq.io). The homepage says Tactiq doesn't record or store meeting audio, and that transcripts are visible only to the user by default (tactiq.io). That suits people who want text notes without a participant tile labeled "Notetaker" and without audio files piling up.

The trade-off is scope. Tactiq's official pages describe video calls in the browser. The pages checked for this comparison don't describe recording a conversation in a meeting room or on a phone call, though the plan table does list uploading meeting files (tactiq.io).

Laxis offers several capture paths (laxis.com): - No-bot recording directly on your device, with an optional assistant bot that joins calendar events you choose - File upload for existing audio or video - Phone calls and in-person conversations through the mobile app or Laxis earbuds - A Chrome extension for Google Meet

The Laxis homepage also lists Apple Watch support and a 360° meeting camera for in-person recording (laxis.com). If your meetings move between Zoom, a conference room and a phone call, Laxis covers more of them on paper.

Free plans: transcript count vs minute budget

The two free plans measure usage differently:

  • Tactiq Free counts meetings: 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits per month (tactiq.io). Length isn't the constraint. The number of meetings is.
  • Laxis Basic counts time: 300 minutes per month, no single recording longer than 45 minutes, and 30 days of conversation storage (laxis.com).

Someone with a few long calls a week may run into Laxis's 45-minute cap first. Someone with many short check-ins may use up Tactiq's 10 transcripts first. See Fireflies vs MeetGeek vs Krisp Free Plans (2026) for how other tools compare.

Limits that change the decision

Recording length. Laxis prints a per-recording maximum on every paid tier below Enterprise: 2 hours on Premium and 4 hours on Business (laxis.com). Tactiq's features page says no other limits, such as meeting length, are stated (tactiq.io). Check Tactiq's help center if you need a hard answer for very long sessions.

AI credits. Tactiq meters AI credits on Free (5) and Pro (10), with unlimited credits from Team up (tactiq.io). Unused credits don't roll over (help.tactiq.io). Laxis's pricing page lists no credit limits.

Storage. Laxis Basic keeps conversations for 30 days. Premium and above keep them indefinitely (laxis.com). Tactiq's buy page lists advanced data retention as a Business feature (tactiq.io). The buy page doesn't state how long Free-plan transcripts are kept.

Team size. Tactiq ties its plans to seat ranges: Team for 1–20 users, Business for 20–200 and Enterprise for 200+ (tactiq.io). Laxis doesn't print seat ranges on its pricing page.

Platforms and integrations

Tactiq lists Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Whereby and Jitsi as meeting platforms. Export destinations include Google Docs, Confluence, Quip, Notion, Slack, Google Calendar, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive and Linear (tactiq.io). Several other tools on that page are marked as coming soon.

Laxis lists Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, with Skype, Discord, Dialpad, Webex, WhatsApp and Calendly shown in its integration graphics. CRMs include Salesforce, HubSpot and Wealthbox. Productivity tools include Slack, Notion, Jira, Monday, Confluence, Google Docs, Dropbox, Google Drive and ClickUp, plus Zapier and a REST API with webhooks (laxis.com). Laxis's pricing page puts CRM automation and "7000+ Apps Integrations" in the Business plan (laxis.com).

Both list CRM destinations, so both come up in sales workflows. See Best AI Note Takers for Sales Teams (2026).

AI features and connectors

Tactiq offers live summaries, action items, in-meeting "Ask AI," custom prompts, transcript translation, and AI workflows that push insights to Linear, HubSpot and Slack. It also has a Tactiq MCP, a Claude connector and a ChatGPT plugin (tactiq.io). The buy page lists the Tactiq MCP and Claude Connector as beta features on Business (tactiq.io).

Laxis offers real-time transcription and summaries, merges your own scratchpad notes into the summary, answers questions across past recordings, and has 50+ report templates (laxis.com). Its pricing page puts AI Writer with 40+ templates, AI Chat, live translation, keyword tracking and an MCP connector in Premium (laxis.com). The two pages give different template counts (50+ vs 40+). Laxis also bundles a voice keyboard for dictation into the same plans (laxis.com), which Tactiq doesn't offer.

Security and data statements

These are vendor statements, not independent audits.

  • Tactiq claims ISO 27001 certification, SOC 2 Type II attestation, and GDPR and HIPAA compliance. It says it uses OpenAI's enterprise API, which it says doesn't train on submitted data (tactiq.io).
  • Laxis says it is SOC 2 Type I certified, encrypts data in transit and at rest on Google Cloud, and never uses meeting or dictation data to train AI models (laxis.com).

Tactiq Business adds SAML SSO (tactiq.io). Laxis lists full API and SSO integration on Enterprise (laxis.com).

Education and non-profit pricing

Tactiq's buy page invites teachers and students to apply for an education plan, with no price printed (tactiq.io). Its help center says approval goes through a school-issued email (help.tactiq.io). Laxis's pricing FAQ mentions specialized pricing for non-profits and educational institutions through its partnership team, also with no amount published (laxis.com).

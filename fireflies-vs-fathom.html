Quick verdict by use case

There is no single winner. These are readings of the official pages, not test results.

  • A $0 plan whose limits are written clearly, with no monthly meeting cap. Fathom is the clearer free card. The pricing page lists unlimited recordings and transcriptions, and a help article caps advanced summaries at the first 5 calls each month rather than capping the number of meetings. Fireflies also has a $0 plan called free forever, but its pricing table and free-plan help do not agree on transcripts, storage, or meeting counts.
  • A lower paid entry price. Fireflies Pro at $10 per seat per month billed annually is lower than Fathom Premium at $16 billed annually. Fireflies Pro billed monthly, at $18, is also lower than Fathom Premium billed monthly at $20. Fathom Team annual, at $15 per user per month, is closer, and it has a 2-user minimum.
  • Bot-free capture on Windows, or a no-bot path that still saves audio or video. Fireflies documents a Desktop App Take Notes mode and a Chrome extension for Google Meet that do not add a bot. Take Notes, Fireflies says, does not save a separate audio or video file. Fathom’s bot-free capture is in beta on Mac, listed for Zoom, Google Meet, and Slack Huddles, with Windows support for the new desktop app described as coming. I have not tested either path.
  • A bot that can join when you are not in the meeting. Both document a calendar bot that can join Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Fireflies says it can record without you attending if the platform allows guest bots. Fathom’s features FAQ says a Fathom user needs to be in the meeting for bot-free capture; the Notetaker bot is the path named for joining those three platforms.

Free plans, and where Fireflies pages disagree

Fathom’s free card is the more consistent of the two. Unlimited recordings and transcriptions are on the pricing page and in the features FAQ. The extra limit I found is in the Free vs. Premium help article, edited 4 June 2026: advanced summaries for the first 5 calls each month, then only the General/Enhanced template, plus a 30-day preview of Premium features after sign-up.

Fireflies’ official pages still do not line up. The pricing page I loaded on 20 November 2026 showed Free at $0, “Free forever,” unlimited transcription with an asterisk, limited AI summaries, 400 minutes of storage per team, and 20 AI credits, with a 2-hour recording limit in the comparison table. The help article What’s included in the Fireflies Free plan says 3 meetings are included, auto-join allows unlimited transcriptions, storage is 400 minutes per seat, and the plan includes 20 AI credits per month. I am not choosing one of those readings as the product’s real cap. See Fireflies.ai pricing explained for the same conflict in more detail.

How they record meetings

Fireflies’ join-and-record FAQ says the bot appears as Fireflies.ai Notetaker, may need to be admitted from a lobby, and can record without you attending if the platform allows guest bots. It stays up to 2 to 4 hours depending on your plan. The same FAQ says the bot cannot be hidden, and that a paid add-on can rename it at $5 a month for a shared name across 5 users, or $5 a month per user for individual names. For recording without a bot, that FAQ names the Chrome extension (Google Meet only) and the mobile app for in-person recordings. A separate article, Bot vs. No Bot, says the Desktop App’s Take Notes mode captures system audio without joining, produces a transcript and notes, does not save audio or video, and labels speakers as Speaker 1 / Speaker 2 rather than by name.

Fathom’s features page FAQ says the Fathom Notetaker captures Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Bot-free capture is in beta in the Fathom desktop app on Mac, available on every plan including Free, requires a Fathom user to be in the meeting, and is listed for Zoom, Google Meet, and Slack Huddles. The new desktop app, that FAQ says, is on Mac (macOS 14.2 or later), with Windows coming. The iOS app captures in-person meetings. Premium, according to the Free vs. Premium help article, can remove in-meeting banners and rename the meeting bot.

I have not checked which product actually starts, stops, or is admitted in a live meeting. Both vendors also tell you to obtain any required consent before recording.

FAQ

Is Fireflies or Fathom free?

Both publish a $0 plan. As of 20 November 2026, Fathom’s Free plan is called free forever, with unlimited recordings and transcriptions. A Fathom help article says Free includes advanced summaries for the first 5 calls each month, and after that only the General/Enhanced template is available. Fireflies also calls Free free forever, but official pages disagree on the limits: the pricing table marks transcripts Limited, storage 400 minutes per team, and a 2-hour recording limit, while free-plan help says 3 meetings are included, auto-join allows unlimited transcriptions, and storage is 400 minutes per seat.

How much do Fireflies and Fathom cost?

As of 20 November 2026, Fireflies Pro was $10 per seat per month billed annually or $18 billed monthly; Business was $19 or $29 on the same pattern; Enterprise was $39 per seat per month with annual billing only. Fathom Premium was $16 per user per month billed annually or $20 billed monthly; Team was $15 or $19 per user per month with a 2-user minimum; Business was $25 or $34, also with a 2-user minimum; Enterprise was $35 per user per month with annual billing only. Check each current pricing page before you buy.

Can Fireflies and Fathom record without a bot?

Yes, with different limits each vendor publishes. Fireflies documents a calendar bot that appears as Fireflies.ai Notetaker and cannot be hidden, a Chrome extension for Google Meet that does not need a bot, and a Desktop App Take Notes mode that captures system audio without joining. Take Notes, Fireflies says, does not save a separate audio or video file and does not apply speaker names. Fathom’s features FAQ says bot-free capture is in beta in the Fathom desktop app on Mac, is available on every plan including Free, works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Slack Huddles, and that Windows support for the new desktop app is coming.

Which is cheaper, Fireflies or Fathom?

It depends on the plan and the billing period. As of 20 November 2026, Fireflies Pro at $10 per seat per month billed annually is lower than Fathom Premium at $16 billed annually, and Fireflies Pro billed monthly at $18 is lower than Fathom Premium billed monthly at $20. Fathom Team annual, at $15 per user per month, is closer to Fireflies Business annual at $19, and Fathom Team has a 2-user minimum. Fathom’s own free plan already includes unlimited recordings, while Fireflies’ free-plan limits are described differently on official pages. These figures are list prices, not a ranking of quality.

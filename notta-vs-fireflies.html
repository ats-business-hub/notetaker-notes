How they record meetings

Notta’s official homepage, checked 22 November 2026, describes bot-free recording: the Notta desktop app records Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex, and the same public copy mentions bot-free calls for Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, and Slack huddles, on Mac and Windows. I have not tested that desktop app, and I am not claiming it is the only capture method Notta offers.

Fireflies usually joins as Fireflies.ai Notetaker. Official help, already cited on this site, also documents a Google Meet Chrome extension that is audio-only and does not work for Zoom or Teams, and a desktop “Take Notes” mode that records system audio without a bot joining as a participant (that mode does not save separate audio or video files, according to official help).

Whichever tool you use, tell participants they are being recorded and follow the consent rules that apply to your calls.

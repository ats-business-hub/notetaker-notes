Plans and list prices, as of 20 November 2026

The pricing page states that all prices are in USD. It shows four self-serve plans: Pay As You Go, Core, Advanced, and Pro, plus a custom Enterprise option. Annual and monthly amounts both appear on the Core, Advanced, and Pro cards. The figures below are list prices from that page on 20 November 2026. Annual figures are the yearly price the page shows, not a converted monthly equivalent I calculated myself.

How hourly billing works, according to the FAQ

The pricing FAQ says “per hour” of transcription is the length of the audio or video file you upload, not the time it takes to transcribe or the time you spend on the Sonix website. It also says charges are prorated to the nearest second, and gives this example: a 52-minute file is billed for 52 minutes, not a full hour. AI analysis hours are measured the same way: the length of the recordings you run AI on, not processing time.

The same FAQ says translation runs on your transcription hours. On Core, Advanced, and Pro it draws from the same hours included in your plan; on Pay As You Go you purchase more hours. Alignment and burned-in subtitles are described the same way.

The free trial

The pricing page advertises a 30-minute free trial, with no credit card required, and says you can cancel anytime. The FAQ “How are my free minutes applied?” says every new account comes with 30 free minutes, and that if you purchase minutes while free minutes remain, the free minutes are applied to your first upload. The FAQ “Can I cancel my subscription plan anytime?” says you can stop a subscription from your account page, and that you can still upload and transcribe new files until the anniversary of the subscription. I have not opened a trial account for this article, so I am only restating those official sentences.

What official pages say is included

The pricing page says all plans include the in-browser editor, 54+ languages, and what it calls “award-winning accuracy.” I am not repeating the accuracy figure as a result I measured. The features page, also last updated October 2026, says Sonix delivers up to 99% transcription accuracy on clear recordings, transcribes a one-hour file in about 5 to 6 minutes, accepts 44+ audio and video formats, and exports to 18+ formats including DOCX, PDF, SRT, and VTT. It also lists speaker identification, an in-browser transcript editor, automated subtitles and captions, translation, shareable transcript links, and an AI meeting recorder as one intake path alongside file uploads and integrations. I have not tested those features, and I did not find a public page that explains in detail whether the meeting recorder joins as a visible bot.

The pricing comparison marks AI workspace usage as not available on Pay As You Go, and included on Core, Advanced, and Pro at the hour allowances in the table above. API access, Zapier, and webhooks are listed in the comparison. SOC 2 Type 2, SSL, encryption at rest, and two-factor authentication appear in the security rows; HIPAA, a signed BAA, SSO/SAML, and SCIM are described on the Enterprise block.

Languages, including Japanese

The pricing and features pages both say transcription and translation cover 54+ languages. The languages page names Japanese among those languages and says Japanese transcripts are returned in kanji, hiragana, and katakana. A dedicated Japanese transcription page repeats that you select Japanese as the spoken language and receive a transcript in standard Japanese script that you can edit in the browser and export. I have not tested Japanese accuracy, dialects, or mixed-language audio.

Discounts mentioned on the pricing FAQ

The pricing FAQ says Sonix offers special discounted pricing for students, teachers or professors, faculty members, and people who work at a non-profit. The public pricing page I read does not list those discounted amounts. If you need those prices, you have to check with Sonix. The FAQ also says teams that transcribe over 1,000 hours per year should contact the enterprise team. I am not listing coupon codes or unpublished special terms on this page.

Questions, answered from the official pages

How much does Sonix cost?

As of 20 November 2026, Pay As You Go was $10 per hour; Core was $25 a month or $275 a year for 5 hours a month; Advanced was $50 a month or $550 a year for 20 hours a month; Pro was $80 a month or $880 a year for 40 hours a month; and Enterprise was custom. Additional hours on subscription plans are $10 per hour. Extra seats on Core, Advanced, and Pro are $25 a month or $275 a year and do not add more hours. Check the live page before you buy.

Does Sonix have a free plan?

The public pricing page describes a free trial, not an ongoing free plan. It says every new account comes with 30 free minutes, with no credit card required, and that you can cancel anytime. The pricing FAQ says remaining free minutes are applied to your first upload.

How does Sonix bill by the hour?

The pricing FAQ says “per hour” refers to the length of the audio or video file you upload, not the time it takes to transcribe or the time you spend in the product. Charges are prorated to the nearest second. Translation on Core, Advanced, and Pro draws from the same included hours; on Pay As You Go you purchase more hours.

Does Sonix transcribe Japanese?

Yes, according to official pages. Sonix lists transcription and translation in 54+ languages. The languages page names Japanese and says Japanese transcripts are returned in kanji, hiragana, and katakana. I have not tested accuracy.

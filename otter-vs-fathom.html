Quick verdict by use case

There is no single winner. These are readings of the official pages, not test results.

  • A $0 plan with no monthly meeting cap. Fathom is the more generous free card on this point. The pricing page lists unlimited recordings and transcriptions on Free. Otter Basic is also $0, but the comparison table caps it at 300 transcription minutes per user each month, 30 minutes per conversation, and the 25 most recent conversations.
  • A clearly stated monthly minute budget. Otter is the clearer page. The caps above are written on the comparison table. Fathom’s free limit, as a help article states it, is on advanced summaries (the first 5 calls each month), not on the number of recordings.
  • Bot-free capture on Windows, or on Microsoft Teams without a bot. Otter’s desktop help lists macOS 13.0 or higher and Windows 10 or higher, and describes botless recording without Notetaker joining. Fathom’s features FAQ says bot-free capture is in beta on Mac, works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Slack Huddles, and that Windows support for the new desktop app is coming. Teams is named for the Fathom Notetaker bot, not for that bot-free list.
  • Japanese, as the help centers state it. Otter’s language article, updated 10 November 2026, lists Japanese as a transcription language. Fathom’s features FAQ names seven transcription languages that do not include Japanese, while a help article says 38 languages without listing them. I have not tested accuracy in any language.
  • A lower paid entry price for one person, billed annually. Otter Pro’s annual tab showed $8.33 per user per month, which is lower than Fathom Premium at $16 billed annually or Fathom Team at $15 with a 2-user minimum. Otter Pro billed monthly, at $16.99, is close to Fathom Premium’s annual figure and lower than Fathom Premium billed monthly at $20.

Free plans, in more detail

Otter’s comparison table is specific. Basic is 300 meeting and recording transcription minutes per user each month, with no rollover, 30 minutes per conversation, 3 lifetime pre-recorded file imports, and conversation history limited to the 25 most recent. The Basic plan card also lists Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet, live transcription, speaker identification, multi-language support, iOS and Android apps, and an Otter MCP server.

Fathom’s Free card lists unlimited recordings and transcriptions, a choice of bot-free (in beta) or bot capture, instant AI call summaries, and clips, playlists, and search across calls. The features FAQ, “Is Fathom free?”, says yes, and that the free plan includes unlimited meetings, transcription, and AI summaries, plus the Fathom Notetaker, bot-free capture in beta on Mac, multi-language support, and the iOS app. The help article Free vs. Premium for Individuals, edited 4 June 2026, adds that Free includes advanced summaries for the first 5 calls each month, after which only the General/Enhanced template is available, and that a free preview of Premium features ends 30 days after sign-up.

How they record meetings

Otter’s Notetaker overview, updated 21 August 2026, says Notetaker automatically joins Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams meetings to record and transcribe in real time. It joins as a participant, with the default display name “[Your Name]’s Notetaker (Otter.ai).” The same article says Notetaker cannot join anonymously or without appearing to other participants, and that meeting settings must allow guest participants. For botless recording, it points to the Otter Desktop app. The desktop app article, updated 6 November 2026, says the app lets you record and transcribe meetings from your computer without Notetaker joining as a participant, lists macOS 13.0 or higher and Windows 10 or higher, and names Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and Slack huddles among supported meeting types, plus other conferencing, dialer, and messaging apps.

Fathom’s features page FAQ says the Fathom Notetaker captures calls on Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Bot-free capture, it says, is in beta in the Fathom desktop app on Mac, is available on every plan including Free, requires a Fathom user to be in the meeting, and works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Slack Huddles. The same FAQ says the new desktop app, with bot-free capture, live summaries, and Scratchpad, is on Mac (macOS 14.2 or later), with Windows coming. The iOS app, it says, captures in-person meetings. Premium, according to the Free vs. Premium help article, can remove in-meeting banners and rename the meeting bot.

I have not checked which product actually starts, stops, or is admitted in a live meeting. Both vendors also tell you to obtain any required consent before recording.

Languages, as official pages state them

Otter’s language article, updated 10 November 2026, lists the transcription languages named in the table above, including Japanese. It says selecting a language tells Otter which language is spoken in the audio; it does not translate an English conversation into Japanese. It also says some languages are available in beta, without naming which, and that the Otter interface remains in English. The pricing comparison table is narrower: “AI transcription in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and Chinese.” Both pages include Japanese. I am repeating the two lists rather than picking one.

Fathom’s features FAQ names seven languages for transcripts and summaries, and translation into 29 more through Ask Fathom. The Free vs. Premium help article says unlimited transcriptions in 38 languages. Those two official pages do not agree, and the 38-language sentence does not come with a list, so I am not treating Japanese as confirmed or ruled out on Fathom from that article alone. Japanese is not in the seven-language list on the features page.

FAQ

Is Otter or Fathom free?

Both publish a $0 plan. As of 20 November 2026, Otter Basic is free, with 300 transcription minutes per user each month, 30 minutes per conversation, and the 25 most recent conversations. Fathom’s Free plan is called free forever, with unlimited recordings and transcriptions. A Fathom help article says Free includes advanced summaries for the first 5 calls each month, and after that only the General/Enhanced template is available.

How much do Otter and Fathom cost?

As of 20 November 2026, Otter Pro was $16.99 per user per month on the monthly tab, or $8.33 per user per month on the annual tab. The same page also showed starred Pro figures of $8.49 monthly and $4.17 annual, with a footnote that the offer is valid only with an India-issued credit card. Otter Business annual was $19.99 per user per month. Fathom Premium was $16 per user per month billed annually or $20 billed monthly; Team was $15 or $19 per user per month with a 2-user minimum; Business was $25 or $34, also with a 2-user minimum; Enterprise was $35 per user per month with annual billing only. Check each current pricing page before you buy.

Can Otter and Fathom record without a bot?

Yes, with different limits each vendor publishes. Otter’s Notetaker overview says Notetaker cannot join anonymously or unseen, and points to the Otter Desktop app for botless recording on macOS 13.0 or higher and Windows 10 or higher. Fathom’s features FAQ says bot-free capture is in beta in the Fathom desktop app on Mac, is available on every plan including Free, works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Slack Huddles, and that Windows support for the new desktop app is coming.

Does Otter or Fathom support Japanese?

Otter’s language help article, updated 10 November 2026, lists Japanese among supported transcription languages. Otter’s pricing comparison table names a shorter set that still includes Japanese. Fathom’s features FAQ lists seven transcription and summary languages that do not include Japanese, and a help article says 38 languages without listing them. Official Fathom pages do not agree on the language count. I have not tested accuracy in any language.

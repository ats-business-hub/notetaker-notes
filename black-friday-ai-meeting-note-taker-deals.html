Noota: 40% off the first year of a new annual Pro or Business plan

On 20 November 2026, Noota’s official pricing page advertised: “Black Friday 2026 · until Monday, November 30, 11:59 pm (Paris time)” and “40% off annual plans.” The same block said the offer is for Pro and Business, for the first year, on any new annual subscription, including new customers, returning customers, and monthly subscribers who switch to annual. It also said: “No code needed: the discount is applied at checkout,” and “No discount on monthly plans.”

The discounted first-year prices shown on that page were €11.40 per user per month billed annually for Pro (€136.80 for the first year instead of €228) and €23.40 for Business (€280.80 instead of €468). The same page listed regular prices in euros: Free €0 with no card needed; Pro €19 per user per month billed annually, or €29 billed monthly; Business €39 billed annually, or €49 billed monthly. Enterprise is custom, for 50 or more seats.

MeetGeek: 30% off new Pro and Business for 12 months

On 20 November 2026, MeetGeek’s official pricing page advertised: “BLACK FRIDAY 30% OFF Pro & Business — 12 Months,” with the code BLACKFRIDAY2026, and “Offer valid for monthly and annual new subscriptions.”

A script on that page sets the offer window from 16 November 2026, 00:00 PST to 30 November 2026, 23:59 PST. The same script stores sale prices of Pro $11.19 monthly and $6.99 yearly, and Business $18.90 monthly and $11.90 yearly, against list prices of Pro $15.99 monthly and $9.99 yearly, and Business $27 monthly and $17 yearly. Those sale figures appear only while the banner is visible. I have not confirmed the code at checkout.

Sembly: 20% off any plan for 12 months

On 20 November 2026, Sembly’s official Black Friday 2026 page advertised: “Black Friday deal · November 16–30,” and “20% off Sembly for 12 months. Use code BF2026 on any plan, billed monthly or annually, until November 30.” The FAQ on that page says the code works from 16 November to 30 November 2026, and that “The deal ends on November 30, 2026.” The visible text does not name a time of day or time zone. The same offer also appears on Sembly’s pricing page as “Black Friday: 20% off all plans for 12 months · ends Nov 30.”

The Black Friday page says BF2026 works on Basic, Pro, and MAX, and that on team plans the discount applies to the price of each seat. On monthly billing, the next 12 monthly charges are 20% lower. On annual billing, the first year is. After 12 months, the plan continues at its regular price. The page also says the code is not applied automatically: you click Add next to “Promotion code,” paste BF2026, and start a free trial. If you already use Sembly, the FAQ says to add the code in Settings > General > Plans.

Discounted prices shown on that page were: Basic $11.20 per month, or $8 per month billed annually; Pro $23.20 per user per month, or $16 billed annually; MAX $31.20 per user per month, or $24 billed annually. The regular prices shown next to those figures were Basic $14 or $10, Pro $29 or $20, and MAX $39 or $30. I have not confirmed the code at checkout. Prices and offers change. Check the official page before you buy.

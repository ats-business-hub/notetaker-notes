How much does tl;dv Pro cost?

Official structured data on the pricing page, checked 22 November 2026, lists Pro at €18 per seat per month billed annually (€216 per year), or €29 per seat per month billed monthly. Official blog pages publish the same figures in dollars. Recheck the live checkout for currency and any promotion.

How much does tl;dv Business cost?

The same structured data lists Business at €29 per seat per month billed annually (€348 per year), or €39 per seat per month billed monthly. Official blog pages publish the same figures in dollars.

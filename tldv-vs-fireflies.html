tl;dv vs Fireflies.ai for small teams (2026)

This article uses official vendor websites and pricing pages, checked on 21 November 2026 (Japan time). Fireflies list prices also follow official pages already extracted for this site on 19–20 November 2026; I re-fetched fireflies.ai/pricing on 21 November 2026, but the dollar amounts were not present in the static HTML, so please recheck the live page before you buy. I have not long-term tested either tool. This is not a hands-on review of accuracy or reliability. Prices, limits, and the currency you are billed in can change.

I compared the two on three questions that matter to a small team:

  1. What do the official free and paid plans actually list?
  2. How do they record Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams?
  3. What do official pages say about notes, languages, and integrations?

Quick pick

Fireflies may fit a team that already wants a calendar-connected notetaker with a documented bot, a no-bot desktop “Take Notes” mode, and a free plan that the vendor calls free forever — with storage and credit limits that official pages do not fully agree on. tl;dv may fit a team that wants a free plan with unlimited recordings and transcription listed on the public pricing page, and paid plans that officially advertise unlimited AI notes (Pro) or no usage-based AI fees (Business). I have not tested which product is more accurate.

How they record meetings

Fireflies usually joins as Fireflies.ai Notetaker. Official help, already cited on this site, also documents a Google Meet Chrome extension that is audio-only and does not work for Zoom or Teams, and a desktop “Take Notes” mode that records system audio without a bot joining as a participant (that mode does not save separate audio or video files, according to official help).

tl;dv’s official integrations hub says it can “Record and transcribe meetings from Google Meet, Zoom and MS Teams.” The Zoom page (fetched 21 November 2026) offers Mac and Windows (Alpha) downloads and says you can “Record any Zoom meeting in one click,” with recordings stored in a personal library. The pricing page also mentions custom bot appearance on Pro. I did not find a single official sentence, in this check, that clearly states whether a participant-bot always joins, never joins, or is optional. Read the current Zoom, Meet, and Teams setup guides before you promise a no-bot call to a client.

Whichever tool you use, tell participants they are being recorded and follow the consent rules that apply to your calls.

Languages, notes, and integrations

tl;dv’s public pricing page lists unlimited transcription in 100+ languages on the free plan, and “Translate Zoom meeting transcripts” into 40+ languages on the Zoom product page. I have not tested accuracy, and I have not confirmed whether Japanese is individually named on a language list. Check tl;dv’s current language documentation if Japanese is a requirement.

Fireflies lists Japanese (ja) among 100+ languages. Its pricing FAQ still calls English the primary language and other languages beta (as already documented on this site from official pages).

For workflow tools: tl;dv’s Zoom page names Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, Notion, and Google Docs, and says it connects with over 5,000 tools via Zapier. Fireflies officially lists 240+ apps, including Notion (help says saving transcripts to Notion needs Pro or higher), Slack, HubSpot, and Salesforce. I have not verified how reliable these syncs are in daily use.

Who each tool may fit

Fireflies may fit a team that wants a widely documented bot, a written no-bot fallback, and a low annual Pro list price ($10 per seat per month on the 19–20 November 2026 check). Read the conflicting free-plan limits before you rely on “unlimited” transcription. A longer, sourced write-up is in Fireflies.ai review and Fireflies pricing. If the other choice is Otter, see Otter.ai vs Fireflies.ai.

tl;dv may fit a team that wants the vendor’s stated free-plan combo of unlimited recordings and transcription, with AI-note limits that only kick in on the free tier (10 meetings / 10 Ask AI queries). The official annual list prices in euros (€18 / €29 per seat per month) are not directly comparable to Fireflies’ dollar prices without checking the live checkout currency and exchange. This site does not yet have a full tl;dv review.
